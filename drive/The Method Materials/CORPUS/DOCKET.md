## 1. Conventions in force

- **CONVENTIONS worth keeping, two new.** A first-person probe must carry **`mine`** and `myself`
  (chat 126's lacked *mine*; L9700 *the trajectory was mine* is the site). **A one-generator surd
  count is not a count**: name the candidate generator (n ≤ 7 gives 17, n ≤ 8 gives 19). Strip the
  section number from BOTH sides of a PP heading comparison. Print a short cited section rather than
  probing it. A roster that wraps is read on the whitespace-normalised join. An unmarked-sub-heading
  test requires a blank line above, and its hits are then READ: a bold one-sentence paragraph and an
  italic epigraph are not headings. A literal phrase is not a test (chat 127's fault 6: *Seaton's
  ratio* scored 1 as a phrase; *Seaton* word-bounded scores 1 main, 33 reg, 28 mc, 14 pc — which is
  the finding). A substring test on a header word can drop a DATA row (fault 4: *term*). A token
  count is not an object count (fault 5: one *both PASS* over two objects). A count word counts DATA
  rows. Never split a regex on a comma the printed form contains. `\b(I|my|we|our)\b` matches the
  Roman numeral in *He I*. A pointer-site regex must be `§N(?!\d)(?!\.\d)`; a lettered pointer needs
  `§([A-Z]\.\d+(?:\.\d+)*)`; `heading_line` is numeric-only; the volume heads appendices `## Appendix
  X — …` twice.

## 2. Standing method

**Standing method, unchanged and all of it earned:** exact-token heading resolver, never prefix,
resolved to the **body** occurrence; never span a section by heading rank; never bound a Part-ending
section by `section_span`; grep lowercase `register NNN` by hand; check every printed pair count
against C(N, 2) and name the denominator; resolve every pointer to the claim and not the heading,
under both resolvers, and locate where the claim does live; test on the raw line, case-insensitively,
word-bounded, in the word's other forms, left-bounded for a stem — raw for a symbol, never
transliterated — and on the whitespace-normalised join. Give every negative claim its own witness and
state what a sweep covered. Where a section is short, print it rather than probe it. Check the
arithmetic of every ratio and percentage; never round with `round()` — `Decimal.quantize`, convention
named. State a budget rather than a negative when a computation is too large. **Sweep the convention,
not just the base** — a surd count needs its generator, a median needs to be called a median
(chat 127's 16z-06), a sequence-agreement count its comparison convention, a combination count C(6,2)
= 15. A formula numerator is not a value; a citation is not a declaration; a heading is not a
statement; a bound is not a measurement; a token probe is not a reading; a colon-terminated lead-in
is not a list item; a count word counts DATA rows; a Register entry restating a count is not
independent corroboration of it; an identity entailed by another is not an independent test; a
literal string is not a test. **Where the text prints a sample, measure the population; where a
population has a stated ceiling, read the DATA that fixes the ceiling before deriving it** (16z-11).
**Grep the volume and the Register for a later or exact statement before recording any figure as
unreproducible — and read the Register's WARNING lines: they are where a figure is withdrawn.**
**When an instrument disagrees with a hand reading already taken from the file, or with a totals
line the source states about itself, the instrument is wrong until proved otherwise. When a
reconstruction disagrees with the record, the finding is about the reconstruction.**
