**The 14D-as-observation-boundary.** Per the original-works project's own answer (grounded in its register): there is **no built Λ₁₄**; the fourteenth axis was *bounded but not built* (entry 333); the tower runs Λ₈→Λ₁₃ everywhere. ## 4. The reframing of the time material: observation selects the frame

**Measured + your ruling:** the apparent §6.2-vs-§11 tension (arrow exists / arrow does not survive) is not a contradiction. Both are true; they are the same object under two **observational frames** — destinations-begin-empty vs occupied-destinations-represented. Observation selects the frame. The arrow is neither present nor absent until the frame is fixed. "The arrow is a property of the assumption" = it is observation-relative.

**This is the connective tissue the current 1.6 prose leaves implicit**, and making it explicit is the heart of the reader-facing work. It is the same spine as the seed and as human observation (the ToE document, context only): each is definable only from outside, each bottoms out in a binary before subject and object.

**Do not treat this as a fresh absorption of a paper.** Treat it as three distinct, correctly-sized tasks:

**(A) Reader-facing prose treatment of the already-absorbed time material**, with the observation frame made explicit. This is the same prose pass we are already doing, applied to §6.2 and Chapter 12's §12.11.0.x when we reach them in reading order — plus the deliberate addition of the "observation selects the frame" reading that reconciles the two arrow-statements. No new construction; prose + one honest framing.

**(B) Two small absorptions into subject matter** — the Ravi/tangent-length presentation-triangle and the q-as-articulation-vertex reading. These go through the Register and the build like any subject-matter addition, attached to the three-body material and the q-coordinate introduction respectively. Small, self-contained, do when convenient.

**(C) Author the 14D-observation-boundary synthesis with you**, then revise the Preface conjecture **once** to state it — seed = horizon = observation, Löwdin/Schrödinger lineage, named-not-built. This is the only part that is genuinely new authoring, and it is the capstone, not a prerequisite.

**Sequencing:** (A) happens naturally as the prose pass reaches those chapters. (B) can be slotted whenever. (C) is last, because the Preface revision should state the boundary with the whole picture settled — and because it depends on your authoring input, not on transcription.
