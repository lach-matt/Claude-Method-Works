# Front matter — a note on reading the numbers

**Placement:** front matter, after the Preface material and before Chapter 1 (or folded into an existing reading-note). It applies from the first cap-dependent number in the book (Chapter 4's counts), which is why it belongs at the front rather than in a later chapter. Moved here from old §8.0; the self-audit that surrounded it there is cut per the standing rule.

---

### On the caps

Λ has no natural end — the shell numbers *n* and *e* run without limit — so every *count* in this book is taken at fixed, finite caps on the coordinates. Unless a section names other settings, every cap-dependent figure is computed at

      (n_max, e_max, ℓ_max, k_max, f_max) = (3, 3, 1, 3, 1).

Where a figure is recomputed under variation, the settings are printed beside it. A cap-dependent figure that names no caps is not thereby unqualified — it is at these caps. What would be a fault is a figure computed at *other* caps that does not say so, and one of the book's standing audits checks for exactly that.

The counts move with the caps; the *shape* does not — a claim made exact in Part I, where the pattern that generates the cells is shown to hold across a large spread in cell count. Claims about shape are checked at several settings, so that nothing rests on where the counting happened to stop.
