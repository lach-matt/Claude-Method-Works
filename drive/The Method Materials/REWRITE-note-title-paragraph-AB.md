## VERSION A — as currently approved (no anecdote)

**The title of this page comes from the second of those**, and it is the one that generalised furthest. I had computed that a particular identity, w = V·e (it multiplies two derived quantities, V = 4ν/3 and e, to give a third), detects no error in the data. ## VERSION B — with the 14D origin on the page
