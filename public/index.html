    Reading this index

    The top level is the drawn periodic layout: … cells held, … admitted by ℛ, and the … the operator admits but the table does not hold drawn as ghosts, each labelled with its subshell — 1d, 1p and 2d forbidden by ℓ ≤ n−1 (the lighter tint), 3d and helium's vacated slot deferred (the fuller tint). Tap one for its definition, and the plate offers the priced alternative: helium drawn at group 2, where E = 20. Switch to the Janet layout to see the same elements on (n+ℓ, ℓ), where E = …. (These figures are filled from data/index.js, not typed here.)

    Tap an element and it opens as its slab of the lattice in three dimensions, the way the source figure draws Λ_spectra: ionisation stage up, ℓ into the page, one node per cell, side by side along the third axis where a site holds two multiplicities; a node carries the same mark as in the nested view — a filled sphere for a measured cell, a ringed disc for an exact one, a small grey dot for a computed one; the glowing Λ₈ ionisation ladder climbs the front edge, one rung per step of the record's ladder, nothing invented. Drag to rotate, wheel or pinch to zoom, Shift-drag or a two-finger drag to pan, tap a node for its cell, a numeral for its ion. The third layout, Lattice, is the whole solid: every element a slab, Z across, the measured wedge at low Z and low ℓ. Nest shows the same element as the nested circles instead: ions in spectroscopic order (I is neutral) joined by the ladder, each ion opening into its eight ℓ channels, each channel into its cells. Every cell is a row of COORDINATES-2.13.

    Every value in the detail plate carries a status badge. READ is a value read from a source table or paper; PINNED a definition stated at the precision a program needs; DERIVED arithmetic on those, nothing added; RECOVERED measured out of the index's own computed column; RECONSTRUCTED the sources state the object but not the form a program needs. A status is never flattened.

    Navigation

    • Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, tap a node to fly to it; the detail plate follows the selection.
    • With the canvas focused (click or tab to it): Esc up one level · H whole index · + − zoom · arrow keys pan. / focuses search from anywhere outside a text field. Table rows in the detail plate open with Enter.
    • Search accepts a symbol, Z, name, or a path: Fe II, Fe II d, Fe II d 3.
    • The address bar carries the path, so any node is a link.

    Query the index

    The console answers deterministically from the loaded data: help, go Fe II d 3, axes, closure, sources, caveats, count, measured Fe, residuals Fe, B Pd, ladder Fe. When the page runs as a claude.ai artifact a second answerer, Claude, is offered beside it and is always labelled; it is instructed to answer only from the selected record and to name the status of every number it quotes.

    Particles and references

    Particles, where this build carries it, opens what the sources state of the binders and particles beyond the electron — the muon and its window [119, 918] mₑ with the paper's energy balance as an instrument, the pion, tau, kaon, antiproton and Σ⁻ it excludes, antimatter and the exotic atoms by CPT and reduced mass, antiprotonic helium's worked cell, the photon's single restored cell, the 27 constants of Λ_phys, and the terms counted absent from the sources — every figure read from the passage that states it, with its status, and none of them a lattice figure, because the lattice carries configuration and not scale. The eighth solver mode runs the muon energy balance over sticking, density and the production cost, and refuses below the kinematic floor. References lists every outward identifier the sources cite — NIST ASD with its DOI, and the arXiv and DOI identifiers, each quoted only from a paper released to the site — and links them by construction; a compilation named without an identifier is cited as a string. A measured cell's plate links its source the same way, and no target is invented.

    What computes and what does not

    The explorer computes nothing; the solver suite does, and every solver carries a selftest against the instrument's own numbers. Every value the explorer shows is tools/populate.py's, serialised by tools/webindex.py. A finding is recorded, never repaired: where the spectra index disagrees with the observed configurations, both values are shown.
