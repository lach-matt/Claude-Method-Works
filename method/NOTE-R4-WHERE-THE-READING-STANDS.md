## What this changes about the work

**Phase 1 is not a re-read. It is the settlement of a read that has already happened.** Re-reading a
chapter that carries a recorded read produces what the record already holds — this was demonstrated
today on Chapter 2, where an independent reading found the SCOPE/CENSUS collision that chat 69 had
already recorded, and where a figure I first called unreproduced (§3.7's *362 comparisons*) is
resolved in the record as 4·81 + 2·19.

So the order of work is:

1. **Read the recorded read before re-reading the chapter.** The READ member and the chapter's
   working-register entries first, then the chapter, and only for what they leave open.
2. **Settle the 83 that have an instrument**, since they cost a run each.
3. **Build the 37 that need one**, from the corpus's own seated functions.
4. **Put the 22 rulings to M** one at a time, as he asked.
5. **Read the four chapters and the appendices that have no read** — 29, 30, 31, 33, and Appendices
   A to G.

**The full hold governs throughout.** A finding is recorded, never repaired; the prose pass is M's
and comes after; pointers and section numbers are last.
