# DRAFT-R4-SERIES-RELABEL — prepared, built twice, seated nowhere

**NOTHING IN THIS FILE IS IN THE STORE.** It is the W text and the substitution spec for the
Spectra Compendium's full-configuration relabel, which M ruled on 8 September. The build was made
twice and discarded twice: the first time because the gate caught §B.3 orphaned, the second time
because the second build surfaced that the relabel reaches **four Register entries, which are never
edited**, and a passage of the main volume that *argues from* the old notation. **The ruling is back
with M** and this is kept so the work is not lost.

`DRAFT-R4-SERIES-RELABEL.json` is the spec: 544 table rows, 6 flagged-channel lines, 1 legend. It is
stamped against BUILD293's md5 and applies cleanly to it; it does **not** carry the main volume's
four lines, the two successor instruments, or the Register entry that would record the change.

The W text below is written as though seated, because that is what it would be. It is not.

---

### W-299 — the series column in full configuration, and the parents this work has not verified

- **M RULED: seat all 596 in full configuration, and mark the rows owed a level source.** The form is the one the volume already prints where it is complete — `3s2.3p4.(3P).nd 4D J=7/2`. **544 rows are relabelled; 52 already stood in that form.**
- **THE COLUMN WAS IN SIX NOTATIONS, NOT THE FOUR I REPORTED.** A parser written to read all 596 found two more only when it failed on them: **`2s2(1S)np 2P° J=1/2`**, a core prefix carrying a parenthesised term without dots, and **`2p·ns ³P° J=0`**, which separates the configuration with a **middle dot**. Counted before the parse: 402 bare, 160 core-prefix, 20 short-parent, 14 full. **A count taken by pattern was short by two whole notations, and only reading every row found them.**
- **THE VOLUME'S OWN USAGE GOVERNS WHERE IT HAS ONE.** The compendium writes `3s2.3p4.(3P)` for Ar III and `5p5.(2P*<3/2>)` for Ba IV — it carries the s² subshell for one and not the other. Rather than impose a convention over the volume, **each species that already prints a full-form row supplies its own parent string**, and 66 rows take theirs that way.
- **WHERE THE PARENT CAME FROM, ROW BY ROW.** **449 from the isoelectronic sequence** — the ground states the volume itself already writes, `2s2.2p6.(1S)` for a Ne-like parent, `3s2.3p.(2P*)` for an Al-like one. **66 from the volume's own full-form siblings.** **5 from the measurements store**, whose `limit_label` names the parent outright — `Ga_II_(4s2_1S<0>)`. **76 from the isoelectronic sequence with nothing to check them against**, and those carry the mark.
- **THIRTY-EIGHT ROWS HAVE NO PARENT AT ALL, AND THAT IS NOT AN OMISSION.** A one-electron species — He II, Li III, Be IV, B V — converges on a **bare nucleus**, which has no configuration and no term. Their labels stay as they are. **Seventy-one rows carry no J and keep none**: where the source does not resolve J, inventing one would be a worse fault than the gap.
- **FOUR SPECIES HAVE GENUINELY SEVERAL PARENTS, AND A SEPARATION MEASURED IN THIS VOLUME FIXES EACH.** **Si I** at **287.240 cm⁻¹** — the Al-like `3s2.3p 2P*` fine structure, `<1/2>` lower. **Ne II** at **25,840.700** — the parent is O-like `2s2.2p4`, whose only terms are ³P, ¹D and ¹S; ³P's own splitting is under 1,000 cm⁻¹ and ¹S lies far above, so the second limit is **¹D by exclusion**. **Ne I** at **780.340** — the F-like `2s2.2p5 2P*`, inverted, `<3/2>` lower. **Ba III** at **17,550.000** — the I-like `5p5 2P*`, and the volume's own ten labelled rows confirm `<3/2>` at the lower limit.
- **AND THREE SPECIES THAT LOOKED LIKE THEY HAD TWO PARENTS HAVE ONE.** **Ca II's limits differ by 0.010 cm⁻¹, Li I's by 0.036, Zn I's by 0.020** — and their parents are `3p6 1S<0>`, `1s2 1S<0>` and `…4s 2S<1/2>`, **every one a single level.** A single level cannot produce two limits. **These are one limit printed at two roundings**, and it is recorded here and not repaired: correcting a printed figure is a repair and needs its own ruling.
- **THE MARK IS THE VOLUME'S FOURTH, DECLARED WHERE THE OTHER THREE ARE.** L297 read *"Three marks state a limitation"* and now reads **four**: a **double dagger on the parent term** marks a parent this work has not checked against a published level table. **77 rows carry it**, across Bi I/II/III, Ca I, Cd I/II, Ga II, Ge III, Hg II, Ne I, Ti III, Zn I/II — the 4s/3d crossover, where the electron count does not fix whether `4s 2S` or `3d 2D` lies lower, and the heavy sequences beyond argon.
- **A FIRST BUILD OF THIS PASS WAS SEATED AND DISCARDED, AND THE GATE IS WHAT CAUGHT IT.** Relabelling the table alone left **§B.3 Flagged channels** quoting the old notation — *"Al I  3s²nd ²D  0.630"* and four like it — so the section no longer named any row of the table it flags. `r2-ch20a3` reported it exactly: *"flagged-by-threshold rows NOT among the printed five: 4"* where the banked figure is 0, and its own line *"Every flag in this collection has an identified cause; none is unexplained"* had become false. **The build was discarded and remade from BUILD293** with the five flagged rows and their header carried, the column widened to fit. **A relabel is not a table operation: every place that quotes a label moves with it.**
- **THE SOURCE WAS SOUGHT AND IS NOT REACHABLE FROM HERE.** `physics.nist.gov`, `en.wikipedia.org` and `arxiv.org` are all blocked by this container's egress proxy; a literature search through the paper connector returns analyses of individual spectra and no compilation of ion ground states. **The mark says exactly that, and no more.**
