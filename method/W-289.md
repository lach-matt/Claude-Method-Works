### W-289 — the map rebuilt to the function, and a finding of mine corrected

- **M RULED ON 8 SEPTEMBER: *"the handle map is old and stale. it is yours to build to the function you need."*** That changes what has to be built. **The handles are not an addressing scheme to be recovered — they are a dead index** — and the function the bibliography performs is stated in its own opening line: *"Every object of this compendium names a work. What follows is those works, ordered by year, with the objects each carries."* **So the column is the INVERSE of the attributions the objects already carry**, and every object carries them in a form a reader reads. `method/proofs/bibindex.py` (29/29) is seated with this build and derives it.
- **THE DERIVATION, AND WHAT IT REACHES.** For each of the 299 objects, every (work, year) its own entry names on its **grade line** — *"Computed — M §20 / the statistical language; Deming & Stephan 1940; Csiszar 1975."* — and in its **prior-art quote**. Names normalise to an unordered set of surnames, so *Deville et al. 1999* and *Deville 1999* are one work and *Murray & von Neumann* keeps its particle. Against the 162 seated rows: **127 DERIVED, 12 YEAR-DIFFERS, 7 NAME-FORM, 16 NOT-ATTRIBUTED.**
- **THE HANDLES ARE STALE AND THE CROSS-CHECK SHOWS IT RATHER THAN ASSUMING IT.** `handlemap.py` resolves 112 handles on evidence independent of any ordering; **145 of those land on a row this instrument derives, and 134 agree.** On **every one of the eleven that do not, the derived answer is the object the work is named for**: `Borchers 1992` derives to *"Borchers 1992 / Wiesbrock 1993"* where the handle column points at the unfinished modular object; `Racah 1943` to *"Racah 1943 seniority"*; `Karp 1972`, whose paper is the one that made set cover famous, to *"The seed is a set cover"* where the handle says *"The unit template, corrected"*. **M's ruling is confirmed by measurement, not taken on authority.**
- **AND A FINDING OF MINE IS CORRECTED. W-281 SAID THE FALSE UNIVERSAL IS FALSE BY 34. IT IS FALSE BY 12.** *"Every object of this compendium names a work"* — **34 is the count of objects carrying no HANDLE in a column M has now told us is stale, and that measurement stands exactly as taken.** What was wrong was the label I put on it. **Counted from the attributions themselves, twelve objects name no work**, and the instrument names all twelve — *The statistics operator*, *The alphabet law*, *The tower's two ends joined*, the unfinished modular object, *The isoelectronic ladder*, *The exchange factor*, five of the Löwdin family and *The triangle form on K₃* — so the claim can be judged rather than taken. **W-281 stands unedited; this entry supersedes it**, which is the rule that governs a Register entry and governs this one.
- **AN ATTRIBUTION IS NOT A MENTION, AND THE TWO ARE NOT MERGED.** Widening the scan to an object's body prose reaches **129 rows against 127**. The two extra are kept out: **the grade line and the prior-art quote are where an object says what it RESTS ON, and its body is discussion.** A work discussed is not a work the object names, and merging them would turn a mention into a source silently.
