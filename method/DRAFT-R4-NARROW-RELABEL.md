**NOTHING IN THIS FILE IS IN THE STORE.** No byte of any volume has moved, no member has been

M ruled the narrow option: relabel only the rows where the parent is genuinely ambiguous.

## The count is right and my species list was wrong

I put the option to M as *"the 66 rows in Ar II and Ba III"*. **That was 44 + 22 — every row of two
species — and it is not the ambiguous set.** Read against the volume's own rule, that *an open-shell
core gives one series per parent, all interleaved, converging on different limits*, ambiguity needs
**two limits in one species and a row that names no parent**:

| species | rows | naming a parent | bare | distinct limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ba III | 22 | 10 | **12** | 289,100.000 · 306,650.000 |
| Ne II | 37 | 0 | **37** | 330,388.600 · 356,229.300 |
| Si I | 33 | 19 | **14** | 65,747.760 · 66,035.000 |
| Ne I | 7 | 4 | **3** | 173,929.750 · 174,710.090 · 390,977.350 |

12 + 37 + 14 + 3 = **66**. The same number, a different sixty-six — **coincidence, not agreement**,
and it is recorded as coincidence.

**Ar II is not in it.** All 44 of its rows carry one limit, 222,848.300, so no Ar II row is ambiguous
about which parent it has. Ar II's fault is a different one and the Register already holds it:
**register 815's withdrawal** states that `(³P)ns ⁴P J=5/2` and `3s2.3p4.(3P).ns 4P J=5/2` are *the
same series from two capture files*. Measured, four such pairs stand — L326/L369, L327/L368,
L328/L367, L331/L366 — **identical in n-range, levels, interior, n\* range, δ to four decimals, fits
and limit**, differing only in notation, in bracket (`1/1` against `untested`) and in σ(δ), which the
later row prints 2.26–2.45× smaller in all four. A full relabel would have made those four pairs
**byte-identical rows**. That is the fault the narrow option avoids.

Three further species show two limits with bare rows — **Ca II, Li I, Zn I** — and they are not
parent cases at all: 95,751.870/95,751.880, 43,487.114/43,487.150, 75,769.310/75,769.330 are **one
limit printed to two roundings**, the item already owed M separately.

## What is NOT settled, and is M's

`2p5(2P°3/2)nd`, `3s2.3p4.(3P).nd` and `(³P)ns`. Ba III and Ne I each already use one. **Si I and
Ne II use none**: Si I's nineteen rows carry a jj *pair* label `(3/2,5/2)*`, which is not a core
prefix, and no Ne II row names a parent at all. So 51 of the 66 need a style chosen for them.
