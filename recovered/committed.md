# Committed before computation — B.2.13

Object: Λ_cav, rectangular cavity mode index.
Coordinates (m, n, l, s), caps m≤M, n≤N, l≤L, 1 ≤ s ≤ ν(m,n,l) − 1,
where ν = [m>0] + [n>0] + [l>0].

C1. |Λ_cav| = 2MNL + MN + ML + NL, and this equals the direct TE+TM count.
C2. Λ_cav is closed under coordinatewise JOIN but NOT under MEET.
    Reason committed in advance: ν is monotone but not a meet-morphism
    (sum of mins ≤ min of sums), so cap(x∧y) can fall below min(cap x, cap y).
C3. Therefore the polarization axis is NOT an admissible coordinate,
    and the object is a join-semilattice, not a lattice.
C4. The (m,n,l) product-of-chains alone IS a distributive lattice, E(X)=0,
    order dimension 3, rank-modular.
C5. F(−1) ≠ 2 for Λ_cav. (Λ's F(−1)=2 has a cause in §6.8.1 specific to Λ.)
C6. Lattice bracket (all covers) is strictly narrower than channel bracket
    (one axis) on a majority of interior cells; mean ratio committed as
    0.3 – 0.6.
C7. Domain exit ω_c = (π²c³Q/V)^(1/3), to be confirmed by a second route.
