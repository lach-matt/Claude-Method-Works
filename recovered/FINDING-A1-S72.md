## 4 · WHAT THIS SETTLES
**(i) THE VOCABULARY IS LÖWDIN'S, AND NEITHER SUPPLIED SENTENCE CARRIED IT.** "Simple energy
rule" (Scerri) and "axiomatic quantum theory" / "the simple (n+l,n) rule" (Schwarz) appear in
neither sentence handed to this project earlier. That is the THIRD and FOURTH independent
indication that both earlier sentences were RECONSTRUCTIONS. They must not be quoted as
Löwdin's words anywhere in the deliverables.

**(ii) NO NON-RELATIVISTIC CLAUSE AND NO EXACTNESS CLAUSE APPEAR.** The demand as quoted is
that the rule be DERIVED within axiomatic quantum theory. Neither source, at the page,
restricts the field to the non-relativistic one, and neither demands the rule hold without
exception. This CONFIRMS LCP61 from a fifth and sixth route. It does NOT overturn M's s70
ruling on criterion 5: what the Challenge asks and the standard M holds this project to
remain separate objects, and the stricter standard is M's to keep.

**(iii) THE TIE-BREAK IS INSIDE THE DEMAND, NOT OPTIONAL.** Löwdin names the object
"(n+l, n)" -- the ordering AND the secondary n rule together, as ONE rule, written as a
displayed equation. Deliverable 1 covers both and must continue to.

**(iv) THE SCOPE WORD IS "NEUTRAL ATOMS."** Consistent with the walk, which is neutral
ground configurations Z=2..108 and nothing else.
