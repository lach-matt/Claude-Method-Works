# PREDICTION — INSTRUMENT A (THE WIDTH RULE, BY BASE RATE)

Filed 2026-08-20T15:38:25Z, session 57, BEFORE the enumeration A-1 is run and
before any claim is classified. R 1449.

M's ruling: the proposed rule "a width is never established from one end" is an
empirical claim about this chain's own error history and must be measured, not
asserted. The trap named in the ruling is selection bias: F44.2, F55.2, F56.3 are
a FOUND-FAULT sample, known because they were checked.

---

## PREDICTION A-1a — THE ENUMERATION IS SMALL

Count/width/lag claims in the sealed chain and Deliverables 1-4: **12 to 20**,
most probable **15**. The chain is mostly per-row ORDERING claims, which assert a
winner and not a width; only the collapse and transit work asserts step counts.

## PREDICTION A-2a — THE PARTITION IS LOPSIDED TOWARD ONE-ENDED

    TWO-ENDED                2 to 4     (the s56 transit widths, both ends run)
    ONE-ENDED-CHECKED        3          (F44.2, F55.2, F56.3 and nothing else)
    ONE-ENDED-UNCHECKED      6 to 12    most probable 9

Rationale: a two-ended check costs a second solve at a Z nobody was interested in.
The chain's own economy predicts that such solves were run only when a fault forced
them. **If ONE-ENDED-UNCHECKED comes back at 0, this prediction is badly wrong and
the chain is more careful than its fault register suggests.**

## PREDICTION A-3a — THE MEASUREMENT WILL BE UNDERPOWERED

The TWO-ENDED failure rate is predicted to be **0 of 2-4**, i.e. an empty or
near-empty denominator. The ONE-ENDED-CHECKED rate is already 2 of 3.

**2/3 against 0/3 is not a base rate. It is two small numbers.**
Fisher-exact on 2/3 vs 0/3 does not reach any conventional threshold.

## PREDICTION A-4a — THE DECISION

**The rule will NOT be adopted as a law of this chain**, because A-3 cannot
supply the comparison A-4 demands. It is predicted to be adopted instead in the
weaker and honest form:

> A one-ended width claim is UNCLOSED, not WRONG. It carries the label
> ONE-ENDED and may not be cited as a closed result until the missing end is run.

This converts the question from an error-rate claim (unmeasurable at n=3) into a
labelling requirement (verifiable by inspection). **Residue is not closure** applies
to the chain's own claims exactly as it applies to its physics.

## PREDICTION A-4b — THE BY-PRODUCT OUTRANKS THE RULING

The ONE-ENDED-UNCHECKED list is predicted to contain **at least one claim that a
single solve would overturn**, and that claim is predicted to lie in the collapse
or promotion work rather than in the ordering walk, because the ordering walk is
scored row by row against a reference and the collapse work is not.

---

## FALSIFIERS

* A-1a fails if the enumeration exceeds 25 or falls below 8.
* A-2a fails if ONE-ENDED-UNCHECKED is 0, or if TWO-ENDED exceeds ONE-ENDED.
* A-4a fails if the two-ended sample is large enough to support a rate comparison
  at all — in which case the rule must be decided on the measurement as filed and
  this prediction's "underpowered" reasoning is void.
