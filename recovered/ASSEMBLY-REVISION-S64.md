## R4 · WHAT IS NOT PROPOSED, AND WHY

**NO MECHANISM CLAIM IS ADDED.** The s64 rows show the f channel near-hydrogenic at
Z=89 (5f binds at 1.57x the hydrogenic value where every g channel returns exactly
1.00x) and fifteen times hydrogenic at Z=91. That is consistent with f-orbital
collapse and it is the natural reading. **It is not proposed for the assembly.** The
project has measured energies, not radial extents; an energy ratio is not an ⟨r⟩, and
this chain has a five-session record of the failure mode where a comparison is read
for more than it holds fixed. The assembly says the tie-break is false and that the
field is right. It does not say why, and should not until a fixed-reference control
separates nuclear charge from occupancy.
