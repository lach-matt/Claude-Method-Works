# F73.2 — RESOLUTION MISMATCH IN THE 2x2 DIAGONAL. Session 73. RAISED BEFORE ANY CONCLUSION.
The 2x2 built in ci2b.py carries:
    OFF-DIAGONAL  V  -- a DETERMINANT-level Slater-Condon element, one specific pair.
    DIAGONAL  E_A,E_B -- AVERAGE-OF-CONFIGURATION HF total energies of the two configurations.
These are not the same object. The determinantal term correction (hfterm's E_open - E_avg) is
tens of mHa at these rows and is NOT included in the diagonal.
**CONSEQUENCE, STATED SO IT CANNOT BE FORGOTTEN:** the root-shift table computed at s73
(shift vs gap, and the apparent strong-mixing flag at Mo 42 where the AOC gap is 4.33 mHa and
V is 17.75 mHa) is INDICATIVE ONLY and IS NOT SCORED. **No claim is made that any row flips.
No claim is made about the configuration column.** The Mo flag may be an artefact of the
missing term correction alone.
**WHAT IS UNAFFECTED:** CI-2, CI-6 and CI-7 as scored. V itself is a clean determinant-level
quantity and does not depend on the diagonal. The Cu/Ag/Au exact zeros are symmetry results
and depend on no energy at all.
**WHAT IT REQUIRES:** the term-resolved 2x2 -- diagonal and off-diagonal at the SAME
resolution, on one common orbital set -- is a NEW OBJECT. It is NOT covered by any clause of
PREDICTION-CI2x2. Under R 1449 it needs a FRESH prediction, written and hashed before it runs.
