## The five findings (measured, front matter)
1. The clock was an assumption, not a law. Occupancy result holds for non-accretive destinations, not just empty ones; §7.1 silently assumes empty. At §7.4 caps, 31.6% of composable steps run backwards in the index that distinguishes placed-vs-present g. (§11)
2. Time here is a selection, not a structure. One clock per coordinate, no others (sums/max/min fail to close). Each carries forward arrow, backward co-arrow, timeless core. 16 combos distinct, closed under intersection never union; all four imposed → one component per object. (§36–38)
3. The object is a certificate — closes the antecedent. An arrow is a ranking function, the maximal sub-index a supporting invariant. A certificate has nothing to predict; "is it time?" is the wrong question to put to a proof technique. (§40)
4. Treeness is the load-bearing hypothesis, never named. Decay theorem, Markov property, balance identity, cut law all rest on: constraint graph is a tree. All four fail at Λ₉′ (the one tower stage that closes a cycle). (§41.1)
5. The framework measures distance-from-membership where neighbours settle membership. 4 instances: 2-decomposability, interval-dismantling, starshaped class, ranking functions. (§41.2)

## The eight further results (measured)
2. Λ₉'s composition law carries a global time function: occupancy non-increasing along composition; closed timelike curves exist exactly within level sets; every step of one is conservative. One-line proof, no caps, shown able to fail. QUALIFIED: precedent exists (weight decreasing along digraph edges is standard); antecedent narrower — g≤q≤k presumes destination begins empty; 31.6% of composable steps raise occupancy in the occupied-destination index. (§6.1, §13)
3. Three-body configuration space indexed and closed — E=0, treewidth 1, four caps; elevates to n bodies as a chain of Cesàro bounds; exact subset a meet-semilattice at every n. (Part IV)
4. That object is Brylawski's lattice of integer partitions (1973), found by one search after four turns. Claim WITHDRAWN; what survives = the closure defect of the dominance order (which the search did not return). n-body work retained as the route by which later work was reached. [NOTE: overlaps the absorbed three-body paper / Ch 36]
5. Past/present/future form a path not a triangle; future conditionally independent of past given present — 33 distinct pasts → one future set at every transfer value; future never a value (0 of 97 pairs determine one); it is a saturated interval. (Part V)
6. Nothing can be removed. Every Λ₈ cell implied back by ≥16 pairs of the others. Min set that can leave: X∖[a,b] is a sublattice iff a join-prime and b meet-prime — 0 disagreements in 116,138 intervals. (§28)
7. g ≤ min(q, 4f+2) is the unique two-parent bound in the whole tower, all 7 stages; a cut law holds across 89 component tests, 1 violation — at Λ₉′. (§29–30)
8. The reversed index is a lawful closed index, not Λ₉. Agree on 389 cells (all g=q=k); intersection is a groupoid (closed, factorising, identities + two-sided inverses). Union NOT closed — E=2,857 — so a structure containing both directions of time is not available at any price. (§33–35) [THIS is the "two indices / union does not exist" — the time-and-space framing]
