# Book criteria — retained

**The Lach Index Lattice — a hydrogenic cylinder over ν**

---

## A. Specified

1. **Table of contents**
2. **Fully illustrated, with a narrative matching the content** — figures carry
   argument, not decoration; every figure earns its place by showing something
   the prose cannot.
3. **References and sources**
4. **An index**
5. **The complete computation collection**

---

## B. Proposed additions

### B1. A withdrawal register
Every retracted claim, its correction, and the section it affected. Currently
74 in the paper, plus 19 from the current session. **This is the book's
strongest evidence, not its apology** — a paper claiming 652 verified cells
with no record of what failed is unfalsifiable.

### B2. A provenance table
Every number traced to its source: compilation, table, page. Six compilations
so far (Sansonetti 2008 ×2, Kramida & Martin 1997, Kaufman & Martin 1991,
Sugar & Corliss 1985, NIST ASD). No number in the book without a route back.

### B3. A stated Ω — the open items, named
Principle 13 forbids a claim of coherence while Ω ≠ ∅. The book must therefore
either close Ω or **state it**. Current Ω:

- four unchecked documents (Edlén 1964, Ritz PZ 1908, Paschen & Götze 1922,
  Dunz 1911)
- 29 of 33 candidate spectra uncollected
- antiprotonic helium untested
- C₆ measurement density unknown

### B4. A domain statement per result
Where each result holds **and where it declines**: r ≥ 5, ν_V, the dilution
bound, the ν ≈ 7–11 deduction boundary, the p = 1 pole.

### B5. The cap-dependence notice
All counts are cap-dependent (§2.4). The Birkhoff generator count changes with
the caps; the shape does not. Stated once, prominently, not buried.

### B6. Novelty limits
The ~75% figure for V = 4ν/3 and the four documents that would settle it.
**A book that names its own unchecked prior art is stronger than one that
claims priority and gets corrected.**

### B7. Reproducibility
Every computation runnable. Data files, scripts, and the exact figures they
produce.

### B8. The principles, with their formalisations
Ten have exact form; seven are procedural and do not. **Three of the ten were
corrected by the act of formalising them** — that record goes in, because it
demonstrates the method on the method.

### B9. Two demonstrations of self-defence, verbatim
- **The Z error**: wrong core charge for Al II and K II. The bracket held
  56/56 and could not notice, because Z cancels from containment. δ and V
  both flagged it — exactly 𝒟 = 2.
- **The check catching the check**: relation (b) written down wrongly, with
  the two disagreeing numbers printed on the same line. **𝒟 ≥ 1 does not
  require the auditor to be right.**

### B10. The index must satisfy its own law
An index of a book about indices should be self-referencing: 𝓡(index) = index.
Concretely — every term in the index should be recoverable from the book's own
structure, and the index should contain the entry for itself.

## C. Structural criteria

### C1. Nothing appears twice
The audit found the interval-size product formula already proved in the paper
while I was preparing to present it as new. Every result must be checked
against the existing text before it is written.

### C2. Every assertion is proved, measured, or marked
Principle 13. An unmarked assertion is a defect.

### C3. Negative results are Part I, not an appendix
The theorems about what an index cannot do are the strongest contribution.
They should not be positioned as limitations of the method.

### C4. The collection is closed, not open
The census bounds the candidate set at 33 spectra. **"Open by construction"
was wrong.** The book states the bound, the count collected, and the count
remaining.
