## WHY THE ORIGINAL TEXT WAS WRONG, AND THE SHAPE OF THE ERROR
Lieb-Simon 1977 constrains nothing but orthonormality. Φ constrains the orbital form
itself. The two minimisation problems are not the same problem and are known not to
coincide in general (Ruskai & Stillinger, J. Math. Phys. 25 (1984) 2099; discussion
in Griesemer & Hantsch, ARMA 203 (2012) 883). Lieb and Simon do extend to certain
restricted cases in J. Chem. Phys. 61 (1974) 735, and that extension is recorded in
the literature as NOT covering the restrictions at issue here.
**THE ERROR SHAPE IS R-A's: a theorem cited by NAME and by ITS SUBJECT AREA, never
checked against the object in hand. R-A says an audit by name is not an audit. This
amendment extends that reading from levers to CITATIONS: A CITATION BY NAME IS NOT A
CITATION. THE HYPOTHESES MUST BE MATCHED TO THE OBJECT, TERM BY TERM.**
