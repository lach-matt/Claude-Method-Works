## PARKED — three outside the ASD

**1. Antiprotonic helium p̄He⁺ transition table.**
The only antimatter system with a real quantum defect and a real series
(*n* ≈ 32–40, transitions to parts in 10⁹). Antihydrogen cannot be tested —
δ = 0 exactly, so the bracket has nothing to grip, and it is 1.8 × 10¹² times
wider than ALPHA's existing comparison.

**2. C₆ coefficients versus *n* for any alkali.**
Decides whether the bracket beats direct measurement at *p* = 11. The cost law
is verified there (*V* = 4*n*/10); what is unknown is how densely C₆ has been
measured in *n*.

**3. Edlén, *Handbuch der Physik* XXVII (1964), section on series formulas.**
The last serious hiding place for *V* = 4ν/3. Chapter is in English despite the
German series title. Novelty currently assessed at ~75%; Ritz 1908 (ApJ) read
in full and contains no bound; Martin 1980 checked and gives a flat ±0.03 cm⁻¹.

*Also outstanding on that question:* Ritz *Physikalische Zeitschrift* 9, 521
(1908) — reprinted in *Œuvres* (1911) pp. ~142–150, platform e-rara.ch;
Paschen & Götze, *Seriengesetze der Linienspektren* (Springer, 1922);
Dunz, *Seriengesetze der Linienspektra* (Leipzig, 1911).
