## 3 · THE MISSING WORDS WERE **"FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES"** — AND FOUR THINGS FOLLOW
**(i) THE DEMAND IS "FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES", INSIDE "AXIOMATIC QUANTUM THEORY."** That is
the whole of it. Recorded in Löwdin's own words at a stated page, by an author writing
about these very remarks.

**(ii) NO NON-RELATIVISTIC CLAUSE. NO EXACTNESS CLAUSE. NO SCHRÖDINGER CLAUSE.** The
sentence does not name the Schrödinger equation, does not restrict the field, and does not
demand the rule hold without exception. LCP61 is now confirmed from the quoted sentence
itself, not by triangulation around it. **M's s70 ruling on criterion 5 is untouched: the
project's standard is stricter than the Challenge's and remains M's to keep.**

**(iii) LÖWDIN'S OWN NAME FOR THE OBJECT IS "THE SIMPLE ENERGY RULE."** Schwarz renders the
same sentence with "(n+l,n) rule" substituted in. **That substitution is Schwarz's, not
Löwdin's.** Scerri's version carries editorial brackets exactly where a substitution is
made -- "[the rule]", "[(n+l,n)]" -- and there is no bracket at "the simple energy rule".
Registered as a caution: **two secondary sources rendering one sentence differently is
precisely how the two earlier reconstructions arose.** Quote it as "the simple energy rule".

**(iv) THE TIE-BREAK IS IN THE OBJECT.** Löwdin's own text calls the thing Bohr arrived at
"the modified rule", bracketed by Scerri as (n+l, n) -- ordering and secondary n together.

## 4 · A FIFTH SOURCE, AND A SCOPE FACT THAT BEARS ON THE WALK
Schwarz et al., Front. Chem. 8:813 (2020), quote Löwdin 1969 directly on a DIFFERENT

under ionization as an open question, not as a defect.** The walk is neutral ground
configurations Z=2..108. That is the Challenge's own scope, from the Challenge's own text.
