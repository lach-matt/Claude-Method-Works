## §0 · WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS

This is the statement of the solution in theorem form: hypotheses, construction,
conclusions, and a ledger separating what is **derived**, what is **bounded with the
bound stated**, and what is **not modelled**. It is not the demonstration. Every
claim below carries a receipt into the sealed runtime, and the receipts are named.

It answers P.-O. Löwdin's 1969 challenge to derive the aufbau ordering from the
Schrödinger equation without empirical input. **Löwdin's own paper has never been

## §1 · THE STATEMENT

> **THEOREM (THE ORDERING CLAUSE).**
> Let the differentiating electron at nuclear charge Z be the channel entered by
> the aufbau construction Φ defined in §3, built from the Hamiltonian H of §2 with
> no fitted or empirical input. Order the channels (n,ℓ) by the value of Z at which
> each is first entered.
> **Then n + ℓ is non-decreasing along the entire sequence, without exception, for
> all 107 scored steps Z = 2..108.**

> **THEOREM (THE TIE-BREAK CLAUSE, AND ITS FAILURE).**
> Among channels sharing a value of n + ℓ, Madelung's secondary rule asserts that
> the channel of lower n is entered first.
> **Under Φ this holds for n + ℓ ≤ 6 and is FALSE at n + ℓ = 7 and 8. At both
> places where it fails, Φ agrees with the measured periodic table and the rule
> does not.**

**These two clauses do not have the same standing, and reporting the result as
"the Madelung rule is derived" without this sentence attached is a misstatement.**
The ordering clause is a law of the field. The tie-break clause is an empirical
regularity of the light blocks.

## §2 · THE HYPOTHESES

**H1 · THE ORIGIN.** The exact non-relativistic many-electron Hamiltonian,

    H = Σᵢ(−½∇ᵢ²) − Σᵢ Z/rᵢ + Σᵢ<ⱼ 1/rᵢⱼ ,   HΨ = EΨ ,   Ψ antisymmetric.

**Nothing stands above this.** Every term in the radial equations of §3 descends
from a term here: the kinetic operator to −½d²/dr² + ℓ(ℓ+1)/2r², the nuclear
attraction to −Z/r, and — this is the whole of the matter — the electron repulsion
to the direct Y⁰ and the exchange X.

**H2 · BORN–OPPENHEIMER, POINT NUCLEUS, INFINITE NUCLEAR MASS.**
*APPROXIMATION.* Finite nuclear size measured by first-order perturbation theory on
point-nucleus orbitals: |dD| = 0.27480 mHa at Z=89, 11.61091 mHa at Z=120; **the
entrant is unchanged at all three rows tested.** Nuclear mass: exact scaling
argument only — a uniform μ/m on every channel scales all margins identically and
leaves the argmin invariant. **The 0.05 mHa working floor covers numerics and does
NOT cover the nucleus; that was falsified at 232× the floor and the falsification
stands in the record.**

**H3 · THE SINGLE-DETERMINANT (HARTREE–FOCK) ANSATZ.**
Ψ → one Slater determinant of spin-orbitals ψᵢ = (P_nℓ(r)/r)·Y_ℓm·χ_s.
***APPROXIMATION. THIS IS THE LARGEST SINGLE ASSUMPTION IN THE CHAIN AND IT IS
NAMED AS SUCH.*** It is a variational restriction, not a derived identity. §6
states exactly what it costs and §5 states what is known about where it bites.

**H4 · THE AVERAGE-OF-CONFIGURATION RESTRICTION.**
The determinant is averaged over m_ℓ and m_s within each shell, leaving shell
occupations q_nℓ. *APPROXIMATION, BOUNDED:* single-configuration displacement
affects **11 of 107 rows, none of them a first-entry row; the ordering is
untouched.**

**H5 · THE SCALAR-RELATIVISTIC EXTENSION.** *REQUIRED, NOT OPTIONAL.* At Z=90 the
non-relativistic field selects 5f and the scalar-relativistic field selects 6d,
**a reversal of 253 mHa**, in agreement with the measured actinide. The extension
has an exact c → ∞ limit and c is **measured, not fitted**.

## §3 · THE CONSTRUCTION

**Φ, the aufbau operator.** At each Z, given the chain's own prior configuration
cfg(Z−1):

1. Enumerate the candidate channels admissible at Z.
2. For each candidate, solve the radial Hartree–Fock equations self-consistently
   in the scalar-relativistic field generated by cfg(Z−1) and nuclear charge Z:

       [−½ d²/dr² + ℓ(ℓ+1)/2r² + V_a]P_a − X_a = ε_a P_a
       V_a = −Z/r + Σ_b (q_b − δ_ab)·Y⁰_bb/r
       X_a = Σ_{b≠a} (q_b/2)·Σ_k c_k(ℓ_a,ℓ_b)·Y^k_ab/r·P_b

   with c_k = (ℓ_a k ℓ_b; 000)², w = (2ℓ+1)/(4ℓ+1).
   ***DERIVED.*** These are the Euler–Lagrange equations of the determinantal
   expectation value; the c_k are Wigner 3j symbols **computed from factorials, not
   looked up in a table**.
3. **Enter the channel that binds the differentiating electron most strongly:**
   the argmin of the total-energy difference over admissible channels.

**Φ CONSULTS NO MEASURED CONFIGURATION.** The observed table enters the program
only *after* the winner is fixed, solely to grade it. **This is measured, not
asserted:** with the observed table made unreachable, the chain still solves and
still chooses — **16 of 16 agreements, including across the 4s/3d crossover**, with
the runtime log byte-identical before and after. *(Limit: 16 of 107 rows; the
f-block and tie-break rows are untested by that blind walk.)*

**THE SEED IS NOT A CHOICE.** cfg(1) = 1s was labelled CHOSEN and is **DERIVED**:
solved at Z=1, E(1s) = −0.5000080791, E(2s) = −0.1250033510, E(2p) = −0.1250013413.
1s is the argmin by 375 mHa — the exactly soluble case, and the walk gets it right.

**C5 · THE PHYSICAL ORIGIN OF THE EFFECT.** At Z=1, E(2s) and E(2p) agree to 2

## §6 · WHAT THE STATEMENT DOES NOT CLAIM

**IT DOES NOT CLAIM THAT THE FIELD SOLVED *IS* THE SCHRÖDINGER EQUATION.** H3 is a
genuine approximation, not an identity, and no amount of further work inside
Hartree–Fock removes it. What is claimed is a chain in which **every departure from
H1 is named, bounded where a bound exists, and shown not to touch an ordering the
construction gets right.**

**IT DOES NOT CLAIM THAT CORRELATION CANNOT CHANGE AN ORDERING.** That sentence is
contradicted in print: in correlated relativistic calculations of superheavy
elements, multiconfiguration ground-state configurations differ from uncorrelated
ones in roughly half of cases, and improving the correlation treatment changed the
ground-state level in 4 of 51 cases. **Correlation demonstrably moves ground-state
configurations.** The claim made here is narrower and survives that: at the rows
this derivation decides, the first-order coupling vanishes by selection rule.

**IT DOES NOT CLAIM THE TIE-BREAK CLAUSE.** It derives that clause to be false
where nature says it is false.

**THE STANDING LAW UNDER WHICH CORRELATION IS EXCLUDED:** a mechanism earns a place
in the derivation only if it changes an n+ℓ ordering the construction gets wrong.
**Correlation has not earned one.** That is the argument's spine, and it is an
argument about what is *needed*, not a proof that nothing else could matter.

## §8 · THE OPEN REFINEMENT

**The claim that would remove H3 rather than defend around it:**

> **The argmin of §3 is invariant under relaxation of the single-determinant ansatz,
> to all orders.**

The first stone is laid: the vanishing of V at the deciding rows follows from
occupancy and parity, which are properties of matrix elements of the **exact**
Hamiltonian H, not of Hartree–Fock. **The difficulty is named rather than deferred:
the argument does not extend naively — parity forbids the odd orders, but two
excitations restore parity, so second order is not killed by the same rule.** Any
all-orders claim must be made on the **argmin margin**, not on the individual
matrix element. It may fail. **It does not gate the solution above.**

**A TRAP, RECORDED:** the correlation branch present in the code is a
Gell-Mann–Brueckner functional whose coefficients are derived nowhere in this
chain. Enabling it would import underived constants and **break criterion 4**.
Deriving the relaxation and enabling that branch are opposite moves.
