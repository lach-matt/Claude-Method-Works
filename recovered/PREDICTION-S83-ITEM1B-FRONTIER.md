## THE OBJECT

At each Z the walk needs one thing only: among ADMISSIBLE channels, the nu-minimiser
is the sigma-minimiser.  It does not need the whole ladder ordered.  So define, at
each Z, the FRONTIER of width w: the w channels of least nu.  Ask:

    **W := the smallest w such that the g-condition holds on the frontier of width w
    at ALL 107 rows, and fails at width w+1 somewhere.**

W is a cross-channel property of F_core with a BOUND attached — the width of the
window on which the field must be, and measurably is, Madelung-ordered.  This is
route (b)'s shape (a property carrying a bound) reached through route (c)'s field.

## CLAUSES

Q1 · W EXISTS AND IS SMALL.  **W is between 2 and 5 inclusive.**  Filed because the
     entrant contest is decided between rank 1 and rank 2 in the sealed walk, and a

     statement reduces to "rank 1 beats rank 2", which is the row's own criterion

Q3 · THE FRONTIER CONDITION STILL FAILS SOMEWHERE AT EVERY WIDTH >= 2.  Specifically
     **Z=57, 89 and 90 fail the frontier g-condition even at w=2**, because those are

     If they do NOT fail at w=2, the instrument is not measuring the walk's criterion

Q4 · THE COUNT AT w=2.  The number of rows failing at w=2 is **between 2 and 8.**

     fault in one of them and must be reported as such rather than explained.

Q5 · MONOTONE.  The failure count is non-decreasing in w.  A decrease is
     arithmetically impossible and is filed as the machinery check.
