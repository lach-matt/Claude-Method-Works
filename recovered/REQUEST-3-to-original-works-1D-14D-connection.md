## The thesis to test

M maintains that 1D and 14D are the two ends of ONE transition, provable from the books, and that the proof is likely **binary-only** — expressible when each coordinate is read as at-an-extreme (1) or not (0), and not otherwise. The reasoning offered as a lead (test it, don't assume it):

So if 1D and 14D are joined by the collapse, the join should be visible precisely in the bit-reading and invisible in the full-resolution reading.
