## THE LAW THAT BINDS THE WHOLE DESIGN
**NO FINITE INSTRUMENT CAN SAMPLE AN INFINITE SET. The instrument can only ever be a
FALSIFIER, never a prover.** If it finds a critical point that flips an entrant, the
selection rule is load-bearing and Deliverable 1 inherits a declared dependency. If it
finds none, NOTHING IS PROVED -- only that the falsifier was survived. **This must be
written into the instrument's own output so no future session can quote it as a proof.**

## RUNG B — NODE-EXCITED SAMPLING. **BUILDABLE, AND IT IS THE DIRECT ANALOGUE OF LIONS.**
The radial Fock operator H_l of the restricted theory has infinitely many eigenvalues.
Occupying a channel with the k-th radial eigenfunction instead of the lowest, and
re-converging, yields a different critical point at the SAME occupancy. **That countable
family IS the restricted-theory shadow of Lions' sequence**, and it is exactly the axis
Rung A does not touch.
  * Build: inside the SCF loop, select eigenindex k rather than the minimal node count;
    everything else unchanged. Small, local change to the shooting selection.
  * **LEVER TEST FIRST, R-A BINDS: poison k and the converged energy MUST move. If k does
    not move the output, the instrument is dead on arrival -- this is F54.2's exact shape
    and the one failure mode this project has already paid for twice.**
  * Scored quantity: D(Z) recomputed over the enlarged family, against m(Z).

## RUNG C — THE BOUND OVER ALL CRITICAL POINTS. **NOT AN INSTRUMENT. ANALYSIS.**
No sampling closes this. What is needed is a bound on the DIFFERENTIAL spread over the
whole critical set -- and the natural route is a spectral gap on the radial Fock
operator. **Hantsch's Remark (b) records that the gap result (Bach-Lieb-Loss-Solovej,
PRL 72 (1994) 2981) is NOT known in the restricted theory and that its method does not
transfer.** So Rung C is simultaneously our open item and the literature's.
**THIS IS ROUTE B's LANGUAGE, NOT ROUTE A's, AND IT IS WHERE THE PROOF MUST LIVE.**
