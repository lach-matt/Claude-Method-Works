## H · PRIOR ART ON DERIVING n+l ITSELF (the Challenge's own literature)
H6  Demkov, Ostrovsky (1971/72) JETP 33:1083; 35:66. Maxwell-fish-eye-type potential whose ZERO-ENERGY coupling-constant spectrum reproduces
    the n+l degeneracy; "replacing quantization of level energies with quantization of coupling constants at zero energy" (Thyssen-Ceulemans
    2017 p.381, via arXiv:2002.12128). SECONDARY. NOT a derivation from the many-electron Hamiltonian: a model potential with the rule built
    in. Does not touch our objects; recorded so T4 can state why it is not an answer to Lowdin.
H7  Kitagawara, Barut (1983) J. Phys. B 16:3305; (1984) 17:4251. FETCHED (abstract, IOP): the DO problem "describes correctly the Madelung
    quantum number n+l and its degeneracy"; invariance group Delta_N x SU(2); they themselves note the link to "the actual dynamics of
    atomic electrons" requires a further transformation. Group-theoretic classification, not dynamics. SECONDARY as to body.
H8  Kibler (2005) arXiv:quant-ph/0503039; Thyssen-Ceulemans (2017) "Shattered Symmetry"; Kholodenko-Kauffman (2020) arXiv:2002.12128 (modified
    Bertrand theorem); Varlamov (2025/26) arXiv:2501.18272, 2607.08105 (SO(4,4), double SO(4,2) tower). FETCHED (abstracts/html). All are the
    "elementary-particle approach" (Rumer-Fet-Barut): the table as a representation space, not as ground states of H_{N,Z}. None supplies
    an energy margin; none can address the differentiating electron (criterion 2). Recorded as prior art that ANSWERS A DIFFERENT QUESTION.
H9  arXiv:2606.17359 (2026) "Canonical regularization of the stationary Coulomb problem and an Aufbau-like spectral ordering." TITLE/ABSTRACT
    ONLY (alphaXiv listing): hydrogen-level regularisation producing an Aufbau-like ordering -- one-electron, no many-body field. Same class
    as H6. Not fetched further.
H10 Scerri (2003) "Lowdin's remarks on the Aufbau principle" (archive A1). Already on the archive. The primary-source gap (Lowdin 1969) stands.
