# RULING — THE EVIDENTIARY BOUNDARY IS Z = 108 (s49, M)

Carried verbatim, and binding on every session after this one.

## THE RULING, IN M'S WORDS

> "The Löwdin solution will be derived solely from the 108. 109 to 120 will be predicted and
> stated as such, based on the Löwdin solution, which will give future researchers values to
> test once our tech allows us to witness and measure the continued spectra."

## WHAT IT FIXES

**1 · THE DERIVATION'S BASE IS Z = 1..108 AND NOTHING ABOVE IT.** `ground.py` carries OBSERVED
configurations to Z=108 and PREDICTED labels above. The walk's evidentiary rows are therefore
exactly **107 steps, Z = 2..108**. That is the whole of the solution's support.

**2 · 109..120 ARE OUTPUTS, NEVER INPUTS.** They are walked, they are labelled **PREDICTED**,
and they are stated as predictions. They are never scored, never counted in `ok_score` or
`cfg_score`, and never cited as evidence for any clause. No claim about n+ℓ may rest on a row
above 108.

**3 · WHY THIS MATTERS TO THE PROOF, NOT JUST TO THE BOOKKEEPING.** Configurations above 108 in
every published table are themselves derived — frequently from the Madelung rule or from
calculations that assume it. Walking them and then scoring against them would be reading our own
premise back as confirmation. The boundary at 108 is what keeps the derivation non-circular, and
it is the same discipline as RECALLED-NOT-ENTERED applied to the periodic table's own edge.

**4 · THE PREDICTED ROWS ARE THE DELIVERABLE'S FORWARD FACE.** They are what the solution is
FOR: values a future spectroscopist can test when the superheavy spectra become measurable. A
prediction that could not be wrong would be worthless; these can be, element by element, and
each is to be published with its channel depths and margins so the test is specific.

## CONSEQUENCES ADOPTED IMMEDIATELY

- PG-8's terminal integers, **ok (97,107)** and **cfg (68,107)**, are the FINAL scores of the
  derivation. No later row changes them.
- The chain rows above 108 carry `prov = PREDICTED` and are reported in a separate table from
  the derivation's 107.
- Any future gate whose expectation counts chain rows must count to 108 for the derivation and
  report the predicted rows separately. Mixing the two in one denominator is a fault.
- The T4 writing task, when it opens, states the law from the 107 and lists 109..120 as
  predictions with margins attached.
