# F81.1 — THE RESTART LADDER IS NOT A DESCENT. AT cfg88+7p IT RISES.
# F80.2's NUMBER SURVIVES. F80.2's WORDS DO NOT.
# RAISED AGAINST A PRIOR SESSION'S INTERPRETATION, ON THIS SESSION'S MEASUREMENT.

## WHAT F80.2 SAID
*"The sealed Z=89 6d value sits 0.034493 mHa ABOVE ITS OWN BASIN FLOOR"*, by a
*"monotone geometric restart ladder"*, because *"the scheme halts at a point that is
stationary enough in eigenvalues and still DESCENDING IN ENERGY."*
The mechanism given — run2 stops on eigenvalue drift while Step 0(ii) is stated on the
energy — is correct and is not disputed here.

**THE 7p LADDER RISES BY 0.022195 mHa AND SETTLES THERE.** It is monotone for three
passes and then flat to 1e−12 Ha, exactly as 6d is — same shape, opposite sign.
Z=90 gives two more: 6d² **−0.025471 mHa** and 5f **−0.060204 mHa**. Four measurements,
three down and one up, spanning a factor of 2.7 in magnitude.

## THE CORRECTION
**THE RESTART LADDER MEASURES THE DISTANCE FROM THE STOPPING POINT TO THE ITERATION'S
FIXED POINT. IT DOES NOT MEASURE DESCENT TOWARD A MINIMUM, AND ITS DIRECTION IS NOT A
PROPERTY OF THE SCHEME.** run2 is a damped fixed-point iteration on the eigenvalues, not
a line search on E. Its fixed point is where the field stops moving; the energy at that
point may lie above or below the energy where the drift test happened to trip.
**SO "BASIN FLOOR" IS THE WRONG NAME FOR THE LIMIT OF THIS LADDER**, and the sealed 6d
value cannot be said to "sit above its own basin floor" on this evidence. What is
established is weaker and still useful: **the sealed value differs from the iteration's
fixed point by ~0.02–0.06 mHa, and that difference is a RESOLUTION.**

## WHAT SURVIVES UNCHANGED
1. **THE NUMBER. 0.034493 mHa at Z=89 6d stands**, and so does its use as F80.3's floor.
   §1 of this session's item-2 result re-labels s80's rows at it.
2. **THE MECHANISM.** Stopping on eigenvalue drift while defining the field on the energy
   leaves a discrepancy. That is right; only its sign was over-specified.
3. **THE CONSEQUENCE FOR PRECISION.** Any instrument comparing two energies finer than
   ~0.035 mHa must restart to stationarity first. **This session strengthens that: it must
   restart BOTH SIDES (J2), and doing so shrinks the scatter 17-fold.**
4. **s80's "it touches no ordering, no entrant, no margin" is unaffected.** The largest
   figure measured is 0.060 mHa against m(89) = 32.332 and m(90) = 54.05 mHa.

## WHAT IT CHANGES GOING FORWARD
**A LADDER DROP MAY NOT BE ASSUMED NEGATIVE, AND A PER-CONFIGURATION MEASUREMENT IS
REQUIRED, NOT A SCHEME-WIDE CONSTANT.** J1 already measures it every run; this fault is
why that was the right design and not over-engineering. The prediction filed for item 2
asked exactly this question (W4) and would have accepted a single scheme-wide floor had
the numbers allowed it. They do not.
