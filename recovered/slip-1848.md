BODY:
*The Board chat flagged that one artefact referred to a rule which "would have swallowed R 1848",
and observed that only the register at source could settle whether that entry exists. It does not.
The literal example `![caption](file.png)` belongs to entry R 642, at line 2430 of REGISTER-DATA.md;
it renders at line 1848 of the generated REGISTER.md. A grep line number was written down as an
entry id. The citation has since entered the register twice, at R 1710 and R 1728. Max id is 1735
and 1848 matches no heading under the declared instrument.* **THE CLAIM IS CORRECT AND THE
ATTRIBUTION IS WRONG: the Register's one apparent figure tag really is a literal example rather
than a figure, so nothing measured by that finding changes. What changes is what may be cited.
A NUMBER READ OFF AN INSTRUMENT IS A COORDINATE OF THE INSTRUMENT, NOT OF THE OBJECT — the
object-versus-observer fault of R 1578, arriving as a line offset. Disclosed, NOT repaired: R 1710
and R 1728 stand as written, on the R 1711 precedent that repairing an entry means editing the
record.* **And the converse was audited rather than assumed: two lines of REGISTER-DATA.md begin
with a number and a full stop and are not headings — the year 1972 inside entry R 1616, and the
quantity 3348.7 keV inside entry R 1544. The declared instrument excludes both correctly, which
is R 1617's false-heading hazard tested rather than trusted.*
