# RULING-S82 — R82.2. ISSUED BY M AT s82, ON THE F82.2 PROPOSAL. **BINDING.**
# R82.1 (corpus scanners) and R82.2 (physical instruments) are the same law, twice.

## R82.2 · THE DOUBT-SITED CONTROL.
## **A CONTROL MUST BE SITED WHERE THE INSTRUMENT IS DOUBTED, NOT WHERE IT IS TRUSTED.**

**THE BASIS, MEASURED.** The target-node search recovers 6d **exactly, to nine digits**, at
the fallback iteration and at the converged field alike. It cannot see any state above the
valence eigenvalue — f = log(nrm) crosses zero once and rises thereafter. **The control
passed every time for three sessions because the control WAS 6d**, and 6d sits in the one
region where the instrument works. A control sited in the trusted region measures that the
instrument does what its author expected. It cannot measure where the expectation stops.

## WHAT THE RULING REQUIRES
Any instrument reporting on a **domain** — a range of energies, radii, Z, occupancies,
fields or configurations — must, before any question row is scored:

  1. **DECLARE ITS DOMAIN**, split into a TRUSTED region and a DOUBTED region, in the
     source, in the instrument's own vocabulary. A domain left undeclared is a domain
     assumed, and this project has registered nine faults of that family.
  2. **SITE AT LEAST ONE CONTROL IN THE DOUBTED REGION.** A control whose expected answer
     is known independently of the instrument, positioned where the instrument's ability
     to answer is in question.
  3. **STATE THE DOUBT-CONTROL'S EXPECTED ANSWER BEFORE RUNNING IT**, filed and hashed
     with the prediction (R 1449). An expectation formed after the output is not a control.
  4. **HALT IF NO DOUBT-SITED CONTROL CAN BE CONSTRUCTED.** rc=5. **The impossibility is
     itself the finding and is recorded as one** — it is the statement that the
     instrument's reach cannot be established, which is exactly what F82.2 turned out to
     be and what three sessions of passing controls concealed.
  5. **A DOUBT-SITED CONTROL MAY FAIL WITHOUT KILLING THE RESULT**, provided the failure
     is reported and the result's claims are restricted to the trusted region. **What is
     forbidden is not failing there — it is not looking.**

## WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
It does not replace the can-fail law or R82.1. Synthetic can-fails still run and still
gate; trusted-region controls still run and still gate. **R82.2 adds the region those two
cannot cover, and F82.2 is the measurement of what lives there.**

## SCOPE
Every domain-reporting instrument from s83 onward. **The harness is `pack82/doubt82.py`,
built at s82 and NOT YET EXERCISED against a question row. Its first use is ordered at
s83**, and the first instrument it binds is whatever answers R81.6 — if M rules that
question worth a session.

Read the units · read which one it picked · read whether the instrument can see what it
claims · read whether a direction was measured or assumed · read what the patch actually
replaced · read whether the test that proves the instrument can see was drawn from the
same picture that made it blind · **and read WHERE the control was sited.**
