## NEW PHYSICS RESULTS

### The bracket is limit-free
*T* = *I* − *E* for constant *I*, so containment in *T* ⟺ containment in *E*.
**ν, δ and *V* need the ionisation limit; containment does not.** The method
applies wherever levels are known even when the threshold is not.

### The failure condition — and its inversion
> **The bracket fails ⟺ |Δ*T*| > 2*Z*²*R*/ν³**

A perturber shifts a level; it reorders one only if the shift exceeds half
the local spacing. **Hg II is the strongest mixing in the work** — 5d¹⁰7p at
60% leading percentage, 44% admixture — with perturbers 2–3 × 10³ cm⁻¹ from
a half-spacing of 24,634 cm⁻¹. It cannot fail.

**Read backwards, every passing cell is a measurement:**
> **bracket held ⟹ |Δ*T*| < 2*Z*²*R*/ν³ at that cell**

1,061 cells yield perturbation bounds; **tightest 1.398 cm⁻¹** (Al I 3s²54f).
Deductive, where a quantum-defect fit's equivalent is inferential.
