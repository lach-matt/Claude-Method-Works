**As the ANSWER KEY this is legitimate and always was** -- criterion 4 prohibits empirical
data as a FOUNDATION of the proof, not as the target the walk is scored against. Deliverable
1 is unaffected: the chain does not consume it.
**AS AN INPUT TO CONSTRUCTION IT IS AN EXPOSURE.** At s73 the observed table selected which
configuration is A at ten rows. That is empirical data inside the object, not outside it.
**THE RESULTS SURVIVE, BUT THE CODE PATH DOES NOT.** The pair (d10 s1, d9 s2) is nameable
with NO observation: B is the MADELUNG configuration at Z=29,47,79,111, and A is B with one
electron promoted s->d. The 2S/2D selection rule is pure angular momentum and holds for that
pair however it was reached. **So the s73 conclusions are re-derivable from the Madelung side
alone -- but they were not derived that way, and until they are, they are not criterion-4
clean.**
