# F80.3 — perturb80's COMPARATOR TOLERANCE WAS SET BELOW THE SCHEME'S OWN RESOLUTION,
# SO IT LABELLED ORDINARY SCATTER "SECOND BASIN, LOWER -- O-C1 FALSIFIED" THREE TIMES.
# RAISED AGAINST MY OWN INSTRUMENT, IN THE SAME SESSION, BEFORE ANY LABEL WAS REPORTED
# AS A FINDING.

## WHAT THE INSTRUMENT PRINTED
TOL = 1e-6 Ha. Three of ten perturbed runs returned E below the stationary reference by
0.005, 0.030 and 0.034 mHa and were labelled **"SECOND BASIN, LOWER -- O-C1 FALSIFIED"**.
Four returned higher by 0.004 to 0.009 mHa and were labelled "SECOND SOLUTION, HIGHER".

## WHY EVERY ONE OF THOSE LABELS IS WRONG
**F80.2 measured the scheme's own stopping resolution IN THE SAME SESSION: 0.034493 mHa.**
The full spread of the ten runs is -0.0337 to +0.0093 mHa — **a range of 0.043 mHa, the
same size as the restart ladder's own drop.** The deepest "second basin" is 0.0337 mHa
below reference; the ladder alone moves 0.0345 mHa. **The instrument was resolving its own
convergence scatter and calling it structure.**
All ten converged runs agree to 4e-5 Ha on a total energy of -25694 Ha — **agreement to
1.7 parts per billion.**

## THE CORRECT STATEMENT OF THE RESULT
**No second basin is RESOLVED. O-C1 IS NOT FALSIFIED, AND IT IS NOT ESTABLISHED EITHER.**
What the test delivers is a SENSITIVITY, and that is new: **this falsifier can detect a
competing basin only if it lies deeper than about 0.035 mHa below the reference.** A bare
null says nothing; a null with a measured floor says how much has been excluded.

## THE REPAIR, SPECIFIED
TOL must be set from the measured stopping resolution, not chosen. It is not silently
edited here: **the runs are reported with their raw dE and the labels are OVERRIDDEN in
the result by the stated floor**, so the record shows both what the instrument said and
why it was wrong. A future run sets TOL >= 0.05 mHa and re-labels.
