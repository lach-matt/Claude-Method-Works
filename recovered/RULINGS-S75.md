## R-A · THE POISON RULE — ADOPTED AS STANDING LAW.
Proposed at s74 §4 after F74.2. **ACCEPTED BY M AT s75 OPEN.**

  **AN AUDIT BY NAME IS NOT AN AUDIT. THE LEVER MUST BE POISONED AND THE
  CONSUMER MUST DIE.**

PROVENANCE: F54.2 (s54) — the patched name was bypassed positionally, so the
audited name was not the lever used. F74.2 (s74) — the chain calls
`ground.expand`, which `ground_occ` wraps; "nlchain.py: ZERO SITES" was true of
the NAME and false of the THING. Source-tracing reported clean twice; the poison
test caught it. **TWENTY SESSIONS APART, THE SAME FAULT.**
STANDING BEFORE IT: R 1968 (s74) — a can-fail battery written from the
instrument's own patterns is not a can-fail battery; ground truth comes from
outside. **R-A IS THAT LAW APPLIED TO AUDITS RATHER THAN TO BATTERIES.**
FAILURE MODE IT NAMES: an audit that reports on the name it admitted rather than
on the mechanism it was asked about. Cf. R 1671 — an instrument that narrows its
own input reports on what it admitted.
