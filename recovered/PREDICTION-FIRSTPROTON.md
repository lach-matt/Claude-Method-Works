The first proton of f-transit is shared by both fields and is therefore NOT relativistic.
This prediction is a READ of sealed rows only. No new solve. No new number entered.

The s55 RULING is honoured: every comparison below carries n+l for BOTH channels.

## FP-1  IT IS A TIE-BREAK EVENT, NOT AN ORDERING EVENT
At Z=57 the shell that opens is n+l=7 and its members are 4f, 5d, 6p — all n+l=7.
At Z=89 the shell that opens is n+l=8 and its members are 5f, 6d, 7p — all n+l=8.
The observed entrant is 5d at 57 and 6d at 89, each carrying the SAME n+l as the f
channel it beat. PREDICT: no channel of lower n+l is admissible at either atom, so
neither first proton can reach the ordering clause. (F55.2's test, applied forward.)

## FP-2  WHERE THE f CHANNEL SITS INSIDE ITS OWN SHELL
FP-2a  At Z=89, 5f is LAST of the three: shallower than 6d AND shallower than 7p.
FP-2b  At Z=57, 4f is SECOND of the three: deeper than 6p, shallower than 5d.
If 4f is last at 57, or 5f is second at 89, this clause FAILS.

## FP-4  THE SHARED INVARIANT, STATED IN ADVANCE
PREDICT that the one thing true at BOTH opening atoms, and the whole content of the
first proton, is: THE f CHANNEL HAS NOT REACHED THE DEPTH OF THE d CHANNEL OF ITS OWN
SHELL AT THE ATOM WHERE THE SHELL OPENS. PREDICT the two atoms reach that state by

that treats La and Ac as the same case is wrong. (Deliverable 3 §4 already split them;
this predicts the split survives being read as a shell-wide comparison.)

then depth does not decide the first proton and this route is dead.
