answer was no, on provenance: a book stating a value is a value with a citation, a capture is the
served bytes with a quoted destination, and the generators read the captures from disk, so removing

held. RE-MEASURED HERE AND THE FOUR ARE CONFIRMED THE FOUR LARGEST. The reporting chat's full
classification gives regenerable output at about two thirds, irreplaceable raw captures at about
four percent, and a seed of raw plus code plus authored text plus transcripts near fifteen percent;
that classification is carried as reported and NOT re-derived here.** *So the bank has the shape

carrying the seed plus the manifest's hashes, with the receiving session rebuilding, which changes
the coverage clause from regenerate-and-diff to regenerate-and-match-a-hash. Three risks are named
and none is discharged: determinism is established for one generated artefact only, the built
output's chain is unverified, and the present clause catches a stale held copy while the hash form
removes that fault by removing its detector.**
