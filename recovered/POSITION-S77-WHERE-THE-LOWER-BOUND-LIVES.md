are SAMPLING. **A sample exhibits points. It never bounds the set it is drawn from.**

## WHAT THE BOUND ACTUALLY HAS TO BE — AND IT IS NARROWER THAN "ALL CRITICAL POINTS"
Hantsch Thm 2.1 gives an ATTAINED minimiser at each occupancy for N = Z. So the object is
not an infimum over an unbounded family; it is a comparison of TWO ATTAINED MINIMA.
**But the 6f candidate is not a free minimiser at a fixed occupancy at all.** Minimise
E^RHF freely with an added f electron at Z=89 and it does not stay at two nodes — cfg_prev
carries 4f14, so orthogonality to the occupied f manifold forces nd >= 1, which is 5f.
**Two nodes requires orthogonality to a state that is NOT OCCUPIED. The label "6f" is a
node-count constraint imposed by the shooting, not by the functional.**
So the required statement is: **the constrained minimum of E^RHF over the 2-node f sector
lies above the minimum over the 3-node d sector.**

## WHERE THAT IS FREE AND WHERE IT IS NOT
**FREE, AND ALREADY RIGOROUS:** for the LINEAR radial Fock operator at a FIXED field,
Courant-Fischer gives the ordering eps_1 < eps_2 < ... exactly. Higher eigenvalues carry
lower bounds by min-max with no extra work. **In a fixed field, more nodes means higher,
and that is a theorem, not an extrapolation.**
**NOT FREE:** the field is not fixed. It relaxes with the occupancy. **THE ENTIRE GAP IS
THE NONLINEARITY, AND NOTHING ELSE.** What must be bounded is how far self-consistent
relaxation can move a level — not the level itself.
**THAT IS A SPECTRAL-GAP / STABILITY STATEMENT ON THE RADIAL FOCK OPERATOR. IT IS RUNG C.**
Hantsch Remark (b): the gap result (Bach-Lieb-Loss-Solovej, PRL 72 (1994) 2981) is not
known in the restricted theory and its method does not transfer. **Open here, open there.**
