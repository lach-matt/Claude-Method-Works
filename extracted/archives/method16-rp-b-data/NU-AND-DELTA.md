# ν AND δ, SIDE BY SIDE

*Written 2026-08-11 after the WKB route closed. Registers 1341, 1416, and the
three refutations of δ = a√p. This is a comparison of two objects the work has
repeatedly tried to identify, laid out so the identification cannot be attempted
again by accident.*

## WHERE THEY WORK

**ν works on the ordering.** It answers which subshell is entered, one step at a
time, from node counts and capacities with a single carried parameter. Its
corridor is non-empty at 106 of 106, and the same construction applied to the
nuclear shell ordering gives an EMPTY feasible set — so the form can fail and
does not. Within a Madelung group it reproduces the second rule exactly:
a below every crossing (√p_A + √p_B)/2 **is** *smallest n first*.

**δ works on the values.** It is the only object in this work whose cells carry
NUMBERS rather than membership, which is why Λ_spectra is the only index on
which the metric half of the method has anything to measure. It is graded — 205
measured, 5 bracketed, 42 bounded, 954 propagated, 73 formal, 33 unconstrained —
and the grading is honest about how far each statement travelled.

## WHERE THEY DO NOT WORK, AND WHY THEY CANNOT BE JOINED

**They describe different electrons.** ν is about the last bound electron
arriving at the ground configuration. δ is about an outer electron in a series
running to the ionisation limit. Register 1416 said this in words; it is now
measured.

**Their domains are almost disjoint.** Of 114 neutral channels comparable with a
walk step, the walk's entrant (n, ℓ) falls inside the channel's own range in
**5 cases** — Li 2s, Na 3s, K 4s, Al 3p, Ga 4p — and outside it in **109**.
The five are the s- and p-block openings where the ground configuration happens
to be the first member of a series. Everywhere else the objects do not meet.

**And the identification δ = a√p is impossible, three ways over.**

1. **Structurally.** δ is bounded as n → ∞ — that boundedness is what makes a
   Rydberg series converge and why one δ per channel suffices. a√(n−ℓ−1)
   diverges. A bounded quantity cannot equal a divergent one.
2. **Empirically.** Across the 83 held channels spanning ≥ 9 values of n, the
   median |δ| is 0.1392 and the median SPREAD over the whole series is 0.0129.
   He I 1sns ³S runs n = 3–36 with |δ| = 0.2965 and spread 0.0164 — flat to five
   percent — where √p would demand a factor of 5.8.
3. **At the source.** Belokolos states in his own §3.2 that the neutral-atom
   potential goes as −1/r at large r, giving the Rydberg condensation, *whereas
   the Tietz potential decreases more rapidly*. A potential with no Coulomb tail
   supports no Rydberg series, so the WKB integral cannot produce a defect even
   in principle.

Register 1416 had only the numerical refutation and concluded *the failure is
mine, not 1341's*. **It is the relation's.**

## WHAT THIS COSTS

**ν is not an effective quantum number.** It is an ordering functional. Its
square root has only the closure axiom behind it — the demand that the crossing
be linear in the f-values — and the last physical candidate is gone.

**And the Löwdin completion criterion is incompatible with the law.** The handoff
states that the challenge "completes when it populates Λ_spectra with δ per
channel." There is no route from ν to δ: not through 1341, not through the WKB,
not through the Tietz potential. **The criterion and the law are different
objects and one of them must change.**

Three ways to change it, none yet chosen:

- **Narrow the criterion.** The challenge is about ORDERING — which subshell
  fills next — and δ was never part of Löwdin's question. Completion would then
  mean the ordering derived and measured, which is where the work already is.
- **Keep the criterion and change the law.** δ per channel needs a Coulomb-tail
  potential and a core phase shift. That is a different construction from ν and
  would not inherit the corridor.
- **Keep both and drop the join.** Two indexes, two objects, one method — which
  is what register 1341 actually claims: that they share an index, not that one
  number equals the other.

*The third is the honest reading of 1341 and costs least. It also means A5b —
"δ = a√p refuted, the completion still needs a route from the walk to δ" — is
not an open item but a closed one, and should be restated as such.*
