## III · WHAT THE INSTRUMENT IS, STATED ONCE

**THE CORRIDOR.** Posit a form for the ordering. Require the observed order to be
the order the form produces. Each rival gives one linear inequality on the form's
free parameter, so each object confines that parameter to an interval. Then:

> **an EMPTY feasible set REFUTES the form.
> a NON-EMPTY one does not confirm it.**

That asymmetry is the whole of it, and it is why the instrument survives ν's
demotion. It has been applied twice:

- **Nilsson's modified oscillator, nuclear** — six pairs collapse onto 4β + α by
  an identity in ℓ, three demanding each sign. **EMPTY. The form is refuted.**
- **ν, atomic** — 106 of 106 corridors non-empty. **NOT REFUTED**, which is a
  weaker statement than it has been made to carry.

*Register 1366 said this at the time and the work then read the non-emptiness as
confirmation for a hundred and fifty registers.*

## IV · WHY ν WAS BUILT, AND WHY IT IS DONE

Registers 1302–1303: the quantum-defect route failed, and 1303 gave the reason —
a Rydberg defect measures one electron outside a fixed core while the filling
order is a many-electron question, so **no capture of Rydberg series will close
that gap.** 1306 replaced an aufbau table with observed configurations. 1307
found the observed order differs from Madelung at La and Ac. 1308 corrected
admissibility from orthogonality to Pauli and unified the radicand as the node
theorem. **1309 is ν.**

**And 1311 localised the gap immediately:** a transfer index over seven inputs
and five outputs, E = 0, with *the derivation gap not diffuse but one target cell
fed by two source cells* — the amplitude **a**, whose sources are
electron–electron repulsion and exchange, **and the law draws on neither.**

So ν was scaffolding raised after the defect route collapsed, with one member
known to be unsupported from the day it was built. It did its work: it produced
the corridor, and the corridor refuted a fifty-year-old nuclear family.
**Everything ν has failed at since is the one cell 1311 named.**
