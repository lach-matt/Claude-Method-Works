Where the index stops, and why

Every cell is one ion. The triangle is the rule charge ≤ Z; the diagonals running up-right are the isoelectronic count N = Z − charge + 1. Dark diagonals are the counts whose multiplicity fibre holds a single value — the aufbau openings, where one electron begins a new subshell. Extending the bound to 120 adds the pale wedge on the right.
