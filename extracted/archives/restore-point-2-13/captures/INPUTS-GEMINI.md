## INPUT 1 · Temperature and the (n, ℓ, k, T) index

**Verdict: ONE GENUINELY OPEN CLAIM — board row 4b.**

The proposal: a 3D lattice on (n, ℓ, k) with Temperature as a 4th axis,
converting a static structural lattice into a dynamic continuum. Four regimes
along T:

| regime | T | what happens to the lattice |
|---|---|---|
| ground state | → 0 K | rigid, crisp, voxelated; every electron in its lowest coordinate |
| chemical | 100 – 3,000 K | valence layer "pulses"; core stays rigid; phases split |
| plasma | 10⁴ – 10⁶ K | outer shells strip; coordinates dissolve from the outside in |
| nuclear | > 10⁷ K | complete stripping; the lattice collapses to bare Z |

**The capacity claim, VERIFIED at R 1615:** Σ 2n² over n = 1..N equals
N(N+1)(2N+1)/3. Correct — twice the standard square-sum identity.

**The thermal operators named:** Boltzmann occupation, Fermi-Dirac with
chemical potential μ(T), the Saha equation for ionisation, and a proposed
suppression factor Θ ≈ exp(−k_B T / |E_n|) forcing bound occupancy to zero
when thermal energy exceeds binding energy.

**Why it is not automatically refused.** `A.cert` (R 1609) admits only
relabel, re-coordinatise, refine a fibration, drop a coordinate. Adding T
looks like the forbidden fifth operation — **but T differs from the
penetration regime in the way that matters: the regime is a FUNCTION of
(Z, charge, ℓ) and T is not.** A cell can be at any temperature. So the
closure test has not been run and its answer is not predictable from R 1608.

**Numbers held unverified:** 13.6 eV, and the regime edges at 10⁴ and 10⁷ K.

## INPUT 2 · Spacetime transition as catalytic phase change

**Verdict: NO OBJECT IN ANY INDEX HERE. Frame only.**

The reframing: in a metastable medium the energy to trigger a transition is
*activation* energy, not the total energy of the transition — as a nucleation
seed in supercooled water does not supply the latent heat it releases. The
large observed energy would then be **backreaction** (vacuum polarisation,
pair production, gravitational radiation) rather than input power, and the
engineering problem inverts from generating power to surviving output.

Named: Alcubierre metric, Morris-Thorne wormholes, brane cosmology, vacuum
decay.

**The transferable shape, if it ever becomes relevant:** *a small precise
perturbation releasing latent structure already present.* That is the shape of
a seed in this project's own sense (`S.seed`), and if the thought ever finds an
object here, it will be through that door — not through spacetime.

## INPUT 3 · The zero-energy projective trigger

**Verdict: POINTERS are real; the claim has no object here.**

The argument: if no work is done, a trigger cannot be an energy source, so it
must be an *informational or topological state selector* — an operator that
makes ⟨B|A⟩ non-zero rather than transporting anything.

Three mechanisms named, all real physics:

- **Aharonov-Bohm** — a phase shift where E = B = 0, so no force acts and no
  energy transfers, driven by a non-zero vector potential alone.
- **Joint Bell-state measurement** — projecting a state without imparting
  energy, via passive elements that mix quantum paths.
- **Chern number toggling** — a topological phase change at constant energy,
  discrete because the invariant is an integer.

**Landauer's principle** is invoked correctly as the constraint: the kT ln 2
cost applies to *erasure*, so a purely unitary reversible operation has a
theoretical lower bound of zero.

**Worth noting for this project:** the distinction between an operation that
*moves* a system and one that *re-indexes* it is exactly the distinction
between a coordinate and a key (R 1599). The physics is not ours; the
distinction already is.

**THE 14D CLOSURE ARGUMENT IS BACKWARDS (R 1615).** It rests on Bott
periodicity: real Clifford algebras satisfy C(k+8) ≅ C(k) ⊗ ℝ(16), and 14 is
read as 8+6, "the sixth position of the second octave". The periodicity is
real. **The inference inverts it: periodicity says the structure REPEATS,
which gives no residue privileged status and is precisely a reason a hierarchy
does NOT terminate.**

**TESTED AT REGISTER 1624.** Three claims hold as facts, not metaphors:

**The Markov blanket FAILS on test** — 210 witnessed cells touch the exterior

**And the observer-as-4D-object claim is untestable here**, because this project

**What is shared is the SHAPE:** a system whose own state determines what it can

## INPUT 5 · The three-body problem and the Löwdin challenge

**Verdict: PREMISE LITERATURE-CORRECT; one object claimed that we do not hold.**

**On the three-body problem.** Poincaré's non-integrability is stated
correctly. Three operations proposed for a higher-dimensional framework:

1. **mass-invariant coordinates** — Jacobi vectors or mass-weighted metric
   tensors, so trajectories are independent of explicit masses
2. **geodesic mapping** — Maupertuis' principle, chaotic trajectories as
   smooth geodesics on a curved configuration manifold
3. **singularity elimination** — Levi-Civita or Kustaanheimo-Stiefel
   regularisation at collision points

**The benchmark offered:** the figure-8 choreography (Chenciner & Montgomery),
testing whether unequal masses deform the manifold without breaking closure.
**This is a real and well-posed test and `CHAPTER-THREEBODY.md` does not
currently attempt it.**

**On Löwdin.** The premise is correct and the search confirms it: Löwdin 1969
posed the n+ℓ derivation as a major unsolved problem; Allen & Knight replied
in Int. J. Quantum Chem. **90**, 80 (2002); Scerri commented in **109**, 959
(2009) and records **~20 exceptions to the rule** — which corroborates
register 1594's La/Ce and Ac/Pa from the other direction.

**One claim names an object we do not hold:** "the 36-cell null matrix". No
such object exists in `mathreg` or any index here. If M has a source for it,
it needs one; otherwise it is not ours.

**The Löwdin test offered** — derive the 4s/3d crossover at potassium from
geometry alone — is well-posed and is *not* the same as deriving n+ℓ. Worth
keeping distinct, because our chapter's finding is that n+ℓ is the **wrong
target** and the *order* is the right one.

## INPUT 6 · Montgomery-Odlyzko and GUE statistics

**Verdict: ONE STRONG PRIOR-ART HIT; the application to our data REFUTED.**

The account is standard and correct: Montgomery's 1972 pair correlation
function, Dyson's recognition of it as the GUE form, Odlyzko's 1987 numerical
confirmation near the 10²⁰-th zero, the Hilbert-Pólya conjecture, and
Berry-Keating's chaotic-Hamiltonian reading.

**The hit that matters is not the zeta function.** Following it led to

> **Rosenzweig, N. & Porter, C. E., *Repulsion of Energy Levels in Complex
> Atomic Spectra*, Phys. Rev. 120, 1698 (1960)**

which is **prior art for this session's jK rebuild**: level statistics in
atomic spectra require grouping by **good quantum numbers** before they mean
anything. That is the keyability problem of register 1597, stated in 1960.

**And the test on our own data failed its control (R 1615).** The r-statistic
gave 0.328 lumped and 0.539 grouped against GOE's 0.536 — a textbook match.
But requiring six levels per group raises it to 0.619, and **a pure 1/n²
Rydberg series with no chaos in it whatever scores 0.644.** Our levels *are*
Rydberg series; the statistic measures the 1/n³ series law, not repulsion.
