What stands in the way

Not probabilities — named obstacles, each of which may or may not be lifted. The 245 cells with no bound at all are the working queue. That number was 2,929 until register 1597 added keyability: a defect needs a single 2S+1, and a species with a hole plus an electron, or two valence electrons, yields only a multiplet mean. 17,641 cells fail that test — and finding it cost nothing but reading eight spectra already on disk.
