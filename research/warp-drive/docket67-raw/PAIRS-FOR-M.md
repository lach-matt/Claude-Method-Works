# arXiv / journal pairs for M to confirm (ruling M-D67-1)

Ruling M-D67-1: *"a formal paper published to Arxiv.org carries the same weight as the same paper published in a journal and may be used for our purposes, as long as I have confirmed they are the same paper."*

**Scope.** The 58 arXiv papers DOCKET 67's audits read at source for a result keyed by that paper. Not listed: the 10 keyed papers read only by name (nothing was read to confirm), and papers read only as a later author's restatement of an older original (the originals, e.g. Misner-Sharp 1964, have no arXiv version). Kuo & Ford (gr-qc/9304008) is already confirmed (COMPARED-BY-M) and is listed for completeness.

**How the journal references were found.** arxiv.org, export.arxiv.org, INSPIRE, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, APS and PubMed are blocked by this session's network policy, so two agents used alphaXiv (titles, authors) and web search (journal references). `confirmed` = a record (ADS, INSPIRE, OSTI, publisher page, or the paper's own typeset pages) states the pairing; `inferred` = only a search summary named it -- check these first. A DOI marked (pattern) was built from the publisher's numbering, not read off a page. **Unverified by the board; nothing here is seated.** Full evidence per row: `PAIRS-EVIDENCE.json`.

**Version cautions (read before confirming).**

- **2505.02210** (Pisana et al.): the audit read arXiv **v4 (April 2026), which is later than** the journal version, Phys. Rev. D 112, 064067 (2025); there is also an erratum, Phys. Rev. D 113, 109901 (2026). The ruling needs the pair to be the same paper -- v4 may not be.
- **Errata exist** for gr-qc/9305017 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 75, 1872 (1995), withdraws a secondary claim) and gr-qc/0209036 (Phys. Rev. D 80, 069903 (2009)). An erratum is part of the journal record the arXiv copy may lack.
- **gr-qc/9805003** (Olum): a second arXiv number, gr-qc/9806091, carries the same title.
- **0804.2182**: the audit attached PRL 111, 221601 (2013); that is Blanco & Casini (1309.1121). This paper is Class. Quantum Grav. 25, 205021 (2008).
