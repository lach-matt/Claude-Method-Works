## Summary

The paper's result stands. For the massless minimally coupled scalar there is no state-independent spatially averaged quantum inequality over a bounded region in four-dimensional Minkowski space, and the paper's witness family shows it. We reproduced the ln Λ coefficients of ρ₂ and ρ₁ from the exact integrands. We have two small findings: two cross-reference misprints and a parenthesis, and one sufficient condition on p. 10 that is not sufficient as literally written. The condition that does work is the one the paper itself gives on p. 8. Neither finding affects the conclusion. Both are discrepancies, not refutations.

## Findings

### 1. Misprints: "the integral in Eq. (5)" and Eq. (33) (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 4, after Eq. (10)): "We will require that the functional form of b(k₁, k₂) be such that the integral in Eq. (5) remain finite in this limit". On p. 5: "The integral in Eq. (5) can now be written as …".
- **What we find:**
  - Eq. (5) is the normalisation of the sampling functions, ∫dx F_S(x) = ∫dt F_T(t) = 1.
  - The integral that must stay finite as Λ → ∞, and that is then rewritten, is the one in the state normalisation, Eq. (10): ∫d³k₁ d³k₂ |b(k₁, k₂)|².
  - Both references should read Eq. (10).
  - Eq. (33) has an unbalanced parenthesis in the text read.
- **Shown by:** reading pp. 3-5; `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard10.json`, key `gr-qc/0208045`, field `rederivation.outcome` ("MISPRINTS").
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 2. p. 10: "we can choose λ₀ > r₀" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 10): the energy density varies in space only over the scale λ₀ = 2π/p₀ (43). Then: "For a given sampling function F(x), we can choose λ₀ > r₀, so that the energy density is approximately constant over the region being sampled …, yet the energy density in that region is arbitrarily negative."
- **What we find:**
  - λ₀ > r₀ alone does not keep ρ negative over the whole sampled region.
  - The spatial profile f₂ of Eq. (27) first changes sign at p₀r = **3.8703**, which is r = 0.616 λ₀.
  - At leading log, ρ₁ + ρ₂ < 0 only for p₀r below about **3.80-3.86** (for χ₀ between 0.9 and 0.1 of the Eq. (41) threshold).
  - So a region with 0.62 λ₀ ≲ r₀ < λ₀ satisfies λ₀ > r₀ but reaches past the first sign change.
  - The paper's own gloss in the same sentence, that the energy density is "approximately constant over the region being sampled", already points to the stronger condition.
  - The condition that works is the paper's own p. 8 one, r ≪ 1/p₀ (equivalently r₀ ≪ λ₀). Since p₀ is arbitrary, it can always be met, and the conclusion that S(F) is unbounded below is unaffected.

- **Class:** discrepancy.

## What stands

- **The theorem.** No spatially averaged quantum inequality, uniform over states, exists for the massless minimally coupled scalar over a bounded region in 4D Minkowski space.
- **The ln Λ coefficients.** ρ₂'s coefficient 16π²/(15p₀⁵) reproduces from the exact integrand, to 1.15 × 10⁻⁸ on the shell [10³, 10⁴], converging as p₀²/k². ρ₁'s coefficient 2(4π/3)² 4π p₀⁶ also reproduces (Monte Carlo weight 1.999989 ± 0.000002 against 2).
- **The structural results.** Eqs. (14), (23), (25)-(29), (37), (38) and the threshold of Eq. (41) re-derive. The all-space integral removes ρ₂ (Sec. III).
- **The witness states satisfy the temporal inequality.** This is as stated on p. 10. Every finite-Λ member is Hadamard (p. 8).
