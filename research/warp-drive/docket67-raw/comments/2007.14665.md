## What stands

- **The main result:** we raise nothing against the local existence and uniqueness of solutions of the semiclassical Einstein equation on flat FLRW, for a massive scalar field with ξ ≠ 1/6 and "sufficiently regular" states compatible with the initial data. The proof turns the traced equation into a fixed-point equation and applies the Banach fixed-point theorem (Prop. 5.8, Thm 5.9).
- **The method:** the adjudication read the proof and found nothing against its structure. It was read, not machine-checked.

## Finding

### Abstract against theorems: the coupling. Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.** The abstract (p. 1) says existence and uniqueness are proved for a scalar field "with arbitrary coupling to the scalar curvature".

**What we found.**
- The results that carry the proof are stated for ξ ≠ 1/6 (Prop. 4.1, Prop. 4.2, Thm 4.4, Prop. 5.8), or contain factors (6ξ − 1)⁻¹ that do not exist at ξ = 1/6 (Lemma 5.7). The constants c_ξ and M_c of (44) are undefined at ξ = 1/6, as the paper notes after Prop. 4.2 (p. 23). Sec. 5.2 (p. 33) itself announces the main result "for a fixed arbitrary coupling parameter ξ ≠ 1/6".
- The paper itself says on p. 23 that the conformal case ξ = 1/6 "with suitable initial conditions given at finite conformal time and with a suitable choice of renormalization constants has already been solved in [46, 47]".
- So "arbitrary" in the abstract is accurate for the paper together with its refs. [46, 47], under the conditions those works assume. It is not accurate for the theorems proved here.

A small point, recorded only for precision.
