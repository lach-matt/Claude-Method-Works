## Summary

The paper's inputs and the phase-space arithmetic behind its estimate check. The physical argument behind the estimate is not something we can check here, in either direction. Our one finding is a **numerical discrepancy** in a quoted scale. On p. 4 the paper gives E₀ = √6 π m_W/α_W ≈ 15 TeV. At the paper's own inputs (p. 1), the formula gives 18.5 TeV. It is a discrepancy (misprint class), not a refutation. E₀ appears only where the paper reviews the earlier single-sphaleron expansion. It does not enter the paper's own estimate.

## Finding

### p. 4, eq. (12): the value of E₀ (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 4, below eq. (12)): "E₀ = √6 π m_W/α_W ≃ 15 TeV". This is the scale of the low-energy expansion of F(E) quoted from the earlier literature.
- **What we find:**
  - The paper's inputs (p. 1) are g ≃ 0.645, α_W = g²/4π ≃ 1/30 and m_W = gv/2 ≃ 80 GeV.
  - With m_W = 80 GeV and α_W = 1/30, √6 π m_W/α_W = **18.47 TeV**.
  - With present inputs (the tree's α_W and m_W), it is 18.24 TeV.
  - The printed 15 TeV is about 19 % below the formula's value. The ratio is 15/18.47 = 0.81.
  - We do not identify a cause.

- **Class:** discrepancy (misprint class).

### What it does not touch

- The paper's own estimate, κ ~ 10⁻³ in eq. (4) and Sec. XI, uses E_sph = 9.0 TeV and d ≈ 4. It does not use E₀.

## What stands

- **The inputs on p. 1.** g ≃ 0.645 gives α_W = g²/4π ≃ 1/30 (1/α_W = 30.2). m_W = gv/2 at v = 246 GeV gives 79.3 GeV, printed "≃ 80 GeV".
- **The solid-angle fraction behind eq. (41).** The paper's exact expression (the hypergeometric form above eq. (41), p. 10) reduces at d = 4 to (1 − c)²(2 + c)/2 with c = √(E_sph/E_qq), which we reproduce. Its near-threshold form (41) is (1 − c)² at d = 4, a factor 3/2 below the exact fraction there. We note this only as information: it lies within the "~" of (41) and the large uncertainty the paper itself attaches to κ (p. 10), and it is not a finding (`rederive/sphaleron-instanton-and-collider-dispute.out`, lines 42-47).
- **The collider-rate dissent.** The paper's central claim is that multi-sphaleron (Bloch-wave) effects remove the exponential suppression above E_sph. That claim rests on a one-dimensional quantum-mechanical reduction with free factors. It cannot be checked here in either direction, and we offer no view on it.
