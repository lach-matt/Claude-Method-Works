## Finding

### Eq. (9) does not integrate to eq. (11): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 3):
  - eq. (9): T₀₀(r) = (1/8π)[6m_fluid R²/(r² + R²)^{5/2} + αr²/(r² + R²)³ − λ_p αR²/(r² + R²)³];
  - eq. (11): M = ∫d³r T₀₀ = m_fluid + 3πα/(32R) − λ_p 5πα/(32R);
  - eq. (12), with λ_p = 1: M = m_fluid − πα/(16R).
- **What we find:**
  - Integrating (9) as printed, the third term gives −λ_p πα/(32R). The total is M = m_fluid + 3πα/(32R) − πα/(32R) = **m_fluid + πα/(16R)**, not (11).
  - The paper's own equations supply the missing factor:
    - by (6), ρ_fluid contains −5κn^{6/5};
    - by (18), the Poincaré-stress part of p(r) is λ_p κn^{6/5} = λ_p αR²/(8π(r² + R²)³);
    - so the third term of (9) should carry 5λ_p.
  - With the factor 5, (9) integrates to (11) and (12); this stage re-checked both integrals symbolically (sympy). The numbers in (13), 42,351.306 and 70,585.510 MeV, are also in the ratio 3 : 5 that (11) implies.
  - This may be a typesetting slip. Two independent reads of the extracted text show the same thing.

- **Class:** discrepancy.

## What stands

Each item below was re-checked symbolically (sympy) in the verification stage, from the paper's printed equations.

- **The mass decomposition.** Eqs. (11)-(13), with binding energy E_bind = −πα/(16R), follow from (9) once the Poincaré-stress term carries the factor 5 that (6) and (18) supply.
- **The stress distributions.** s(r) and p(r) in (17)-(18) satisfy the conservation relation (16a) identically and the von Laue condition (16b) at λ_p = 1.
- **The long-distance forms (19).** α/(8πr⁴), −α/(4πr⁴) and α/(24πr⁴), as Maxwell theory requires.
- **Not assessed.** The form factors (24)-(29), including the QED-matching leading terms, were not re-derived here; we make no statement about them.
