## Finding

### Problem 3, eq. (109), p. 42: the spatial Ricci components. Class: discrepancy (misprint in a problem-set hint)

**What the lectures state.** Eq. (109) gives R_ij = δ_ij [2ȧ² + aä + 2k/a²].

**What we found.**
- In the r-form of eq. (1), the computed component is R_rr = (aä + 2ȧ² + 2k)/(1 − kr²).
- The printed form agrees only at k = 0 in Cartesian coordinates. For k ≠ 0 the spatial components are proportional to the spatial metric, R_ij = [(aä + 2ȧ² + 2k)/a²] g_ij, not to δ_ij.
- R_00 and the Ricci scalar printed in the same problem are correct.
