## Summary

We raise nothing against the paper's T₀ inferences or its account of how T₀ enters; we did not re-run the fits. Our one finding is minor: the same tension is quoted as 3.3σ and as 3.2σ. Both figures can be recovered from Table I, depending on which side of an asymmetric error bar is used. It is a discrepancy in presentation, not a refutation.

## Finding

### pp. 16-17: the Planck + SH0ES against FIRAS tension, 3.3σ and 3.2σ (discrepancy)

- **The paper states:**
  - p. 16: "The optimal values of T₀ from Planck + SH0ES and FIRAS are separated by ΔT₀ = 0.16 K, which corresponds to 3.3 standard deviations (adding in quadrature the Planck + SH0ES and the FIRAS errors)."
  - The same paragraph continues: "we have traded the usual 4.1σ Hubble tension … for a 3.2σ T₀ tension between FIRAS and Planck+SH0ES."
  - p. 17: "T₀ = 2.56 ± 0.05 K. This measurement is in significant (3.3σ) tension with the FIRAS measurement."
- **What we find:**
  - Table I (p. 15) gives Planck + SH0ES T₀ = 2.564 (+0.049 / −0.051) K, and FIRAS 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K. So ΔT₀ = 0.1615 K.
  - In quadrature with the upper error 0.049 (the side towards FIRAS): **3.30σ**.
  - With the lower error 0.051: **3.17σ**.
  - With the symmetric ±0.050: **3.23σ**.
  - With p. 17's rounded 2.56 ± 0.05: 3.31σ.
  - With the paragraph's own rounded ΔT₀ = 0.16 K and ±0.050: 3.20σ.
  - So "3.3σ" and "3.2σ" are both recoverable, but by different choices, and the paragraph does not say which is meant. The difference has no bearing on the paper's conclusions.
- **Shown by:** Table I and pp. 16-17, re-read at source on 2026-10-04. The arithmetic above is all that is involved. See also `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/adjudications/0911.1955.json`, `corrections_to_record` (the Ivanov item: "a direct quadrature from Table I gives 3.23. That is a discrepancy inside the later source, recorded only").
- **Class:** discrepancy (presentation).

## What stands

- **Table I's T₀ inferences** (p. 15):
  - Planck alone, 3.144 (+0.17 / −0.065) K;
  - Planck + SH0ES, 2.564 (+0.049 / −0.051) K;
  - Planck + BAO, 2.706 (+0.019 / −0.020) K.
- **The earlier Planck 2015 + BAO value**, 2.718 ± 0.021 K (eq. 3).
- **How T₀ enters.** At fixed baryon-to-photon and dark-matter-to-photon ratios, the recombination temperature is fixed, and T₀ acts mainly through the angular-diameter distance to last scattering (pp. 2-3).
- **The conclusion.** Planck + BAO agrees with FIRAS, and BAO "seems to arbiter in favor of Planck + FIRAS" (p. 17).
