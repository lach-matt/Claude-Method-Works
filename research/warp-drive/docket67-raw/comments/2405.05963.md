## Summary

Section 5.2's request, and the doubt about the semiclassical regime that motivates it, stand. Our one finding is a **small imprecision** in describing the HPS throat size. The section says the throat is "of Planck length". HPS's plotted throat is about 0.024 l_P, and every throat in their class is below 0.03 l_P. The imprecision runs in the direction that strengthens the author's own doubt, and it may be intended as order-of-magnitude wording. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Sec. 5.2, p. 19: "of Planck length" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 19): "An interesting, semiclassically self-consistent wormhole solution is using nonminimal coupling to the curvature [90]. The throat of the wormhole derived is of Planck length or up to the order 10² ℓ_pl, so it is doubtful that it can be considered in the regime of the semiclassical approximation."
- **What we find:**
  - **The plotted throat.** HPS's plotted solution has ln f(0) = −2/3. Their eq. (9), r(0) = √(−16K² ln f(0)), then gives r(0) = 1/√(540π) l_P = **0.0243 l_P**. HPS print "~0.02 l_P". That is 1.6 decades below the Planck length.
  - **The whole class.** Every throat in HPS's boundary-data class (9), −1 ≤ ln f(0) < 0, satisfies r(0) ≤ 1/√(360π) l_P = **0.0297 l_P**.
  - **The upper end.** "Up to the order 10² ℓ_pl" matches the other values HPS state: 67 l_P on p. 7, and the 200-300 l_P local solutions mentioned on p. 8, which use other values of ξ and other boundary data.
  - **Direction.** If anything, the regime doubt is understated at the lower end. At the plotted throat, l_P² |Riem|_max = 540π ≈ 1696.

## What stands

- **The request.** To examine the HPS solution using one of the QEIs for non-minimally coupled fields, [17] (Fliss, Freivogel, Kontou and Santos, arXiv:2309.10848) and [30] (Fewster and Osterbrink, J. Phys. A 41 (2008) 025402). The bibliography identifies [90] as HPS.
- **The regime doubt.** It is borne out by the computation above: Planckian curvature at the plotted throat. HPS's 67 l_P example also has a throat curvature radius of about 1.4 l_P.
- **p. 25.** "A quantum gravity regime wormhole with a throat of Planck scale size [90] would not be traversable."
- **p. 9.** State-dependent bounds have been derived for the non-minimally coupled field [17, 29, 30].
