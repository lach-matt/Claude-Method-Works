## Summary

The treatment of the Maxwell stress tensor in Chapters 7 and 8 stands. Our one finding is a **sign misprint** in the flat-space version, eq. (1.111), which disagrees with the same notes' (7.107) and (8.25). It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Eq. (1.111): the prefactor −1/(4π) (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 30): T^{μν}_{e+m} = (−1/4π)(F^{μλ}F^ν_λ − ¼η^{μν}F^{λσ}F_{λσ}). It adds that "T⁰⁰ in each case is equal to what you would expect the energy density to be."
- **What we find:**
  - The notes use η = diag(−1, 1, 1, 1) (1.8) and F^{0i} = E^i (1.75). With these:
    - F^{0λ}F^0_λ = E²;
    - F^{λσ}F_{λσ} = 2(B² − E²);
    - η^{00} = −1.
  - So, as printed, T⁰⁰ = −(1/4π)[E² + ½(B² − E²)] = **−(E² + B²)/8π**, which is negative.
  - The same notes print the prefactor **+1/(4π)**: (7.107), p. 201, and (8.25), p. 222. With +1/(4π), T⁰⁰ = (E² + B²)/8π.
  - (8.25) has exactly the index structure of (1.111), T^{μν} = (1/4π)(F^{μλ}F^ν_λ − ¼g^{μν}F^{λσ}F_{λσ}), so the opposite sign cannot come from a different index placement or contraction order.
  - So (1.111) carries a sign misprint.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/maxwell-stress-energy-curved.py` and `.out` (part D: "with −1/(4π) [Carroll 1.111 as printed]: T⁰⁰ = −(E²+B²)/8π"); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard04.json`, key `maxwell-stress-energy-curved`, fields `hypothesis_drift` and `rederivation.outcome` (D).
- **Class:** discrepancy (a sign misprint).

## What stands

- **(7.107):** the Maxwell stress tensor, with prefactor +1/(4π).
- **(7.108):** the most general spherically symmetric field strength, F_{tr} = f(r, t) and F_{θφ} = g(r, t) sin θ.
- **(7.109):** the curved-space Maxwell equations.
- **The charged black hole:** the Reissner-Nordström solution that follows.
- **(8.25)-(8.26):** the stress tensor is traceless.
