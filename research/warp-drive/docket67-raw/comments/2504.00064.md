## Finding

### p. 7, "v² = 8/9" beside "2p_t/ρ → 1". Class: discrepancy (internal tension in the restatement)

**What the paper states.** p. 7: "… in the limit, p_r/ρ → 0 and 2p_t/ρ → 1. As expected, particle velocity attains the value, v² = 8/9." p. 7 continues: "Buchdahl star may therefore be envisaged as made up of free particles moving with velocity, v² = 8/9, in a very thin shell".

**What we found.**
- For a thin shell of particles all moving tangentially with one local speed v (in the static frame), σ = nμγ and p_t = nμγv²/2, so 2p_t/σ = v².
- On that reading, 2p_t/ρ → 1 means the local speed tends to v → 1, not v² = 8/9.
- The value v² = 8/9 matches the paper's own virial relation on p. 3: "M/R = 4/9 for massive particles with velocity, v² = 8/9". It does not follow from the limit restated from refs. [11, 12].
- So the two statements in the same passage cannot both hold for a single local speed.

If v on p. 7 denotes a different velocity (for example, one not measured by a static observer at the shell), a definition would remove the tension.
