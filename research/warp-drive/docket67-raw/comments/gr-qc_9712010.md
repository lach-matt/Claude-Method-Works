## Finding

### Eq. (56), the sign of the time transformation (p. 16). Class: discrepancy (misprint)

**What the paper states.** For the canonical acoustic black hole (Sec. 8), eq. (56) gives the change of time coordinate that takes the line element (55) to the diagonal form (57). The ± in (56) is printed matched to the ± of (55).

**What we found.**
- Taken with that matching, the transformation leaves an off-diagonal term proportional to 2c r_0²/r², so (57) does not follow.
- The paper's own general transformation, eq. (62), dτ = dt + v·dx/(c² − v²), gives the opposite sign for this inflow. That sign removes the cross term and yields (57) exactly.
- We therefore read (56) as a sign misprint: ∓ in place of ±.
- (57) and everything downstream of it are unaffected.

**What shows it.** `rederive/acoustic-metric-analogue.py`, section 5, checks on "eq (62) sign removes the cross term" and on (57). Re-run 2026-10-04: all checks pass, and the discrepancy is printed as recorded. See also `shards/shard08.json`, key `acoustic-metric-analogue`, rederivation.outcome.

## Scope

This finding concerns arXiv:gr-qc/9712010v2 as read. The CQG print was not read or compared, and nothing here is a claim about it. It is a misprint, a discrepancy, and not a refutation.
