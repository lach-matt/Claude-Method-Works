## Summary

We raise nothing against the paper's main result, that the NEC is violated (or on the verge of violation) at a dynamic wormhole throat under the hypotheses the paper states; its spherically symmetric form, eqs. (72)-(77), recomputes from the metric. So does the Sec. 7 point that spatial flare-out need not imply null flare-out once the geometry is time dependent. We found five places where the text layer differs from what we compute. None changes a sign, and none carries a conclusion. We list them for the authors' convenience, as discrepancies, not refutations. We cannot tell whether they are in the typeset text.

## Findings

All five were computed from the metric with a hand-written Christoffel and Einstein routine, not from the printed formulas.

### 1. Eq. (83), p. 27: the flare-out of the static throat (discrepancy)

- **Text layer:** dθ±/du± at r₀ = (1/r₀²)(−b′(r₀) + 1).
- **Computed:** (1/(2r₀²))(1 − b′(r₀)).
- The two differ by a positive factor of 2. The sign, and with it the simple flare-out conclusion, is unchanged.

### 2. Eq. (86), p. 28: the expansion θ₊ (discrepancy)

- **Text layer:** θ₊ = ½ e^ψ (1 − 2m/r).
- **Computed:** e^ψ (1 − 2m/r)/r.
- The paper's own eq. (88), the flare-out −(2/r²) e^ψ ∂m/∂v at θ₊ = 0, follows only from the computed form. Both forms vanish at the same place, 2m = r.

### 3. Eq. (89), p. 28: the null-null Einstein equation (discrepancy)

- **Text layer:** ∂m/∂v at θ₊ = 0 equals 4πr² T_vv.
- **Computed:** the right-hand side carries a further factor e^{−ψ}.
- The sign is unchanged.

### 4. Eq. (47), p. 18: the null vectors l± = ½(V ± n) (discrepancy)

- **Text layer:** the normalisation factor is ½.
- **Computed:** 1/√2 is needed for the inner-product relations (15) that the text says (47) preserves.
- The normalisation is positive either way, so no sign that the theorems use changes.

### 5. Sec. 6.1.1, p. 24: "only one extremal hypersurface" (discrepancy, wording)

- **The paper states:** "There is therefore, always only one extremal hypersurface in the first patch."
- **Computed:** take HV's family (69) with b = r₀²/r, r₀ = 1, and Ω̇/Ω = 1/4 at t = 0 (an expanding background). Then eq. (74) has two roots in the first patch, r* = 1.03528 and 3.86370. At both, θ₋ = 0.
- The sentence follows directly on the observation that only θ₋ can vanish in an expanding background and only θ₊ in a collapsing one, which (74) does show. That supports reading it as one *kind* of extremal surface, and on that reading it is correct. Read literally, as one surface, it does not hold for this b.

## What stands

- **The dynamic-throat theorem.** At a marginally anti-trapped surface satisfying the flare-out condition, T_ab l^a l^b ≤ 0, strict under strong flare-out (eqs. 50-51). We raise nothing against it under the hypotheses as stated. We recomputed it in spherical symmetry only (eq. 77 below); the general, symmetry-free statement was read, not re-derived.
- **Eqs. (15)/(71) and (72)-(77)** recompute from the metric and agree. On θ = 0, dθ/du = −4π(ρ − τ).
- **HV (75)** agrees in closed form with the computed flare-out at both extremal spheres of the worked family (−4.1920 and −5.0580).
- **The family (69)** behaves as the analysis requires. With b = r₀²/r and a time-dependent conformal factor, the t = 0 slice flares out spatially at r₀, while r₀ is not null-extremal. This is the Sec. 7 non-implication, and it is reproduced.
- **Eqs. (85), (87), (88) and (90)** recompute and agree.
