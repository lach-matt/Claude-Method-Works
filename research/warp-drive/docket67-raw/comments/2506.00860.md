## Summary

We assessed only the paper's restatement of Gödel's metric in its introduction. The cosmographic analysis, the fits and the model comparison were **not assessed**, and nothing here bears on them. The relations stated in the text under eq. (1) are Gödel's correct ones.

Our one finding is a **misprint in eq. (1)**: as printed, the metric is degenerate. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Eq. (1): the coefficient of e^{2x} dy² (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 1): Gödel's 1949 metric as ds² = a²(dt² − 2eˣ dt dy + e^{2x} dy² − dx² − dz²).
- **What we find:**
  - As printed, the (t, y) block of the metric is [[1, −eˣ], [−eˣ, e^{2x}]], with determinant e^{2x} − e^{2x} = 0. So **det g = 0**: the metric is degenerate.
  - Requiring dust plus a cosmological constant, with u ∝ ∂_t, forces the coefficient of e^{2x} dy² to be **1/2**, uniquely. That is Gödel's value.
    - Then ρ = 1/(8πGa²), Λ = −1/(2a²) and det g = −a⁸e^{2x}/2 < 0.
    - The sign of the cross term is a matter of y → −y.
  - **A cross-check from the paper's own eq. (2).** Writing (1) in the form of (2), with t′ = at, R = a and m = 1, gives σ = 1 and k = −1, so k + σ = 0. By the paper's own determinant, |g_{μν}| = −(k + σ)e^{2mx} (p. 3; a factor R⁶ is not written there, which does not affect the conclusion), this vanishes. Gödel's coefficient 1/2 gives k = −1/2 and k + σ = 1/2 > 0. We did this cross-check in this drafting stage; it uses only eqs. (1) and (2) as printed.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/godel-1949-ctc-metric.py` and `.out` (checks E1a′, E1a″, E1a, E1i); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard02.json`, key `godel-1949-ctc-metric`, fields `hypothesis_drift` and `grade_evidence` (3).
- **Class:** discrepancy (a misprint in a restatement).

## What stands

- **The relations under eq. (1).** The text gives ω² = 1/2a² = 4πGε = −Λ. These are Gödel's correct relations (check E1i), and they hold for the metric with coefficient 1/2.
- **Gödel's metric itself** solves the field equations with ρ = 1/(8πGa²) and Λ = −1/(2a²).
- **The rest of the paper was not assessed.** This includes the non-stationary metric (2) and its use, the Kristian-Sachs expansion, the Pantheon+ fits and the AIC comparison. We make no statement about them.
