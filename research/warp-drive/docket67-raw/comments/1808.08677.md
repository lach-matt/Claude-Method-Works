## Summary

The decomposition stands. That includes the trace-anomaly share, the condensate share and the body's quark and glue energies. Our one finding is a **discrepancy between the abstract and the body**. The abstract's quark and glue energy shares (33 % and 37 %) cannot be obtained from the body's central values, which give 32 % and 36 %. The difference is well inside the quoted uncertainties. It is a discrepancy between two printings of one result, not a refutation. No other number is affected.

## Finding

### Abstract against p. 5: the quark and glue energy shares (discrepancy)

- **The paper states:**
  - abstract (p. 1): "the quark energy and glue field energy contribute 33(4)(4)% and 37(5)(4)% respectively in the MS-bar scheme at μ = 2 GeV";
  - body (p. 5): "the quark and glue energy contributions are 32(4)(4)% and 36(5)(4)% respectively".
- **What we find:**
  - Eqs. (1), (2), (5) and (6), with ⟨x⟩_q + ⟨x⟩_g = 1 after the paper's uniform normalisation (p. 3), make the four shares sum to exactly 100 %.
  - From the central momentum fractions on p. 3 (u 0.307, d 0.160, s 0.051, g 0.482; total 1.000) and the condensate share of 9 %:
    - Eq. (5) gives the quark energy (3/4)(⟨x⟩_q − 0.09) = **32.10 %**;
    - Eq. (6) gives the glue energy (3/4)⟨x⟩_g = **36.15 %**;
    - Eq. (2) gives the quarter anomaly (1 − 0.09)/4 = **22.75 %**.
  - These reproduce p. 5 (32, 36, 23).
  - By construction the quark and glue energies sum to (3/4)(1 − 0.09) = 68.25 %.
  - The abstract's 33 % and 37 %, read as rounded values, need a sum of at least 32.5 + 36.5 = **69.0 %**. So the abstract's central values cannot come from the body's central values.
  - The shift of one point in each is far inside the quoted uncertainties, (4)(4) and (5)(4).
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/adjudications/1808.08677.json`, `corrections_to_record` (first item), `decision` (re-runs S2-S6) and `commands_run` (the independent arithmetic H_E 0.321, H_g 0.3615, H_a/4 0.2275, fraction sum 1.000). This supersedes `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard03.json`, key `1808.08677`, check C, which called the difference rounding. The adjudication withdrew that reading.
- **Class:** discrepancy (abstract against body).

## What stands

- **The trace-anomaly share.** 23(1)(1) % under the sum rule, Eq. (2): (1 − 0.09)/4 = 22.75 %.
- **The condensate share.** 9(2)(1) %, imported on p. 5 from the collaboration's earlier arXiv:1511.09089. Its σ_πN + σ_sN = 86.1 MeV is 9.18 % of 938.272 MeV.
- **The body's values.** 32(4)(4) % and 36(5)(4) %, which follow exactly from the p. 3 momentum fractions.
- **The setting.** Every ⟨H⟩ is defined on the nucleon state with degenerate light sea quarks (p. 1, Table I), that is, in the isospin limit, with the error convention (statistical)(systematic) stated on p. 3.
- **Later data agree.** A later, larger dataset from an overlapping group (CLQCD, arXiv:2411.18402v2, Table III) gives 10.05 % and 22.49 %. These are within 0.34 combined σ of 9(2)(1) % and 23(1)(1) %.
