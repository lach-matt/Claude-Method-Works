## Summary

Most of what the paper computes stands, and so does its qualitative point for most of the states it studies. The parts that stand include:

- the definition (3.2), with its normal ordering;
- the general-state formula (2.10) and the middle line of (2.16);
- (3.18) and (3.22);
- the Casimir result (3.45), with its 1/2, its 1/3, and the periodic values 6 and 6/7;
- fluctuations of order unity wherever ρ < 0 for the vacuum + two-particle state, the squeezed vacuum and every zero-mean Gaussian or Casimir state.

Three points are graded **WRONG** after verification:

1. eq. (3.8);
2. the inequality "Δ(x) > 1" stated after (3.8);
3. the universal form of the conclusion ("in all cases studied", "we always have") for the displaced squeezed states of Sec. III.B.

Six further points are discrepancies: misprint-class slips or internal inconsistencies. None of them is a refutation, and none of them moves a result the paper relies on.

## Findings

### 1. Eq. (3.8): WRONG

- **The paper states** (p. 6, Sec. III.A): Δ(x) = (10ε + √2 cos 2θ)/(12ε) for the state (2.15).
- **What we find:**
  - Definition (3.2) makes Δ an absolute value, so Δ ≥ 0. The printed (3.8) is negative at ε = 1/10, cos 2θ = −1: (1 − √2)/1.2 = −0.3452. z3 confirms that such points exist.
  - (3.8) is linear in cos 2θ. Inserting (2.16) into (3.2) gives a quadratic. The exact value, from explicit Fock-space moments of (2.15), is

    Δ = |1 − (2ε − √2 cos 2θ)² / (3(1 + ε²))|.

  - We tried all four combinations of {printed final line of (2.16), exact ρ} with {printed (3.7), exact ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩}. None of them reproduces (3.8).
  - So this is not a one-symbol slip. (3.8) can be rewritten as 1 − [K²(εX)/(1+ε²)²]/(3.7), with X = 2ε − √2 cos 2θ. That is what one would get if the factor εX had been left unsquared in ρ². We offer this only as a possible origin; it is not established.

### 2. The inequality after (3.8): WRONG

- **The paper states** (p. 6): "the condition for the expectation value of energy density to be negative is cos(2θ) > √2ε. In this case we have Δ(x) > 1."
- **What we find:**
  - With Δ as (3.2) defines it, and the exact moments of (2.15), no member of the family has both ρ < 0 and Δ > 1. z3 returns UNSAT, with the vacuity guards SAT for both ρ < 0 and ρ > 0.
  - On ρ < 0 we have 1/3 < Δ < 1, and both bounds are approached.
  - Witness ε = 1/10, θ = 0: ρ/K = −0.2404, exact Δ = 0.5134, while (3.8) gives 2.0118. (Here K = K₀₀ = ω/(2L³), which cancels in Δ.)
  - A sweep over 9,004 points with ρ < 0 finds a largest exact Δ of 0.99999999.
  - The sentence does follow validly from the printed (3.8): given (3.8), ρ < 0 implies Δ > 1 for every real ε ≠ 0 (z3). So the error comes from (3.8), not from a slip in the sentence.
- **Shown by:**

  - `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard00.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-claim-after-3.8`, field `grade_evidence`
- **Class:** WRONG (verified; `VERIFIED-GRADES.tsv` row `gr-qc/9304008-claim-after-3.8`).

### 3. "In all cases studied" / "we always have": WRONG for the displaced squeezed states

- **The paper states:**
  - Abstract, p. 1: "We find in all cases studied that the energy density fluctuations are large whenever the local energy density is negative."
  - Sec. III.B, p. 9: "By the point that the state is sufficiently squeezed to have ρ = ⟨:T₀₀:⟩ < 0, we always have that Δ is at least of order unity."
- **What we find:**
  - Take the squeezed coherent family (3.9), D(α)S(ζ)|0⟩, on the slice of Figs. 1-2 (γ = δ = 0, θ = π/2), along the curve |α|² = (1 − e^{−2r})/8.
  - On that curve, ρ = K sinh r (sinh r − cosh r) = −K(1 − e^{−2r})/2 < 0. Also ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ − ρ² = 0 identically, and ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ > 0. So Δ = 0 exactly.
  - At r = 1, |α| = 0.32876: ρ = −0.4323 K.
    - An independent Fock-space check gives Δ = 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁶ (dimension 220).
    - A second independent check in the adjudication gives Δ = 2 × 10⁻¹⁴ (Gaussian generating function, Fock check at N = 400).
  - The computation uses only the state family (3.9) and the definition (3.2), and none of the paper's printed moment formulas, so it is not a misprint effect.
  - At the same points, the printed (3.18) and (3.19) give Δ = 0.9920, 0.9976 and 0.9999 at r = 0.5, 1 and 2. Fig. 2 might have been computed from (3.19), which would account for "Otherwise, Δ ≈ 1" in its caption. That is a hypothesis; we have not tested it.
- **Proportion, so as not to overstate.** In the slice box r ≤ 2, |α| ≤ 1, among the cells with ρ < 0:
  - Δ < 0.1 in 0.90%;
  - Δ < 1/3 in 3.6%;
  - Δ > 1 in 27.4% (this count includes the cells where ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ < 0, which the normal-ordered square permits).

  The failure is confined to a surface of codimension one and a thin band beside it. What survives is "generically of order unity", not "whenever".
- **The journal version.** This point does not depend on M's comparison: the sentence quoted from the abstract is in the journal's own abstract, which we read on the APS page.

### 4. Eq. (3.7): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 6): ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ = K²·12ε²/(1 + ε²)².
- **What we find:**
  - The exact value is 12K²ε²/(1 + ε²). Printed divided by exact is 1/(1 + ε²): one power of the state normalisation too many.
  - The first and second lines of (3.7) agree with each other, because the six bilinear products sum to 6K². So the extra power sits in the prefactor 2ε²/(1 + ε²)².
  - On its own this could be a misprint, and it is not the basis of the WRONG grade in finding 1. The exponent is read from the text layer only.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-eq3.7-3.8.out`; `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard11.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-eq3.7-3.8`, field `grade_evidence` (i).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 5. Eq. (2.16), final line: discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 4): the final line Kε(2ε − √2 cos 2θ)/(1 + ε²).
- **What we find:**
  - The middle line of (2.16), and the general formula (2.10), give exactly 2Kε(2ε − √2 cos 2θ)/(1 + ε²). This was checked against explicit Fock matrices.
  - The final line is exactly half of it, identically in ε and θ. z3 shows the two lines agree only where ρ = 0.
  - The sign of ρ, and the condition cos 2θ > √2ε, are unaffected.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-eq2.16.py` and `.out` (C1-C7); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard10.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-eq2.16`.
- **Class:** discrepancy (an overall factor of 2 dropped).

### 6. Eqs. (3.19) and (3.23): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (pp. 8-9): ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ for the squeezed coherent state (3.19) and for the squeezed vacuum (3.23).
- **What we find:**
  - The exact squeezed-vacuum result is 6K² sinh²r [cosh²r cos 4θ + 4 sinh r cosh r cos 2θ + 3 sinh²r + 1]. The printed (3.23) differs from it in three coefficients.
  - (3.19) breaks the exact U(1) phase symmetry (θ + φ, γ − φ, δ − 2φ), which the exact moments and the printed (3.18) both keep. At a test point it differs by 21.26.
  - (3.19) at α = δ = 0 does not reduce to (3.23).
  - Four alternative conventions do not rescue either equation: a flipped squeeze sign, a flipped γ sign, a flipped θ sign, and ⟨:T::T:⟩ minus its vacuum value.
  - (3.18) and (3.22) are exact.
  - In the squeezed vacuum, ⟨:T₀₀²:⟩ = 3ρ² for every δ, so Δ = 2/3 there.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-eq3.18-3.23.py` and `.out` (C1-C9; a Weyl-ordering route checked against a 40-digit Fock route); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard01.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-eq3.18-3.23`.
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 7. Eqs. (3.41)-(3.42): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 11): ⟨:φ̇²:⟩²_C = ½(ρ + p₁ + p₂ + p₃)², and likewise for (3.42).
- **What we find:**
  - For the massless, minimally coupled scalar, φ̇² = (ρ + Σp)/2 is an operator identity in either signature. So the coefficient is 1/4.
  - On the periodic image-sum two-point function, rebuilt independently, 1/4 holds and the printed 1/2 fails by exactly a factor 2.
  - The slip does not propagate:
    - The 1/8 in (3.43) forces the coefficient 1/4.
    - The printed 1/2 and 1/3 in (3.45), and the periodic 6 and 6/7, are the values the 1/4 gives.
    - Carrying the printed 1/2 through would instead give a minimum Δ′ of 1 and a periodic Δ′ of 12.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-eq3.41-3.43.py` and `.out` (30 checks); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard02.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-eq3.41-3.43`, `rederivation.outcome` (1)-(6).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 8. Eq. (3.43), leading "ρ": discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 11, as the text layer reads it): Δ′ = (1/8)[(ρ + ξ₁ + ξ₂ + ξ₃)² + …].
- **What we find:**
  - The normalised form needs "1" where "ρ" stands in each bracket.
  - Read literally, the right side at ξ = 0 is ρ²/2, which contradicts the next sentence: "The minimum value of the right hand side of Eq. (3.43) is 1/2."
  - With "1", the identity holds and gives Δ′ = (1 + |ξ|²)/2. (3.45) is unaffected.
  - The page image was not viewed, so this may be a text-layer artefact.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-eq3.41-3.43.out` (the check "(3.43) as printed (leading rho) …"); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard03.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008-eq3.45`, field `hypothesis_drift`; `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard06.json`, key `gr-qc/9304008`, field `grade_evidence` (C6c-e).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 9. The condition cos 2θ > √2ε for ε < 0: minor discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 4): ε is taken "to be real for simplicity"; and (p. 6) ρ < 0 when cos 2θ > √2ε.
- **What we find:**
  - The condition is exact for ε > 0. For ε < 0 it reverses to cos 2θ < √2ε. z3 finds a counter-point with cos 2θ > √2ε and ρ > 0.
  - Replacing ε by −ε is equivalent to shifting θ by π/2, so nothing physical moves.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9304008-claim-after-3.8.out`, check C10.
- **Class:** discrepancy (minor).

## What stands

- **Definitions and exact formulas:**
  - the definition (3.2), with Minkowski normal ordering;
  - (2.10), and the middle line of (2.16);
  - the negativity condition cos 2θ > √2ε (for ε > 0);
  - (3.18) and (3.22);
  - the relation (3.44) between Δ and Δ′ on the Gaussian (Wick) class, where the paper uses it.
- **The Casimir results:** (3.45), Δ′ ≥ 1/2 and Δ ≥ 1/3, re-derived without assuming a diagonal G. This includes the periodic values Δ′ = 6 and Δ = 6/7.
- **The qualitative conclusion where it holds:**
  - vacuum + two-particle state: 1/3 < Δ < 1 wherever ρ < 0;
  - squeezed vacuum: Δ = 2/3;
  - every zero-mean Gaussian state, including the Casimir vacuum: Δ ≥ 1/3.
- **The displaced squeezed states:** the failure is confined as described in finding 3. "Generically of order unity" survives.
- **Sec. V:** the remark that renormalising quartic operator products in curved spacetime "has not yet been developed" was accurate when it was written.
- No measured datum enters these results.
