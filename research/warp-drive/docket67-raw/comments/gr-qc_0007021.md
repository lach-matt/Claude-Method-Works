## Finding

### Eq. (13) read in n dimensions. Class: discrepancy (notational)

**What the paper states.** With A from eq. (11) and G = √(det A) from eq. (12), eq. (13) on p. 7 reads G″/G = −(1/2)[σ_ab σ^ab + R_ab k^a k^b]. The paper states (p. 6): "No assumption will be made about the dimensionality of spacetime, i.e., our results hold in any spacetime dimension."

**What we found.**
- **Read literally, the identity fails.** If A is the full n×n matrix, eq. (13) is not an identity:
  - det A = λ² det A_⊥;
  - in flat space G″ = 2, against 0 on the right-hand side.
- **The coefficient is four-dimensional.** If G = (det A_⊥)^{1/2}, the coefficient 1/2 holds only for n = 4. In n dimensions a residual (n − 4)(n − 2)D′²/(4D²) remains.
- **The correct object.** The object that works in n dimensions is D = (det A_⊥)^{1/(n−2)}, with coefficient 1/(n − 2). Then D″/D = −(σ² + R_ab k^a k^b)/(n − 2).
- **Lemma 1 is unaffected.** The proof of Lemma 1 goes through unchanged with that object.

We record this as notation, not as an error in any result.
