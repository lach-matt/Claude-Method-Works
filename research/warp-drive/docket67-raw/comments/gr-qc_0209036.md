## Findings

### 1. The prefactor in (II.22) and (II.24): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 6):
  - ρ₁(f) and ρ₂(f) are defined in (II.20)-(II.21) with the prefactor 2N²_α;
  - the evaluated forms (II.22) and (II.24) carry N²_α/(2π)⁴.
- **What we find:**
  - Symbolically, (II.20)-(II.21) imply the prefactor N²/(8π⁴) = 2N²/(2π)⁴, against the printed N²/(16π⁴). The ratio is 2.
  - Numerically, at α = 10⁻³, (II.22) as printed gives ρ₂ = −5.110921 × 10⁻⁵, while the position-space evaluation from (II.18)/(II.29) gives −1.022184 × 10⁻⁴. The ratio is 2.000000.
  - The factor multiplies both terms, so the signs and the α-exponents, which carry Theorem II.1, are untouched.
  - (II.22) and (II.24) are not among the equations the Erratum names.

- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 2. The order swap (II.16) → (II.17), and the factor 24 in (II.27), (II.29) and (II.31): already corrected by the authors

- **The paper states** (pp. 5-7): (II.17) with the weight u₁^{2ν+1}, and (II.27) C = 1/(24π²Λ₀⁴).
- **What we find:**
  - The exact exchange of the order of integration in (II.16) gives the weight (Λ₀^{2ν+1} − u₁^{2ν+1}). Then (II.16) gives C = 1/(8π²Λ₀⁴), not 1/(24π²Λ₀⁴): a factor 3 that carries into (II.29) and (II.31).
  - The corrected kernel keeps the properties (i)-(iv) the proof uses. It moves only ρ₁, which goes to zero anyway.
  - The authors' 2009 Erratum names the same equation and the same replacement ("the factor of 24 should be replaced by a factor of 8"). Its corrected display of (2.17) did not render in the page we read, so we have not compared the corrected kernel itself with the weight above; the agreement is in the equation named and in the factor. We record only that arXiv v2 does not carry the correction.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_0209036.out` (check C.II27); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard11.json`, key `gr-qc/0209036`, `rederivation.outcome` (b).
- **Class:** discrepancy in arXiv v2, already corrected by the authors.

### 3. "Variance τ₀": wording

- **The paper states** (p. 12, at (III.12)): "g(τ)² is a normalised Gaussian with mean zero and variance τ₀ > 0".
- **What we find:** g(τ)² = (2πτ₀²)^{−1/2} e^{−τ²/(2τ₀²)} has variance τ₀², so τ₀ is the standard deviation. (III.13) is correct as printed.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_0209036.out` (check F.var).
- **Class:** discrepancy (wording only).
