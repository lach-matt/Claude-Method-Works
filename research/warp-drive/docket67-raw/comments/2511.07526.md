## Summary

The paper's conclusion stands: under the current best-fit cosmological model the NEC, applied to the total fluid, is not violated, past or future. Our one finding is a **sign discrepancy** between the Fig. 3 caption and the text. It is not a refutation.

## Finding

### The sign of w_a in the Fig. 3 caption: discrepancy

- **The paper states:**
  - Fig. 3 caption (p. 4): "the best fit to current data at w₀ ≈ −0.7, **w_a ≈ 1**, and hence w_tot ≈ −0.53";
  - Sec. V text (p. 4): "the current best fit about w₀ ≈ −0.7, **w_a ≈ −1**".
- **What we find:**
  - The two signs disagree.
  - Only the text's value matches the paper's own account of a past phantom phase. With w_de(z) = w₀ + w_a z/(1 + z), as defined on p. 4, the early-time limit is w₀ + w_a. That is −1.7 for w_a = −1, which is phantom and so crosses −1 as Sec. I describes. It is +0.3 for w_a = +1, which never goes below −1.
  - So the caption's "w_a ≈ 1" appears to be the misprint. We did this check in this drafting stage, from the paper's own definitions.
