## Findings

### 1. Case 2's throat radius does not reproduce from the paper's own equations. Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.** Case 2 (μ_0 = m_P) gives r_t = 0.4473670842 l_P.

**What we found.**
- Recomputing from the paper's eqs. (57) and (61)-(65) together with (B7) gives r_t = 0.6968196708 l_P for case 2.
- Case 1's r_t, 1.158822606 l_P, is unchanged by the same recomputation. Its μ_0 is not: the recomputation gives 0.6013169464 m_P where the paper prints 0.3860531213 m_P, and case 2's √G_0 moves in the same way.
- The printed b equals B_ln + (4 ln 2 − 1)·S_A to a relative 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰. In words, the constant 2 ln 2 − 1/2 appears to be counted twice.
- For comparison, the author's 2007 formulas at ω = 1 give 0.4927259145 l_P for case 2.

All three values are O(l_P), and none of them changes the sign of m.

### 2. The minimum in eq. (39) and the printed coefficients a, b, c. Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.** Eq. (39) prints a factor 1/4 for the minimum of eq. (38). The printed coefficients (p. 8, with eq. (66)) are a = 23.35740218, b = 276.6026775 and c = 212.0575042.

**What we found.**
- The true minimum of eq. (38) is w/2. The author's 1301.0221 (eq. 4) prints 1/2.
- An independent WKB count using w/2 gives c = 212.0575041, which reproduces the printed c to a relative 3.9 × 10⁻¹⁰.
- The same count gives a = 106.03 and b = 182.63, which do not match the printed 23.357 and 276.603 (rounded).
- Taken with the prefactors as printed (1/4π² in (61), 1/4 in (39)), eqs. (63)-(66) would give c = 16.875 and a case-1 r_t = 0.3269 l_P. So the printed c and the printed prefactors differ by a factor of 4π.
