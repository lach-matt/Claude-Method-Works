## Summary

In the parts read, the review's account of the quantum energy inequalities agrees with the sources we checked, and so does its restatement of the Fewster-Roman results and their hypotheses. Our one finding is a **typographical discrepancy** in eq. (102), which restates Fewster and Roman's timelike quantum null energy inequality. The factor (γ̇^a k_a) appears unsquared, where homogeneity in the null vector k requires its square; the audit records this as a discrepancy of the review, not of Fewster and Roman. The verification pass also noticed that the power of t₀ in the same equation does not match the scaling that follows from the review's own definition (62), as used in (63). Both points look like one typographical slip, and neither is a refutation.

## Finding

### Eq. (102), p. 34: the power of (γ̇^a k_a), and of t₀ (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 34): "The starting point is the QNEI for flat spacetimes derived by Fewster and Roman [101] … ∫dt ⟨:T_ab: k^a k^b⟩_ω f²_{t₀} ≥ −((γ̇^a k_a)/(12π² t₀²)) C (102)".
- **What we find:**
  - The left-hand side is quadratic in the null vector k. Rescaling k → λk (λ > 0) multiplies it by λ².
  - The printed right-hand side scales as λ. So the inequality as printed is not invariant under the rescaling of k, whereas any correct form must be. The factor must be (γ̇^a k_a)².
  - The same author's later review prints it squared. Kontou, arXiv:2405.05963v2, restates the Fewster-Roman bound as eq. (46), p. 10, ∫dt f(t)² ⟨T_μν ℓ^μ ℓ^ν⟩ ≥ −((ℓ^μ t_μ)²/(12π²)) ∫dt (f″(t))², and uses it as eq. (72), p. 19, E ≥ −(ℓ^μ U_μ)²/(12π²) ∫(f″)²/∫f². The squared factor appears again in eq. (74).
  - The audit, which read Fewster and Roman's v2 in full, records the discrepancy as the review's own, not theirs.
- **The power of t₀ (noted in the verification re-read, not in the audit record).** With f_{t₀}(t) = f(t/t₀)/√t₀ as defined in (62), the Fewster-Roman right-hand side ∫dt (f_{t₀}″)² equals t₀⁻⁴ ∫ds f″(s)². The review's own QWEI (63) carries the same t₀⁻⁴. As printed, (102) has t₀² in the denominator and C = ∫dt f″(t/t₀)², which equals t₀ ∫ds f″(s)², so its right-hand side scales as t₀⁻¹; reading C as ∫ds f″(s)² still leaves t₀⁻². Either way the printed power differs from t₀⁻⁴, while the left-hand side does not depend on t₀ for a constant null energy density. This looks like part of the same typographical slip.

- **Class:** discrepancy (typographical).

### What we do not claim

- Eq. (102) is the starting point the review gives for the Fewster-Olum-Pfenning proof of the ANEC in flat spacetime with boundaries (pp. 34-35). The review describes that argument in words only, and the derivation itself is in the cited paper [16].
- We make no claim about that argument. The finding concerns only the printed form of eq. (102).

## What stands

- **The restatement of Fewster and Roman** (p. 25): no QEI along null geodesics for the massless minimally coupled scalar field in four-dimensional Minkowski space, with the hypotheses stated. Also its remark on the general-QFT route: "no such rigorous result exists to this day."
- **Table 3** (p. 25). The status of QEIs by field, including that the Klein-Gordon field with ξ ≠ 0 has no state-independent QWEI, QNEI or QSEI, though state-dependent ones exist.
- **p. 24.** The hedged statement that "in general dimensions there is usually no QEI for energy densities which are only averaged in a spacelike direction."
- **Sec. 4.3** (pp. 34-35), the derivation of averaged energy conditions from QEIs, as read. Apart from eq. (102) we found nothing there to remark on. Sec. 4.1 was not read.
