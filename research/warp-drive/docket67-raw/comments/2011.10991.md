## Finding

### Eq. (33): the form of the divergence (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (Sec. 6, p. 10, eq. (33)): the conservation law enters as ∫_V d³x √−g [ (1/√−g) ∂_μ T^μ_(C)z − ½ ∂_z(g_tt) T^tt_(C) ] = 0.
- **What we find:**
  - The covariant divergence is (1/√−g) ∂_μ(√−g T^μ_z), not (1/√−g) ∂_μ T^μ_z.
  - The two differ by ∂_j((√−g − 1) T^j_z). That is a spatial total derivative, and it integrates to a surface term on ∂V, where T^μν_(C) = 0.
  - So the step from (33) to (35) and (36) is unaffected. We re-derived the covariant divergence exactly in metric (30). Conservation together with the premise (32) gives ∫√−g T^i_i = 2γ∫√−g z T^tt exactly, so (36) holds at the order to which the paper works.
