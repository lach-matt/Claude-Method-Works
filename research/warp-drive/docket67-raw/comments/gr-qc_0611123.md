## What stands

- **Eq. (10):** Θ = 3ȧ/a = (1/√(a⁶)) d(√(a⁶))/dt for u = ∂_t in FLRW is correct, with shear and rotation identically zero.
- **Independent checks:** our checks reproduce Θ = 3H for k = +1, 0 and −1, and Θ = d ln √h/dt.

## Finding

### The sentence after eq. (10) (p. 9). Class: discrepancy (wording)

**What the paper states.** "Note that a⁶ is the volume of the expanding 3–space."

**What we found.** The volume element of the comoving 3-space scales as √(a⁶) = a³, which is the quantity eq. (10) itself uses; a⁶ is the time-dependent factor of the determinant of the spatial metric (the whole determinant for k = 0 in Cartesian coordinates). The formula is correct, and the sentence names the wrong one of the two.

**What shows it.**
- `rederive/expansion-scalar-3h-flrw.py`, section D. Re-run 2026-10-04 (all checks pass), it prints "(1/sqrt h) d sqrt(h)/dt = 3H (volume of a fluid element ~ a^3)".
- `shards/shard01.json`, key `expansion-scalar-3h-flrw`, later_literature.
