## Summary

The kinematic identity we relied on, Θ = 3H for the fundamental observers of an FLRW model, stands. Our one finding is a **numerical discrepancy** in an illustrative sentence on p. 23. It is not a refutation. We ask the authors to check it against the typeset page.

## Finding

### The Hubble time for H₀ ≈ 50 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹: discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 23, Sec. 4.2.2, as the text layer reads it): "a decrease in the estimate of the Hubble constant to about H₀ ≈ 50 km/sec/Mpc. This corresponds to a Hubble time 1/H₀ of about **1.8 × 10⁹ years**".
- **What we find:**
  - 1/(50 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹) = (3.0857 × 10¹⁹ km)/(50 km s⁻¹) = 6.17 × 10¹⁷ s = **19.6 Gyr**, i.e. 1.96 × 10¹⁰ years. The figure as the text layer gives it is an order of magnitude smaller, and its mantissa (1.8) also differs from 1.96, so a superscript lost in extraction would not by itself account for it.
  - The same sentence prints the globular-cluster age as "about 1.2 × 10⁹ years", with the same exponent. So the comparison the sentence draws (an age below the Hubble time, "implying there is no problem") holds whichever exponent is intended.
  - The text layer was re-read at source in the drafting stage and again in the verification stage (2026-10-04, alphaXiv `answer_pdf_queries`, p. 23), and reads the same each time. What the typeset page shows cannot be settled from the text layer alone.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard01.json`, key `expansion-scalar-3h-flrw`, fields `lacked_data` and `verifiers` (the data-lens note on the mantissa); the arithmetic above; re-read at source in the drafting and verification stages.
- **Class:** discrepancy (to be confirmed on the typeset page).

## What stands

- **Θ = 3H.** Eqs. (11), (25) and (104): the expansion scalar of the fundamental-observer congruence of an FLRW model is three times the Hubble rate. We re-derived it for all three values of k and in four coordinate systems.
- **The qualitative reasoning of p. 23.** An age below the Hubble time poses no problem, and a positive cosmological constant eases the age constraint. Both are unaffected.
