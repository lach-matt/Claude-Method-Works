## Summary

All four of the Letter's results stand under the hypotheses as published:

1. two throats, which coalesce in the static case;
2. the NEC is violated or marginal on each throat;
3. the NEC is violated on an open region whose closure meets the throat;
4. the transverse-averaged NEC is violated on an open interval.

We found no counterexample. Our two findings concern the Letter's **sketch** of the strict-inequality step, and both are **discrepancies**. Neither is a refutation, and the companion paper's own treatment does not share the first.

## Findings

### 1. The step (10) → (11) omits a θ² term (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 3): from the strict second-variation inequality (10), "In terms of the expansion this implies the existence of an open interval in ε … for which ∫ d²x √γ f² dθ/du > 0 (11)."
- **What we find:**
  - The second variation of the area is d²/dε² √γ(x, εf) = f²(θ′ + θ²)√γ.
  - Off the throat, θ ≠ 0, so the θ² term is present, and (10) alone does not give (11).
  - The companion paper's eq. 33 keeps θ² and drops it only on the throat.
  - We built a two-generator toy in which A″ > 0 while ∫ f²√γ θ′ < 0 at the same ε. The toy violates the Letter's eq. (3), the per-generator area minimum, so it is **not** a counterexample to the theorem. It shows that eq. (3), and not (9)-(10) alone, has to carry the step.

- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 2. The parenthetical "(in general (−δ₂, 0) ∪ (0, +δ₂))" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 3, after (9)): "there is an open interval in ε (in general (−δ₂, 0) ∪ (0, +δ₂), different from that above) for which d²A/dε² > 0."
- **What we find:**
  - Consider the C^∞ function A(ε) = e^{−1/|ε|}(2 + sin(1/ε²)). It satisfies A(ε) > A(0) for every ε ≠ 0, yet A″ takes both signs in every interval (0, δ).
  - So the open set {A″ > 0} accumulates at 0, which is all the argument needs, but it need not be a punctured interval.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/gr-qc_9802048.py` (check C5).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

## What stands

- **Results (1)-(2):** re-derived symbolically in the general spherical double-null metric. On θ₊ = 0, dθ₊/dξ₊ = −G₊₊. A z3 check confirms that θ = 0, ω = 0, σ² ≥ 0 and θ′ ≥ 0 imply R_{ℓℓ} ≤ 0.
- **Result (4):** checked by machine in the companion paper's eq. 53 form.
- **The two throats:** distinct in an expanding example, and coalescing when static.
- **Methodology:** the Letter's point that, for a dynamic wormhole, spatial flare-out does not imply null flare-out.
- No counterexample to results (1)-(4) under the published hypotheses was found.
