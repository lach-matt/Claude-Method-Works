## What stands

The arithmetic below reproduces from the paper's printed inputs. It is a check of the calculation, not of the detections themselves.

- **The extrapolation to large bodies:** the size-law step reproduces. ∫D³ D^−3.5 dD = 2√D_max − 2√D_min, so m ∝ D_max^0.5. The factor is 2236, against the 2200 printed.
- **The dust-mass prefactor:** S d²/(κ B_RJ) gives a prefactor of 0.4905, against 0.5 printed.
- **The 1-4 au belt:**
  - T_d = 54.7, 38.7 and 27.4 K at 1, 2 and 4 au.
  - m_dust = 4.23 × 10⁻⁶ M_⊕ (printed 4 × 10⁻⁶).
  - m_tot = 9.45 × 10⁻³ M_⊕ (printed ~10⁻²).
- **The 30 au belt:** 1.44 × 10⁻⁴ and 0.32 M_⊕ (printed 1.4 × 10⁻⁴ and 0.33).
- **A later datum:** MacGregor et al. (2018) later read the ACA excess as a stellar flare. That is a later datum, not a fault of this paper, and we do not raise it here.

## Finding

### The warm-dust mass at ~0.4 au (Sec. 3.2, p. 5). Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.** For the warm component, the printed inputs are a flux density of "∼30 μJy" and a characteristic temperature "T_d ≃ 90 K". The printed dust mass is "∼ 5.5 × 10⁻⁷ M_⊕", with "a total mass of ∼ 10⁻³ M_⊕".

**What we found.**
- The same relation that reproduces the paper's other masses gives m_dust = 2.82 × 10⁻⁷ M_⊕ from 30 μJy at 90 K, a factor of 0.51 of the printed value. The companion total follows suit: 6.30 × 10⁻⁴ M_⊕.
- The printed 5.5 × 10⁻⁷ M_⊕ would need a flux of about 59 μJy at 90 K.
- The paper's figures carry "∼", but a factor of two is well outside the agreement the same relation gives for the paper's other masses, which match their printed values to within 6%.
- We cannot tell from v1 whether the flux, the temperature or the mass is the transcribed figure.
- We did not assess whether anything else in the paper depends on this number.

**What shows it.**
- `rederive/1711.00578.py`. Re-run 2026-10-04 (all checks pass), it prints the note "S=30 uJy, T=90 K gives m_dust = 2.82e-07 M_E, m_tot = 6.30e-04 M_E; paper prints 5.5e-7 and ~1e-3 (ratio recomputed/printed = 0.51)" and "flux that would give the printed 5.5e-7 at 90 K: 59 uJy".
