## Summary

Everything we checked in Sec. 2 stands:

- the exponent and its origin in the instanton action;
- the statement that the Higgs phase only strengthens the suppression;
- the normalisation of g;

Our one finding is a **discrepancy** inside eq. (2.8): its two numbers do not match each other. It is not a refutation, and it leaves the order of magnitude of the suppression, and the conclusion drawn from it, unchanged.

## Finding

### Eq. (2.8): the printed 10⁻¹⁷⁰ does not follow from the printed α_W = 1/29 (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (printed p. 6, PDF p. 7): "Γ_inst ∝ exp(−4π/α_W) ~ 10⁻¹⁷⁰, where α_W = g²/4π = α/sin²θ_W = 1/29."
- **What we find:**
  - exp(−4π·29) = 10^−158.27. That is 11.73 decades above the printed 10⁻¹⁷⁰.
  - 10⁻¹⁷⁰ corresponds to 1/α_W = 170 ln 10/(4π) = 31.15.
  - A possible origin, offered as our inference only: 31.15 = 137.036 × 0.2273 is α(0)/sin²θ_W with sin²θ_W ≈ 0.227, while 1/29 is close to the value an α at the electroweak scale gives. The two numbers in (2.8) would then fit two different conventions for α. The scheme values behind this inference were recalled, not read from a source, and nothing here is a reading of the authors' intent.
  - For scale, the exponent moves 5.46 decades per unit change in 1/α_W.

## What stands

- **The exponent.** exp(−4π/α_W) = exp(−2S_inst), with S_inst = 8π²/g². Eq. (2.7) gives the amplitude as e^{−S_inst}, and (2.8) is the probability.
  - We checked this from the BPST solution (`rederive/thooft-1976.py`, 23 checks pass): self-duality, S = 8π²/g² independent of ρ, and Q = 1.
- **The Higgs phase.** Eq. (2.9), and the statement "Clearly, the tunnelling rate is still suppressed by the exponential factor (2.8)", hold.
  - The Bogomolny bound gives S_E ≥ 8π²/g² for every Q = 1 path in the gauge-Higgs theory, and the Higgs terms are non-negative. So the Higgs can only deepen the suppression.
- **The coupling.** m_W = gv/2, so the g in S_inst is the g of the W mass.
- **The sphaleron estimate (2.11).** The range "from 1.56 to 2.72" attributed to Klinkhamer and Manton reproduces their variational trial values, 1.5662 and 2.7220, so the citation is faithful. The barrier is "of order 10 TeV".
  - See `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/reaudit/klinkhamer-manton-1984.json`.
- **The size of the suppression.** Every convention for α_W we computed leaves it between 10^−158.27 and 10^−173.16 (the scheme inputs other than 1/29 were recalled, not read), so the conclusion drawn from (2.8) does not depend on which convention is used.
