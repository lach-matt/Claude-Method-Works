## Finding

### The instanton suppression factor (p. 1). Class: discrepancy (misprint)

**What the paper states.** "It is shown by 't Hooft that the (B + L)-changing vacuum transition is suppressed by e^{−S_instanton} ≃ 10⁻¹⁶²."

**What we found.**
- With S_instanton = 8π²/g² = 2π/α_W, we get e^{−S} = 10⁻⁸⁰·⁴⁷ and e^{−2S} = 10⁻¹⁶⁰·⁹⁵, both at 1/α_W = 29.49 (from G_F and m_W).
- At α_W ≈ 1/30, e^{−2S} is about 10⁻¹⁶⁴, while e^{−S} stays near 10⁻⁸².
- So the printed 10⁻¹⁶² is the order of e^{−2S}, the suppression of the transition probability. The exponent as printed appears to be missing the factor 2.
- The sentence is introductory. None of the paper's results that we re-derived (eqs. 8-10 and the p. 4 suppression factors) uses it.

**What shows it.**
- `rederive/sphaleron-instanton-and-collider-dispute.py`, with its output `rederive/sphaleron-instanton-and-collider-dispute.out`, line 13. The adjacent lines give the symbolic check exp(−2S_inst) = exp(−4π/α_W).
