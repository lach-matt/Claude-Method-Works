## What stands

- **The continuum result:** T_c = 159.6 ± 0.1 ± 1.5 GeV, the susceptibility-maximum pseudocritical temperature (p. 2, Sec. VII), stands. So does eq. (16), T_c = 159.58 ± 0.06 GeV.
- **The systematic:** the 1.5 GeV is described as a conservative effective-theory uncertainty, consistent with the ~1% matching accuracy (p. 2): 1% of 159.6 GeV is 1.6 GeV.
- **Width:** the cross-over region is 157-162 GeV.
- **The U(1) shift:** the estimate that the U(1) field shifts T_c by ≲ 0.5 GeV (p. 9) stands.
- **Comparison with ref. [20]:** the agreement with ref. [20] within errors stands, and so does the paper's own caution on p. 9 that the agreement is "partly coincidental".

Nothing below moves any of these.

## Findings

### 1. Abstract against body. Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.**
- The abstract (p. 1) gives "T_c = 159.5 ± 1.5 GeV".
- The body gives the continuum value T_c = 159.58 ± 0.06 GeV (eq. 16, p. 5), which rounds to the 159.6 printed on p. 2 and in Sec. VII.

**What we found.**
- The abstract's central value differs from the body's by 0.08 GeV. That is far inside the quoted ±1.5 GeV, but it is a difference within the source.
- The abstract's figure is the value the paper attributes to ref. [20] (pp. 1 and 9).

### 2. The value attributed to ref. [20]. Class: discrepancy (citation)

**What the paper states.**
- p. 1: in ref. [20] (arXiv:1404.3565, PRL 113, 141602), "The cross-over temperature was reported to be T_c = 159.5 ± 1.5 GeV".
- p. 9: "In ref. [20] the central value of the cross-over temperature was reported to be 159.5 GeV".

**What we found.** arXiv:1404.3565v1 prints T_c = (159 ± 1) GeV in three places:
- its abstract (p. 1);
- its body (p. 3): "the pseudocritical temperature can be estimated to be T_c = 159 ± 1 GeV";
- its conclusions (p. 4).

We could not read the PRL version. Whether the published version carries 159.5 ± 1.5 GeV is therefore open, and this finding concerns only the arXiv v1 text of each paper.
