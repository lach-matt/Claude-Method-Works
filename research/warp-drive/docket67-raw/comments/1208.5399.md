## Finding

### Section 1.3, the Dirichlet-scalar Casimir constant (p. 13). Class: discrepancy (misprint)

**What the paper states.** "The known value of the Casimir energy density for the massless minimally coupled scalar field is T_00 = −π²/(1140L⁴) − (π²/48L⁴)(3 − 2cos²(πz/L))/cos⁴(πz/L)."

**What we found.**
- The constant term comes out as π²/(1440L⁴) by two independent routes: the zeta-regularised mode sum, and the periodic image sum.
- With 1440 the midpoint ratio is 6.703% rather than 6.760%, and the range is [3.196%, 6.703%]. The stated "3-7%" is unchanged.
- We read 1140 as a typographical slip for 1440. In the pages we read, nothing else rests on the constant beyond the "3-7%" range, which it does not change.
