## What stands

- **The forward direction:** the paper's use of the forward direction stands. An achronal null geodesic (a null line) has no conjugate points (Hawking-Ellis Prop. 4.5.12). Equivalently, a null geodesic with a conjugate pair can be deformed to a timelike curve (the paper's Proposition 1, p. 4, which it takes from Wald's Theorem 9.3.8).
- **The rest of the paper:** we did not examine the continuity lemmas (Lemmas 1-3) or the proof of the "Galloway statement" beyond the single step below.

## Finding

### The converse, stated as a general fact (p. 4 and p. 11). Class: discrepancy

**What the paper states.**
- p. 4: "An inextendible causal curve that has no conjugate points has achronal image. These are called null lines in the literature [63]."
- p. 11: "one may find eventually an inextensible null geodesic … that has no conjugate points. If this geodesic appears, proposition 1 shows that it can not be deformed to a time like one. Its image is achronal."

**What we found.**
- This is the converse of Hawking-Ellis Prop. 4.5.12, and it does not hold in general.
- Counterexample: in flat spacetime with one spatial direction identified (x ~ x + 2π), a null geodesic that winds around the identified direction has no conjugate points, since the Riemann tensor vanishes and J = λ. But it is not achronal: the point reached after one full winding, γ(2π + ε), lies in the chronological future of the starting point (dt − d = 2π > 0).
- Proposition 1 rules out deforming the geodesic, through a one-parameter family with fixed endpoints, into a timelike curve. It does not rule out a different timelike curve joining the same two points, which is what achronality requires.

Whether the argument on p. 11 needs this converse, or only a weaker property that would hold under further assumptions (for example, a causality condition), we have not assessed. We record the sentence, not the theorem.

**What shows it.**
- `rederive/conjugate-point-not-achronal.py`, check R5. Re-run 2026-10-04 (R5 PASS), it prints "flat cylinder: no conjugate point on gamma (J = lambda), yet dt - d = 2*pi > 0 at gamma(2pi+eps): NON-achronal WITHOUT a conjugate point. The converse of the result is false".
