## Summary

Theorem 5.1 and Corollary 5.2 stand. Our one finding is a power of a in the c = 0 specialisation of Y₂: the printed a² is inconsistent with the general formula (94), which gives a⁴. It is either a misprint or an artefact of extraction. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation, and nothing else in the paper depends on it as far as we read.

## Finding

### Y₂ at c = 0: the power of a (discrepancy)

- **The paper states:**
  - Eq. (94), p. 14: Y₂ := (1/(64π²a⁴))(−16X₂ + 2c² + c² log(½a²)) + (1/(2κ′))(R − 2Λ′) − c′R², with R = 6/a².
  - In the c = 0 specialisation (p. 15): Σ aₙ(n + 1)²/a² = Y₂ = −1/(480π²a²) + (1/(2κ′))(R − 2Λ′) − c′R².
- **What we find:**
  - **Dimension.** With ħ = c = 1, κ′ has dimension length², so (1/(2κ′))(R − 2Λ′) and c′R² both scale as length⁻⁴. Under a → λa, κ′ → λ²κ′, Λ′ → λ⁻²Λ′, m → λ⁻¹m, every other term of Y₂ scales as λ⁻⁴. The printed −1/(480π²a²) scales as λ⁻²; −1/(480π²a⁴) scales as λ⁻⁴.
  - **Agreement with (94).** At c = 0 the first term of (94) is −16X₂/(64π²a⁴) = −X₂/(4π²a⁴). It equals −1/(480π²a⁴) when X₂ = 1/120 (= ζ(−3)). Matching the printed a⁻² form instead would need X₂ to carry a factor a².
  - **Eq. (133)** (p. 29) gives X₂ = 4π²a⁴S₂ + (1 + 10c − 15c²)/120. At c = 0 its constant part is 1/120, which is the value the a⁻⁴ reading requires. The definition of S₂ (p. 27) was not read, so this is partial support only. The dimensional point does not depend on it.
  - The raw extraction shows the superscript 2, so the a² is in the text layer as read. Whether it is a misprint or an extraction artefact, we cannot tell from the text layer.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/2007.14311.py`, check C6 (23 of 23 checks pass in the audit run; C6 solves for the X₂ each reading requires, while its dimension table is declared rather than computed); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard10.json`, key `2007.14311`: field `rederivation.outcome` (C6: "dimensionally inconsistent with (94), where a^4 is required") and field `verifiers` (the refute verifier's reading of (131) and (133) at source, and the rederive verifier's scaling check V9, which computes the dimensions).
- **Class:** discrepancy (a misprint or an extraction artefact).

## What stands

- **Theorem 5.1** and its proof, as read (the pages of the proof that were read; eqs. (86)-(87) on pp. 5-13 were reconstructed, not read, in the check of the spectrum below).
- **Corollary 5.2(3).** Its necessity half was checked with z3 for truncations to N = 2, 4 and 8 terms, with equality only in the ground state. That is a finite check, consistent with the paper's proof, not a replacement for it.
- **The Einstein static universe curvature.** R = 6/a², G₀₀ = R/2, and the left-hand sides of (91) and (92) recompute exactly. c > −1 is equivalent to m² + ξR > 0.
- **The spectrum.** On S³, lₙ² = (n + 1)² + c, re-derived from the Gegenbauer eigenvalues n(n + 2). The paper's own eqs. (86)-(87) were not read, and this form agrees with every equation that was.
- **The ESU Misner-Sharp mass.** It is (a/2) sin³χ ≥ 0.
