## Summary

Eq. (10) stands, and so does the exponential decay of linear static solutions outside a constant-density source. Our one finding is a sentence in footnote 2 about how the nucleon's mass couples to the Higgs field. It differs from the standard heavy-quark (Shifman-Vainshtein-Zakharov) result. The paper sets the coefficient concerned aside, so none of its results depend on the sentence. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Footnote 2, p. 6: "the gluon part which is not coupled to the Higgs field" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (footnote 2):
  - "The mass of neutrons and protons comprises mostly the gluon part which is not coupled to the Higgs field and a small fraction due to the valence quarks. This introduces a proportionality coefficient α which can be extracted from [27, 43-45]. Hence the Higgs field should couple to αρ in (10). We ignore this α coefficient in what follows."
- **What we find:**
  - At tree level the Higgs has no direct coupling to gluons. Through heavy-quark loops, however, it couples to the gluonic part of the nucleon mass.
  - In the restatement by Cline, Kainulainen, Scott and Weniger (arXiv:1306.4710v5, Sec. 5.1, eqs. (11)-(13)), the heavy-quark contributions are Σ_{c,b,t} f_q = (2/9)(1 − Σ_{u,d,s} f_q). That term comes from the gluonic matrix element (their eqs. (12)-(13)). With f_N = 0.30 ± 0.01 (their p. 8), eq. (11) implies a light-quark share of about 0.10 and a heavy-quark (gluonic) share of about 0.20, two thirds of the coupling.
  - So "not coupled" holds for the direct tree-level coupling only. Read as a statement that the gluonic mass does not couple, it would make α of the order of the light-quark share, about a third of f_N.
  - We have not read refs. [27, 43-45]. We make no claim about the value of α they would give.
  - The paper ignores α ("We ignore this α coefficient in what follows"). Nothing in its results depends on the sentence.
- **Shown by:** reading footnote 2 and Cline et al. eqs. (11)-(13) and p. 8 in the on-disk captures; `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard02.json`, key `higgs-yukawa-static-sourcing`, fields `hypothesis_drift` (the coupling-hypothesis item) and `later_literature` (the Brax & Burrage row).
- **Class:** discrepancy (in the wording of a footnote).

## What stands

- **Eq. (10).** It is the static field equation for the Higgs perturbation sourced by matter. Linearised, it is (∇² − m_h²)δφ = (m_ψ/v)n, which we re-derived with zero residual.
- **The exterior solution.** Linear static solutions decay exponentially outside a constant-density source (pp. 13-15, eqs. (54)-(58)).
