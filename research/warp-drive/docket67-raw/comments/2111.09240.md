## Summary

The physics of Sec. 6 stands: eq. (48), the value ≈ −0.22 and Fig. 1, and eq. (49). Our one finding is a **misprinted fraction**: the paper prints −7/16 beside the decimal −0.22, but its own eq. (48) gives −7/32. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### The fraction printed after eq. (48): discrepancy

- **The paper states** (p. 9): after (48), "For the case corresponding to Fig. 1, this gives us −7/16 ≈ −0.22."
  - The Fig. 1 caption fixes the case: E₀ = m₀c² and m₂/m₀ = 0.75.
- **What we find:**
  - Eq. (48) reads E₂ − E₀ = [(m₂² − m₀²)c²/(2m₀)]·[E₀/(m₀c²) − √((E₀/(m₀c²))² − 1)]. At E₀ = m₀c² the second bracket is 1 − 0 = 1, so (E₂ − E₀)/E₀ = [(m₂/m₀)² − 1]/2 = (0.5625 − 1)/2 = **−7/32 = −0.21875**.
  - That agrees with the paper's own "≈ −0.22" and with the large-r value in Fig. 1. It does not agree with −7/16 = −0.4375.
- **Shown by:**
  - `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard04.json`, key `photon-rocket-optimality-floor`, `rederivation.outcome` (C9: "P-Z eq (48) evaluates to −7/32 = −0.21875, which matches their '~ −0.22' and Fig. 1 but not their printed '−7/16'").
  - The repository copy of `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/photon-rocket-optimality-floor.py` predates the C9 check, so the one-line arithmetic is given above in full.
- **Class:** discrepancy (a misprint).

## What stands

- **Eq. (48)** itself, and the value ≈ −0.22 with Fig. 1.
- **Eq. (49).** A flat-space photon ejection from rest turns exactly the fraction Δm/(2m₀) of the rest energy spent into kinetic energy. Four-momentum conservation shows it in one line (c = 1): the photon carries E_γ = (m₀² − m₂²)/(2m₀), the rocket's kinetic energy is m₀ − E_γ − m₂, and dividing by Δm = m₀ − m₂ gives 1 − (m₀ + m₂)/(2m₀) = Δm/(2m₀) exactly. The audit's check of the same identity is recorded at `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard04.json`, key `photon-rocket-optimality-floor`, `rederivation.outcome` (C9).
- **The flat-space minimum (Sec. 7).** At large distance η takes its minimum value Δm/(2m₀), as read from (55).
