## Summary

The review's statements on vacuum decay that we read all stand. We have one finding, and it is typographical.

## Finding

### Printed p. 3: "any destroying" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (printed p. 3): "The bubble then starts to expand, reaching the speed of light very quickly, any destroying everything in its way."
- **What we find:** "any" reads as a typographical slip for "and". The same statement elsewhere in the review reads "it expands at the speed of light, destroying everything in its way" (printed p. 54) and "expands at the speed of light and destroys everything in its way" (printed p. 38, Sec. 5.1).
- **Shown by:** reading p. 3; `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/adjudications/1809.06923.json`, `corrections_to_record` ("Source misprint, printed p.3").
- **Class:** discrepancy (typographical).

## What stands

- **Printed p. 24, Sec. 4.1.** The bubble expands at near the speed of light, converting the space around the nucleation point to the true vacuum. The particle masses inside differ, and the bubble collapses gravitationally.
- **Printed p. 54, Sec. 6.** If the electroweak vacuum is metastable, bubbles of the "true, negative-energy vacuum" can be nucleated, and once formed a bubble expands at the speed of light.
- **Printed pp. 36-37.** Inside the light cone the spacetime is anti-de Sitter, collapsing into a singularity.
- **Eq. (1.1).** The quoted inputs: M_h = 125.18 ± 0.16 GeV and M_t = 173.1 ± 0.9 GeV.
