## Summary

The paper's central results stand, and so does its main inference:

- the detection of a ~1-minute millimetre flare;
- the attribution of the ACA point source to that flare;
- the flare's peak luminosity and its comparison with solar flares;
- the inference that there is "no need to posit" the 1-4 AU belt.

We reproduce the numbers behind each of these, and an independent later analysis confirms the flare.

Our five findings are **discrepancies**: noise figures that do not combine, an uncertainty that does not propagate, a background estimate we cannot reproduce, a luminosity given without its band, and a citation that misnames the authors of a reference. None moves a conclusion of the paper, and none is a refutation.

## Findings

### 1. The rms noise figures: discrepancy

- **The paper states** (abstract and Sec. 3.2, p. 3): the ACA image combining all 13 observations has rms 47 μJy beam⁻¹. The image of the first 12 has 68 μJy beam⁻¹, and the image of the final observation has 150 μJy beam⁻¹.
- **What we find:**
  - Combining 68 and 150 by inverse variance gives 62 μJy beam⁻¹, not 47.
  - A combined image should not be quieter than the optimal combination of its two parts, so the three quotes are not mutually consistent as printed. Flare artefacts in the final image, or different noise regions, could account for it; the text does not let us decide.
  - This does not bear on the detection, which rests on per-observation imaging and the 2-second light curve.

### 2. The uncertainty on δ: discrepancy

- **The paper states** (Sec. 4.1, p. 5): with α = −1.77 ± 0.45 and α = 1.22 − 0.9δ, "we can determine δ = 3.32 ± 0.83".
- **What we find:**
  - δ = (1.22 + 1.77)/0.9 = 3.32, which reproduces.
  - Linear propagation gives σ_δ = 0.45/0.9 = **0.50**, not 0.83.
  - The method behind 0.83 is not stated.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/1802.08257.py` (DISCREPANCY item (iii)).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

### 3. The background estimate 13 (+10, −8): discrepancy (not reproduced)

- **The paper states** (Sec. 4.2, p. 6): "the number of sources with flux density > 150 μJy that are expected within the ACA primary beam (FWHM ∼ 39″) is 13 (+10, −8)".
- **What we find.** Using the source counts the paper cites (Carniani et al. 2015), N(> 150 μJy) = 1.74 × 10⁴ deg⁻², with a range of 1.28-2.17 × 10⁴. The expected count depends on the area taken:
  - the disk inside the FWHM (r = 19.5″) gives **1.6** [1.2-2.0]; ASPECS 2020 counts give 1.0;
  - the primary-beam-weighted area gives 2.3;
  - the disk out to the first null (r = 46″) gives 9.0 [6.6-11.1]. This is the first area at which the expected count falls inside the quoted interval;
  - 13 itself would need a radius of about 55″.

  The paper states neither the area nor the formula. The figure is therefore not reproduced here, which does not make it wrong: cosmic variance, Galactic-plane sources or a different count model could each account for it.

### 4. The quiescent luminosity 10²¹ erg s⁻¹: discrepancy (band unstated)

- **The paper states** (Sec. 4.2, p. 6): assuming a flat millimetre spectrum, the ∼30 μJy excess "yields a quiescent millimeter luminosity of 10²¹ erg s⁻¹ or less than 10⁻⁹ × the bolometric luminosity of the star".
- **What we find:**
  - At 1.3 pc, 30 μJy gives L_ν = 6.07 × 10¹⁰ erg s⁻¹ Hz⁻¹.
  - A flat spectrum from 0 to 233 GHz gives 1.41 × 10²² erg s⁻¹, which is 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ L_bol.
  - 10²¹ erg s⁻¹ is recovered only by integrating over about 16 GHz, close to the observed 224-242 GHz span, which gives 1.1 × 10²¹. The "< 10⁻⁹ L_bol" statement holds under that reading (1.7 × 10⁻¹⁰), not under the 0-233 GHz one.
  - The band is not stated.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/rederive/1802.08257.py` (DISCREPANCY item (ii)).
- **Class:** discrepancy.

## What stands

- **The flare.**
  - A peak of 100 ± 4 mJy, lasting about 1 minute, with FWHM 30 s.
  - Its fluence matches the final-observation flux: we compute 1.16-1.57 mJy against the measured 1.17 ± 0.10 mJy.
  - Image weighting of the final observation gives a combined 199-312 μJy, within 1σ of the measured 334 ± 48 μJy.
  - The peak L_ν of 2.02 × 10¹⁴ erg s⁻¹ Hz⁻¹ reproduces (paper: 2.04 ± 0.15 × 10¹⁴).
  - The comparison with solar flares holds: about 2 × 10¹³, a ratio of about 10.
  - An independent later analysis (Burton et al. 2025) confirms the flare attribution.
- **Other numbers that reproduce:** δ = 3.32, and the 12-m excess of about 30 μJy (we compute 27 ± 9.8 μJy).
- **The belt inference.** "No need to posit" (p. 6) and "no need to invoke" (pp. 1 and 7) the 1-4 AU belt are accurate as worded.
  - The first-12 ACA image disfavours a belt at Anglada et al.'s ~200 μJy at about 2σ, and at 3σ for their full 340 μJy.
  - A fainter belt is not excluded, and the paper does not claim otherwise.
- **The pre-main-sequence sentence** in Sec. 1 is a faithful statement of background.
