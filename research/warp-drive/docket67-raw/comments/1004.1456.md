## Summary

The paper's reproduction of Kodama's results stands, and so does its Brown-York identification. Our one finding is a typographical slip in how the Einstein equations are written in the text. The equation that follows uses the correct form. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Sec. IX, p. 7: "8π G_ab = T_ab" (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (p. 7):
  - G_t̂r̂(r, t) = 2ṁ(r, t) e^{Φ(r,t)} / [r² (1 − 2m(r, t)/r)] (76).
  - Then: "whence, via the Einstein equations 8π G_ab = T_ab, we have the flux density f(r, t) = T_t̂r̂(r, t) = (1/4π) ṁ(r, t) e^{Φ(r,t)} / [r² (1 − 2m(r, t)/r)]" (77).
- **What we find:**
  - (77) is (76) divided by 8π. That is, (77) follows from G_ab = 8π T_ab.
  - The text's "8π G_ab = T_ab" would instead give T = 8π G, larger by a factor 64π².
  - The equation is right and the words are a slip.
- **Shown by:** reading eqs. (76)-(77); `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/shards/shard02.json`, key `kodama-1980-mass-flux`, fields `later_literature` (the Abreu & Visser row) and `grade_evidence` (iv).
- **Class:** discrepancy (typographical).
