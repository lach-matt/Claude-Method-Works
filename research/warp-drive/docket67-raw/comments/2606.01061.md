## What stands

- **Eq. (2.22):** the diagonal-static Misner-Sharp formula is correct. With g_ϑϑ = r² g_rr it gives exactly m(r) = m for the isotropic Schwarzschild metric (2.26).
- **The constant-density star:** with g_rr = (1 + r²/b²)⁻², (2.22) gives exactly the paper's (2.27), m(r) = 2r³/(b²(1 + r²/b²)³).
- **The metric (2.26):** the isotropic form of Schwarzschild's vacuum solution is correct. Its Einstein tensor vanishes identically, and R = r(1 + m/2r)² maps it onto the areal form.
- **Downstream of Sec. 2.3:** the point below does not reach the results that follow, because (2.27) is correct. We did not re-derive (2.28)-(2.33).

## Finding

### Eqs. (2.24)-(2.25), the isotropic-coordinate form of the Misner-Sharp mass (p. 7). Class: discrepancy (in the served text; source of the discrepancy undetermined)

**What the paper states, as served.**
- (2.24): m(r) = (r/2)√g_rr [1 − (1 + (1/2) ∂_r g_rr / g_rr)²].
- (2.25): m(r) = −(r/2)√g_rr [∂_r g_rr / g_rr + (1/4)(∂_r g_rr / g_rr)²].

**What we found.**
- **The bracket is not dimensionless.** As served, the bracket mixes a pure number with ∂_r g_rr / g_rr, which has dimension 1/length.
- **The step from (2.22) puts r inside.** Substituting g_ϑϑ = r² g_rr into (2.22) gives (r/2)√g_rr [1 − (1 + (r/2) ∂_r g_rr / g_rr)²]. The factor r sits inside the bracket, and the r² that follows from it belongs in (2.25).
- **The paper's own check needs that r.** With the factor r, the sanity check that follows (2.25) works: g_rr = (1 + m/2r)⁴ gives m(r) = m, and the star gives (2.27). Without it, the same check gives m(mr − m + 2r²)/(2r³), not m.

Whether the factor r is missing from the PDF or only from the text extraction we could not determine. Since (2.22), (2.26) and (2.27) are all correct, the issue is confined to the printed intermediate forms.

**What shows it.**
- `rederive/schwarzschild-isotropic-vacuum.py`, check E5. Re-run 2026-10-04, it prints "eq.(2.22) with g_thth = r^2 g_rr: m - M = 0" and "eq.(2.25) as text layer reads it: m - M = M*(M - 2*rr**2)*(rr - 1)/(2*rr**3) (nonzero: missing r factors)".
- `shards/shard05.json`, key `schwarzschild-isotropic-vacuum`, rederivation.outcome E5.
- A direct sympy check on 2026-10-04 of (2.24) with and without the factor r, for both (2.26) and the star metric, gave the values quoted above.

## Scope

This finding concerns arXiv:2606.01061v1 as served to us. No journal version was read or compared, and nothing here is a claim about one. It is a discrepancy in two intermediate displayed formulas, possibly introduced by text extraction, and not a refutation. The equations we checked, (2.22), (2.26) and (2.27), stand.
