## Summary

The temperature T₀ = 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K stands, under the hypotheses the paper states for it. Our one finding is small: recomputing the combination from Table 2's own entries gives χ² and a mean that differ slightly from the printed ones. The difference does not change the conclusion that the 16 measurements agree. It is a discrepancy, not a refutation.

## Finding

### Table 2 and p. 5: the combined fit, recomputed from the table (discrepancy)

- **The paper states** (Table 2 and p. 5):
  - 16 measurements: 15 from the literature, chosen by a 50 mK cutoff, plus "This Work" (2.7260 ± 1.3 mK).
  - The mean row reads "Mean 2.72548 .57".
  - "The χ² for the 16 measurements is 17.9 for 15 DOF. A χ² this large should be expected about 26% of the time."
- **What we find:**
  - We combined the 16 Table 2 entries by inverse-variance weighting.
  - The mean is **2.725486 K**, with uncertainty 0.573 mK. Printed: 2.72548 K and 0.57 mK.
  - χ² is **18.07** for 15 degrees of freedom. Printed: 17.9. Three independent recomputations give 18.07-18.1.
  - The difference in the mean is 0.006 mK, about 1% of the quoted uncertainty.
  - Either χ² gives a probability near the printed 26%: P(χ² > 17.9 | 15) = 0.268 and P(χ² > 18.07 | 15) = 0.259 (survival function recomputed in this stage).
  - Every other p. 5 statement reproduces: FIRAS carries 97.8% of the weight (printed 97%); the non-FIRAS average is 2.7294 ± 3.84 mK, 1.01σ from the mean (printed 2.729 ± 0.0038, 1.0σ); the CN average is 2.7420 ± 0.0173 K (printed 2.742 ± 0.017); and the largest pull is 2.30 (p. 6: "agree within 2.5 times their uncertainties").
  - **A cause is not shown.** Rounding of the table entries is a plausible one. The inputs are mutually consistent whichever χ² is used.
- **Shown by:** `research/warp-drive/docket67-raw/adjudications/0911.1955.json`, `decision` (the adjudicator's own recomputation from Table 2) and `corrections_to_record` ("Discrepancy, not a refutation: the recomputed chi2 is 18.07 against the printed 17.9"). The recomputation needs only the 16 rows of Table 2, which we re-read at source on 2026-10-04 and found identical to the adjudicator's transcription.
- **Class:** discrepancy.

## What stands

- **T₀ = 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K** and its hypotheses. These are:
  - a blackbody Doppler-shifted to first order (eqs. 1-2);
  - the 15% sky cut;
  - the inflated FIRAS uncertainty;
  - the condition on WMAP errors (p. 4);
  - the 50 mK selection;
  - the inverse-variance combination.
- **The recalibration through the WMAP spacecraft velocity.** It is known to < 1 cm/s (p. 1), and it gives 2.7260 ± 0.0013 K.
- **The non-FIRAS compilation**, 2.729 ± 0.0038 K.
- **The agreement of the 16 measurements**, which our recomputation reproduces (p ≈ 0.26-0.27 for 15 degrees of freedom).
