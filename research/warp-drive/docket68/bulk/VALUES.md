### 1. A value pins the mass; a label does not (V1, V2)

- **The values put to you were a molecule's mean bond length and its frequencies** (EXACTCOPY.md). Both come from the
  copy's motion under its laws.
- **The mean bond length is strictly monotonic in the reduced mass, across a factor of 100 either way** (V1). So one
  value of it pins one mass. The vibrational frequency, which goes as m^(−1/2), does the same.
- **A label is different** (V2, from exactcopy.py). The set of bound vibrational levels is the same for every reduced
  mass from 0.898 to 1.010 of ours. Inside that window a label leaves the mass free.
