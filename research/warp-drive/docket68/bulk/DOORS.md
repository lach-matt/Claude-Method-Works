## What the mathematics gives

### 1. On the plane, censorship forces the negative reading the plane shows (K3)

- **Your corridor is nonsingular and one-way between two asymptotically flat regions** (plane.py P1). Its path from
  one region's past infinity to the other's future infinity is the kind FSW's Lemma 2 excludes.
  - Applying FSW to genuinely distinct regions, rather than copies in a covering space, is the board's extension of
    Lemma 2.
- **So on the plane, either the averaged null energy fails or global hyperbolicity does.**
- **coin.py reads the averaged null energy as −0.826 E/m per leg: the first.**
- **The negative reading is a consequence of the corridor's geometry, not an accident.** FSW does not apply, and the
  geometry itself is why.

### 2. In five dimensions the null energy holds along the passage (K2)

- **A ray running along the plane is a ray of the bulk** (UMBILIC.md U1).
- **Along it the surface term vanishes,** for either sign of tension, and the bulk term is zero. So the five-dimensional
  averaged null energy along the passage is exactly zero. That is UMBILIC.md U2's result again (STRUCTURAL).
- **This is the board's reading of your items 117 and 120:** the condition holds in five dimensions and only appears
  violated on the plane.

### 3. On the face-to-face composite it holds everywhere, conditionally (K1)

- **With the sheets coinciding exactly, the summed surface has null energy λ_RS·k_y² ≥ 0** (static.py S4).
- **That rests on H-COMPOSITE-SURFACE.**
  - **Control:** position 2's sheet alone reads −(1/3)·λ_RS·k_y², negative.
  - If the two sheets were thickened with different profiles, the pointwise condition could fail.

### 4. What no theorem READ settles

- **The five-dimensional theorems READ are Gao–Wald and Galloway–Schleich–Witt–Woolgar.** Both are theorems of a bulk
  with a conformal boundary. They speak of curves between parts of that boundary, or of time delay.
- **None is shown to speak of a passage between two asymptotic regions of a plane's own geometry,** which is where the
  corridor lies. censor.py said the same: *"How such a theorem bears on it is open."*
- **The premises still open in them** (censor.py):
  - the null generic condition, which pure AdS fails and the corridor plausibly supplies;
  - smoothness at a thin plane;
  - where the bulk ends (H-POINCARE-PATCH).
- **So: no censorship theorem READ forbids the corridor, and none permits it.**

### 5. Entanglement alone carries nothing (K4)

- **The no-communication theorem** (standard, not READ): shared entanglement with no channel carries 0 bits.
- **With unlimited shared entanglement, a sent qubit carries at most 2 bits** (superdense coding, Holevo). So an N-bit
  README needs at least N/2 qubits through a causal channel: 1.37×10¹⁵ at the board's example README (STRUCTURAL).
- **The channel runs between the corridor's two asymptotic regions, not across a distance:** your item 127 has the
  positions coincide.
- **That your passage as entanglement (H-PASSAGE-IS-ENTANGLEMENT, item 136) still needs such a channel is the board's
  deduction,** set against your hypothesis.

### 6. What still bears on the corridor

- **The compact-bulk control stands** (censor.py). In a bounded extra dimension, Chruściel–Galloway–Solis Thm 5.2 would
  forbid the passage outright. Your unbounded dimension (item 136, answer 9) is what keeps it away.
- **On the face-to-face composite, the pair is one Randall–Sundrum II plane at long range** (static.py S2b). That
  narrows what wall C must build: the corridor on one RS II plane.
