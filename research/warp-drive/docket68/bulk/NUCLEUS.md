## What passes

### 1. Molecular lines are where your laws would show (U1)

- **Electronic levels do not depend on μ, the proton-to-electron mass ratio.** Vibrations scale as μ^(−1/2), rotations
  as μ^(−1). That is Hanneke, Kuzhan and Lunstad 2020 (arXiv:2007.15750), p.6, eq. (14), READ.
- **The board's check reproduces their sensitivities:** electronic 0, vibrational −1/2, rotational −1 (STRUCTURAL).
- **Measured sensitivities of real lines** (Bagdonaite et al. 2013, READ):
  - ammonia's inversion line, K = −4.46;
  - methanol's observed lines, K from −1.0 to −32.8;
  - molecular hydrogen's lines, only about 0.05.

### 2. The board refit a key measurement from its published data (U2)

- **Bagdonaite et al. 2013** (arXiv:1311.3438) measured ten methanol lines in a galaxy at z = 0.89, *"a lookback time
  of 7.5 billion years"*. It is the only distant galaxy where methanol has been detected (Muller et al. 2021, READ by
  the verifier).
- **The board refit their Table I (17 points) and reproduced their statistical result:**
  - Δμ/μ = 1.49×10⁻⁷;
  - reduced χ² = 10.2;
  - ±1.50×10⁻⁷ if the fit error is scaled by √χ². That is the group's stated convention in Muller et al. 2021; the
    2013 paper does not say. They quote (1.5 ± 1.5)×10⁻⁷.
- **A null test:** with the K values shuffled among the 17 points, 12% of 2,000 shuffles give a slope at least as large.
  **No change of μ is seen.**
- **The constraint rests mostly on one line,** the 12.2 GHz line, K = −32.8.
  - Without its four points the result is (7.5 ± 6.7)×10⁻⁷: 4.5 times weaker.
  - Muller et al. 2021 (READ by the verifier) note that this line *"probes a different sightline"*.
- **Control** (STRUCTURAL, the fit is linear): a Δμ/μ of 10⁻⁶ injected into the same velocities is recovered as
  1.000×10⁻⁶.

## What it bounds, and what it does not (U3)

- **It bounds a ratio, for a bulk.**
  - Their robust result is (−1.0 ± 0.8 stat ± 1.0 sys)×10⁻⁷.
  - In the board's combination, |Δμ/μ| < 3.6×10⁻⁷ at 2σ.
  - The same group's newer result from the same absorber reaches the same number at 3σ: (−1.8 ± 1.2)×10⁻⁷ (Muller et
    al. 2021, READ by the verifier).
- **It is the ratio m_p/m_e only.** A change of both masses by the same fraction would not show. That is SAMELIGHT.md's
  uniform scaling.
- **It is the methanol-bearing bulk of one absorber.** A minority of other matter shifts the line centres only in
  proportion to its share.
  - At the bound, the K = −32.8 line of the whole population would move 3.5 km/s. The lines are 12–20 km/s wide.
  - So a fraction f of crossed matter is bounded only at about 3.6×10⁻⁷/f, and only if it made methanol there.
- **So the distant gas says this:** our universe's own gas, in bulk, shares our μ to a few parts in 10⁷.
  - It does not search for crossed matter.
  - A crossed sample in hand could be measured far more finely in a laboratory. Today's laboratory limits on μ's drift
    are about 10⁻¹⁶ to 10⁻¹⁷ per year (Hanneke et al. eq. 3, READ).
