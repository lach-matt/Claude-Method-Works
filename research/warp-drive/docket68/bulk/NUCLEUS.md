### 1. Your two sectors are the two mass mechanisms physics knows

- **Hanneke, Kuzhan and Lunstad 2020** (arXiv:2007.15750, p.2, READ):
  - protons and neutrons: *"Around 99 % of their mass arises from the strong interaction"*;
  - the electron: *"Its mass arises from its interaction with the Higgs field"*.
- **So your laws act on exactly the two places mass comes from: the strong interaction (the nucleus) and the Higgs
  field (the electron).**
- **That meets your item 136, answer 2:** the release at position 2 *"activates the higgs field forming the mass"*.
  Under item 144 the Higgs field is one of the two places a universe's laws enter.

### 2. Where μ shows: molecular lines, not atomic ones (U1)

- **Electronic levels do not depend on μ. Vibrations scale as μ^(−1/2), rotations as μ^(−1).** That is Hanneke et al.
  p.6, eq. (14), READ.
- **The board's check reproduces their sensitivities:** electronic 0, vibrational −1/2, rotational −1 (STRUCTURAL).
- **Some molecules are far more sensitive** (Bagdonaite et al. 2013, READ):
  - ammonia's inversion line, K = −4.46;
  - methanol's lines, K from −1.0 to −32.8;
  - molecular hydrogen's lines, only about 0.05.

### 3. The board refit the best measurement from its published data (U2)

- **Bagdonaite et al. 2013** (arXiv:1311.3438) measured ten methanol lines in a galaxy at z = 0.89, *"a lookback time
  of 7.5 billion years"*.
- **The board refit their Table I (17 points) and reproduced their statistical result exactly:**
  - Δμ/μ = 1.49×10⁻⁷;
  - reduced χ² = 10.2;
  - uncertainty ±1.50×10⁻⁷ once scaled by √χ², as theirs is. They quote (1.5 ± 1.5)×10⁻⁷.
- **Control** (STRUCTURAL, the fit is linear): a Δμ/μ of 10⁻⁶ injected into the same velocities is recovered.

- **Across all high-redshift molecular spectra** (Hanneke et al. p.3, eq. 2): *"|∆μ|/μ < 10⁻⁶ − 10⁻⁷ over ∼10¹⁰ yr"*.
