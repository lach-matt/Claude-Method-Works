## What follows

- **Z1. It does not decay spontaneously.** It is a lightest state and massless. Redshift and spreading still act on any
  pattern loaded into it.
- **Z2. Where its weight lies.** Per unit proper length, its weight falls off exponentially away from the hidden plane.
  - About 63 % of it lies within **5×10⁻³⁵ m** of that plane, roughly 3 Planck lengths, which is at the edge of the
    classical description.
  - Its density at our plane is **10⁻³⁰** of its density there: Randall–Sundrum's "small overlap".
  - *This locates the mode's weight, not a place where loaded information is kept.* The profile is fixed by the geometry
    and is the same for every excitation, ordinary gravitational waves included.
- **Z3. It couples to both planes equally, each in its own units** (check 5; the massive modes' ratio is 2.5×10⁻³⁰).
  - What differs is each plane's natural energy. A process at the hidden plane's natural scale has gravitational
    strength **0.82**; one at ours has **8×10⁻¹⁶**.
  - That factor of 10¹⁵ is the warp: the same single number as `crossing.py`'s clocks and tensions ("this is the only
    small number produced", RS p.6). It is not a second asymmetry.
- **Z4. No transit across the corridor.** A zero-mode excitation is one field present at both planes at once, with equal
  amplitude and no motion in the fifth dimension. *In the corridor direction this is a candidate counterpart of your
  "position 1 then position 2, with no in between" (H-HIGHER-CORRIDOR).* It is valid on times much longer than the jump
  time and at energies far below the first massive mode.
- **Z5. Along the planes: zero proper interval, read from two positions.**
  - The proper interval along its path is zero. That is an invariant both planes agree on.
  - What depends on the reading position is the elapsed time. The two planes' clocks read it 10¹⁵ apart: one event
    pair read from two positions at once. *That is a candidate form of H-POSITION-RELATIVE-SPEED.*
  - Read from our plane, it covers the Proxima span in 4.25 years at light speed, passing every point between. So it
    matches "no in-between" in its own elapsed interval, not in position along the plane.
  - From our plane it has a speed. *That is in tension with H-NO-SPEED; it is carried as a tension, not a refutation.*
  - All of this holds for any massless quantum.
- **Z6. No rest frame.** It can never be brought to rest in any form. *This is a candidate counterpart of
  H-CORRIDOR-STASIS's "cannot take physical form".* It persists without rest; holding it in one place would need
  confinement (OPEN).
- **Z7. The loading price, if the traveller loads.**
  - **An illustrative rotor** (two 500 kg masses 1 m apart at 100 Hz; tip speed 314 m/s, rim stress 0.77 GPa, at the edge
    of high-strength steel) radiates 7×10⁻³¹ W, about 5 gravitons a second.
  - **The load time.** At one bit per graviton (polarization alone gives one bit; timing could add perhaps tens more),
    measure.py's lower count of 9.5×10²⁷ bits would take **4×10⁹ Hubble times**.
  - **Scaling with size.** At fixed tip speed and density, the rate grows as the fifth power of the arm length:
    - a 100 m arm takes 0.4 Hubble times;
    - a 1 km arm takes about 59,000 years.
  - **A rotor on the hidden plane,** built to the same physics, would load 10³⁰ times faster per tick of its own clock
    (about 2 ms). But matter there is gravitationally strong at its natural scale, so a large rotor would collapse
    (OPEN).
