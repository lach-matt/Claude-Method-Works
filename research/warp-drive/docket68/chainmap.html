The Warp Theorem · item 150
For an object whose README has N bits, there is exactly one corridor, fixed by N alone, and through it the README becomes, one way and exactly, the object at position 2.

E = √(N h c⁵ ln2 / 8π²G) = 459,404,002.42 J × √N  ·  m = GE/c⁴  ·  r₀ = 2m  ·  throat area = N × 4 ln2 Planck areas. The theorem is proved exactly when no lemma is open (item 151).

The decisive open lemma

Where B4 stands

Worked in order after item 152, and verified once. The shape is settled: your two separate positions, reached through a dimension, are one end of the bulk, and the passage deforms through it (B4a). The far boundary needs ℓ > 2R_reach in the board's model (B4c). What is open is B4b: during the hold the plane is static, so the bulk within the hold's double cone must be the static local bulk, and it must be regular there. The extremal horizon's redshift keeps that depth small near the throat.

r = 2.05mr = 2.25mr = 3m
ℓ ≫ r₀: depth needed1.9–2.2 m4.75 m8.22 m
ℓ ≫ r₀: series-stable to1.50 m1.75 m2.25 m
ℓ = r₀: depth needed1.2–1.5 m2.1–2.4 m3.26 m
ℓ = r₀: series-stable to0.65 m0.80 m1.20 m

Not decided. In the regime measurement allows (ℓ ≫ r₀), nothing the series reaches is singular, and it reaches 1.5 m of the ~1.9 m needed at the throat. At ℓ = r₀ the series stops near its own radius, where a singularity and a bulk horizon look the same. Closing B4b needs a method past the series: an elliptic solve of the static bulk, or a resummation with a bounded error.
