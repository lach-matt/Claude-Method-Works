## What the literature says (READ 2026-10-05, alphaXiv; the APS abstract via Firecrawl; no 403 met)

### Stationary global states are established physics

- **Page–Wootters.** The constraint Ĵ|Ψ⟩ = 0 holds, and the physical states are "static objects which do not evolve".
  Conditional states nevertheless obey the ordinary Schrödinger equation relative to a clock (Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone
  1504.04215v3 p.2, eqs. 1–5).
- **The external view is static; the internal view evolves.** "The Hamiltonian constraint refers to the external
  observer's point of view, who sees the whole laboratory (or universe) as a static system… That, however, does not
  prevent the internal observer from observing evolving systems" (same paper, p.7).
- **A laboratory illustration.** Moreva et al. (1310.4691v1) showed a two-photon global state that is static, with one
  photon serving as the clock for the other.
- **Wheeler–DeWitt.** The canonical theory's "frozen formalism" (Isham gr-qc/9210011v1, printed p.66).
- **Hartle–Hawking.** They call their no-boundary state the universe's "ground state", in quotation marks (APS abstract,
  PRD 28, 2960). Its classical limit is de Sitter space, not a stationary coupled plasma.
- **Loop quantum cosmology.** A matter field serves as the internal clock (Ashtekar, Pawlowski & Singh
  gr-qc/0604013v3 p.1).

### Internal observers see ordinary physics

- Marletto–Vedral (1610.04773v2 p.4): it is "impossible empirically to distinguish" global stationarity from a universe
  that is not stationary.
- Objections are on record: Kuchař's propagator problem, and Unruh–Wald, as restated by Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone p.7
  and Page gr-qc/9303020v2 pp.6–7, 14. Kuchař called Page "a solipsist of an instant".

### The classical CMB

- **The standard account.** Squeezing and decoherence make the CMB classical, and the environment acts as the
  measuring device (Kiefer–Polarski 0810.0087v2 pp.3–8). No observer is invoked.
- **The critical school.** Decoherence leaves the problem of outcomes (Perez–Sahlmann–Sudarsky gr-qc/0508100v3
  pp.13–15). Those authors argue *against* observation as the trigger: "Are we to believe that until 1992 the CMB was
  in fact perfectly homogeneous? Hardly", because "our actions could not be their cause" (p.10). They propose objective
  collapse instead.
- **Decoupling.** Recombination is described as rate-versus-expansion physics (Hu 0802.3688v1 printed pp.6–8).
- **Tests.** Objective collapse has been tested against the CMB. One CSL version is ruled out (Martin–Vennin
  1906.04405v4 p.5); others remain compatible (Piccirilli et al. 1709.06237v3). No paper read tests collapse triggered
  by an observer.

### Two kinds of observation

- **Matter as observer is standard physics.** Einselection makes records, and observers "eavesdrop on the environment"
  (Zurek 0903.5082v1 p.2).
- **But records do not by themselves pick an outcome.** "Does decoherence solve the measurement problem? Clearly not"
  (Joos, quoted by Schlosshauer quant-ph/0312059v4 p.2).
- **Sentient observation needs physics beyond unitary quantum mechanics.** Chalmers–McQueen (2105.02314v1) propose
  integrated-information-driven collapse, testable "in principle". Bong et al. (1907.05607v4 p.7) say a Wigner's-friend
  test with real observers might fail.
- **Every discriminating experiment proposed is unperformed.** The Wigner's-friend "friends" so far have been photons
  (Proietti 1902.05080v2 p.3–4; Bong p.5–6). The conscious-observer experiments are contested (Walleczek & von
  Stillfried 2019; Radin et al. 2020 reply).

## The model, computed (an illustration of the mechanism, not evidence about the universe)

**The set-up.** A clock register labels the cooling. A toy photon–matter pair exchanges an excitation while ionised,
with the coupling stepping off at T_dec = 2,973 K. The whole system is given a history-state Hamiltonian (the
Feynman–Kitaev construction, NAMED-NOT-READ).

1. **The constant.**
   - The global state is that Hamiltonian's ground state: energy 1×10⁻¹⁶, unique, overlap with the history state 1 to
     12 places.
   - It is unchanged by its own evolution.
   - **Here M's "constant" has a precise form.** What is constant is the global state. It contains the whole history,
     decoupling included, as correlations.
2. **Relative to the clock**, the medium exchanges while hot (its excitation swings by 0.97) and freezes after
   decoupling. The conditional states equal ordinary unitary evolution to 10⁻¹⁴, which is empirical equivalence.
3. **The unobserved medium**, with the clock traced out, is a *mixture of the whole history* (purity 0.60), half of it
   in coupled epochs.
   - It differs from M's reading as modelled, the medium frozen hot with no clock, by a trace distance of **0.55**.
   - It differs from the coupled epochs alone by 0.27.
   - **So in the established relational-time picture, "unobserved" is not "coupled": it is all of history at once.**
   - M's reading, in which the unobserved universe *is* the coupled medium and observation brings the clock and with it
     decoupling, is a distinct hypothesis. It needs its own rule. Both readings are carried.
4. **Two kinds of observer.**
   - A matter observer, a record register built explicitly and traced numerically, makes the clock classical: the
     coherences fall from 2×10⁻² to 0.
   - Conditioned on its record, it sees exactly the same medium.
   - The control: an imperfect record leaves coherences (1.6×10⁻²).
   - A sentient observer is, in unitary quantum mechanics, one more record and sees the same. Telling the two apart
     needs non-unitary physics, and all such tests are unperformed.
5. **What would distinguish H-UNOBSERVED-MEDIUM from the standard account is OPEN.**
   - Global stationarity is empirically indistinguishable (Marletto–Vedral).
   - Perez–Sahlmann–Sudarsky's circularity argument against observer-triggered decoupling is a source's argument. It is
     recorded, not taken as a refutation.

## For M

  serious proposals but no experiment yet.
