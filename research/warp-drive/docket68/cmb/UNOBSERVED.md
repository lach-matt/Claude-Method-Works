## What the literature says (READ 2026-10-05; routes in the instrument)

### Relational time

- **A static global state.** In Page–Wootters, Ĵ|Ψ⟩ = 0, and the physical states are "static" objects. Conditional
  states obey Schrödinger's equation relative to a clock (Giovannetti, Lloyd & Maccone, 1504.04215v3 p.2).
- **The unobserved view.** The external view is the static global state itself, and the external observer is "a
  hypothetical entity" (GLM p.7).
- **The clock weighting.** GLM (p.4) call the weighting over clock readings "completely arbitrary".
- **Wheeler, quoted there:** "the past has no existence except as it is recorded in the present".
- **Zurek and Landsman, quoted by Schlosshauer (p.8):**
  - Zurek: "Our experience of the classical reality does not apply to the universe as a whole, seen from the outside,
    but to the systems within it".
  - Landsman: "A world without parts … is a world without facts".
- **Status.** This is a standard *proposal* with live objections: Kuchař and Unruh–Wald, as GLM p.7 restates them. It
  has "never been developed beyond the toy-model stage" (Marletto & Vedral p.3).
- **Hartle–Hawking.** They call their state the "'ground state' or state of minimum excitation" (APS abstract). Its de
  Sitter limit is scoped to a minisuperspace model.

### The classical CMB

- **Standard account.** Squeezing and decoherence make the perturbations classical, with no observer invoked.
- **The critical school.** Perez, Sahlmann & Sudarsky (gr-qc/0508100v3 p.10) argue against observation as the trigger,
  and propose objective collapse instead. Objective collapse is one candidate for what M calls matter observing
  non-consciously.
- **Scope of both.** Both concern *inflationary perturbations*, not photon–baryon decoupling. The CSL tests against the
  CMB are scoped the same way.

### Two kinds of observation

- **Matter's.** Records made by matter are standard physics: Zurek's einselection and redundancy.
- **Records don't pick an outcome.** "Does decoherence solve the measurement problem? Clearly not" (Joos).
- **Eraser experiments** (Kim et al.; Walborn et al.; Ma et al. 1203.4834v2):
  - A which-path record held *only in matter* removes the fringes.
  - Erasing it restores them only in conditioned subsets.
  - The authors give awareness no role: "regardless of whether or not an observer accesses this information" (Ma et
    al. p.2).
- **Consciousness-collapse models.**
  - Chalmers–McQueen (2105.02314v1 pp.29–32) and Okon–Sebastián (1801.05487v2) both reproduce Born statistics.
  - Chalmers–McQueen's *simple* versions "are falsified by the quantum Zeno effect", while "more complex versions remain
    compatible" and "can be tested by experiments with quantum computers" (abstract p.1).
  - Chalmers–McQueen (p.43) float a biased variant. Perceptual consciousness may obey the constraints while "agentive
    experience does not". Collapses from it "might be biased", and "it is arguable that our current evidence leaves
    room open for it". They add: "We do not find this picture especially attractive".
  - Their bias is **agentive**: perceptual observation stays Born. So it fits M's "conscious observation forces" only in
    part. It does fit O9's scenario, where Alice acts.
- **Outcome bias by intention has been studied.** The second build's "no outcome bias has been measured" was wrong.
  - Bösch, Steinkamp & Boller 2006 (Psychol. Bull. 132, 497; public APA abstract) pooled 380 studies. They found "a
    significant but very small overall effect size", strongly inversely related to sample size, which "could in
    principle be a result of publication bias".
  - Radin et al. 2006 replied (title only READ).
  - **The effect size itself is not yet READ.** Pricing ε at it is OPEN.
- **Double-slit attention studies.**
  - These measure interference visibility, not outcome bias.
  - Their pre-registered arms are null (Walleczek & von Stillfried 2019; Guerrer 2019), and Tremblay's open-data
    re-analysis finds no significance after correction.
  - Radin et al. 2020 dispute the statistics. This is recorded, not adjudicated.

### Many observers

- **QBism.** "reality differs from one agent to another … What is real for an agent rests entirely on what that agent
  experiences" (Fuchs–Mermin–Schack 1311.5253v1 p.3), and "An outcome is created for the agent … only when it enters the
  experience of that agent" (p.4). Of the readings, this is the closest to M's item 51.
- **No-go theorems.** Brukner, Frauchiger–Renner and Local Friendliness (Bong et al.) support observer-relative facts
  *conditionally*: if quantum theory holds at the scale of observers, absoluteness of observed events is one candidate
  to drop. The experiments so far used photons as the "friends".
- **The balancing view.** Adlam–Rovelli (p.9) add a cross-perspective-link postulate. With it, "we will never have a case
  where a physical variable takes two different values relative to different observers"; only the assigned states
  differ.
- **Routes back to agreement:** cross-perspective links (Adlam–Rovelli, a postulate), redundant records (Zurek), and
  communication (QBism).
- **The CMB.** Each observer sees a different CMB sky, and a Lorentz boost relates one to another (Planck 2013 XXVII).

## The model, computed (an illustration of mechanisms, not evidence about the universe)

### The set-up

- **The clock.** A clock register labels the cooling, with 48 ticks from 2T_dec to T_dec/2 (H-CLOCK-IS-COOLING).
- **The medium.** A photon–matter pair exchanges an excitation while ionised, with coupling g(T) = g₀x_e(T). Here x_e is
  a logistic step at T_dec = 2,973 K (H-TOY-MEDIUM, H-TOY-SAHA).
- **Decoupling.** The pair is *detuned*, so decoupling changes the medium's eigenbasis
  (‖[H_hot, H_cold]‖ = 0.212). The freeze after decoupling is built into the toy.
- **The starting state.** The medium **starts in the coupled medium's own ground state** (H-START-COUPLED-GROUND). That
  is M's "ground state" read literally, and it is stationary for as long as the coupling holds (fidelity 1.000000). The
  second build's photon start is kept as a second case.
- **The whole.** The whole system is given a history-state (Feynman–Kitaev) Hamiltonian.

### 1. A universe constant as a whole

- The numerical ground state *is* the history state: overlap 1 to 12 places, energy 2.7×10⁻¹⁶.
- Its gap equals the construction's exact value, 1 − cos(π/2N) = 5.354×10⁻⁴.
- Conditioning that ground state on each clock reading gives ordinary evolution, with uniform clock weights.
- **Control:** a state that is not an eigenstate *does* evolve under the global Hamiltonian (fidelity 0.910).
- **What "ground" means here.** It is a property of the construction, mapped as H-CONSTANT-IS-STATIONARY.

### 2. "Unobserved", read three ways, none preferred

- **STATIC.** The global state itself, which is constant. This is M's "constant without observation", per GLM p.7.
- **TRACED.** The medium with the clock traced out: a mixture of the whole history, weighted by an arbitrary clock
  weighting (H-CLOCK-WEIGHT).
- **M-SUPPORT.** M's stronger reading as a computable rule: only the coupled epochs count. From the coupled ground state
  it is **pure and stationary**, which is M's reading taken literally.

From the coupled ground state, TRACED and M-SUPPORT differ by these trace distances:

- **From the coupled ground state, the difference is decoupling and nothing else.** The control is exactly zero.
- **From the photon start, the window contributes too.** The distance is 0.235 / 0.330 / 0.422 with decoupling and

### 3. Matter's records keep every probability

The epoch is recorded in matter with overlaps exp(−γ|Δk|), the form Marletto–Vedral give for a cosmological clock. A
reader then reads those records. Starting from the photon start:

  item 50, computed: matter's observation keeps every probability available.
- **Purity appears only when a reader conditions on an outcome**, and it depends on how the reader reads

### 4. Conscious selection (H-CONSCIOUS-SELECTS)

- **After matter's records, Born selection changes nothing.** P(+) is 0.5000 with selection and 0.5000 without. By
  linearity this holds for every later statistic (STRUCTURAL).
- **Before the records, selection differs from the coherent statistic.** It gives 0.5000 against a coherent 0.4871, a
  difference of 0.0129. This is the in-principle signature that Chalmers–McQueen's interference and Zeno tests target.
  None of those tests has been performed.
- **So reading (a), Born selection, matches the standard account in Born frequencies only.** The simple versions are
  already falsified, and the complex ones are untested.
- **Reading (b)**, biased selection, is priced in section 6.

### 5. Many perspectives (H-MANY-PERSPECTIVES)

- **Sequential model (H-SEQUENTIAL, H-LABEL-MATCH).** The second observer looks after the first. They disagree with
  probability sin²(θ/2), whatever the state, and that independence is **by construction**: the first collapse erases
  the state (STRUCTURAL).
- **Independent copies.** The disagreement depends on the state, p(1 − q) + (1 − p)q.
- **Agreement is computed, and returns** with probability 1.000000000000 in two cases: when one observer reads the

- **At the corridor's ends.**
  - Earth and Proxima see CMB skies that differ by a **0.294 mK** dipole. They move at 32.4 km/s relative to each
    other, and the Sun's dipole is 3.362 mK.
  - Earth's own annual motion modulates its sky by about **0.271 mK**. One observer's perspective already varies about
    that much within a year.
  - **Proxima's sky can be built from the Sun's.** Boosting the Sun's photons one by one gives a pure boosted blackbody

    Galilean sum misses by 6.5×10⁻¹¹. This is a real recoverability check, not a formula inverted.
- **So "inhomogeneous" is literally true** of different perspectives on the same object. It is recoverable when records
  are shared or the boost is known.
