## Where it is wrong as written (READ unless marked)

1. **"In 1935, Einstein and Rosen demonstrated that a black hole and a white hole are … two mouths".** Einstein & Rosen
   (Phys. Rev. 48, 73, p.73 abstract; pp.74–75) built a *static* solution of *modified* field equations: two sheets
   joined at r = 2m, read as a particle. The black-hole and white-hole regions come from Kruskal (1960, pp.1743–1745).
2. **The diagram.** In the Einstein–Rosen/Kruskal bridge the two mouths are the two *exterior* regions. The black hole
   and the white hole are each one interior region touching both mouths (Kruskal Fig. 2, p.1744), and nothing passes
   through.
3. **"Pinches off instantly".** It pinches off in a finite time: Fuller & Wheeler (1962, abstract p.919); Morris & Thorne
   (1988, p.397). Computed: πm from maximum to pinch-off, 2πm for its whole life.
4. **"Exotic matter with negative mass/energy density".** Morris & Thorne define exotic as tension exceeding a
   *positive* density, τ₀ > ρ₀c² > 0 (p.405): null-energy violation. A negative density is seen only by fast observers.
5. **The ER bridge as a "shortcut between two points".** It joins two separate asymptotic regions, between universes
   (Kruskal Fig. 1; Morris & Thorne Fig. 1(a); Hochberg & Visser gr-qc/9704082 p.2), and it is not traversable.
6. **"Breaks thermodynamics if static".** At odds with Hawking (1976, abstract p.191): in equilibrium a black hole is
   indistinguishable from a white hole. A white hole's established defect is instability (Eardley 1974).
7. **Cosmology page.**

     (Carlstrom, Holder & Reese, astro-ph/0208192, pp.4, 7).
