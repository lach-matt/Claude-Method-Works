### 1.1 The Hawking partner

    world-lines"*, with the warning that these pictures are *"heuristic only and should not be taken too literally"*.

    the initial form of the Hawking mode (82) on the other side of the horizon"*. The pair is a two-mode squeezed,

    state.
  - READ, Trivedi hep-th/9211011v2, PDF p.7: *"for these black holes, the Boulware, Unruh and Hartle-Hawking states are
