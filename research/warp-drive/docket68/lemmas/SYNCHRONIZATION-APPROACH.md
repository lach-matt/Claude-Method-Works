## 2. What general relativity gives (the test every reading must meet)

  The apparent horizon sits at r = 2Gm(v)/c^2 and grows as the energy enters. The event horizon anticipates the
  energy, but it too grows (focusing_parts F5's traces). Neither stands at full size from the opening.
- **Deduced (a standard identity; computed as T0 in stage 2).** At a marginally trapped sphere of areal radius r, the

  encloses exactly E(N) of Misner-Sharp energy at the opening. That count includes positive and negative energy alike.
- **So the question becomes exact.** What is the E(N) inside the face at the opening, when the README itself enters
  over the write? Each reading of synchronization below is judged by how it answers that.

## 5. Stage 2 -- four readings of synchronization, each with a pass/fail test

- **Y1. The entanglement sets the size.** Take RT/Bekenstein-Hawking with 137 (1): the face's area is
  4 l_P^2 N ln2, fixed at the formation, which is the synchronization. *Test (deduced, exact, sympy):* this area
  equals the board's bit area r0(N) at both ends. If it does, the size is set from the opening with no ramp (162). It
  fails if the two differ by any factor.
- **Y2. The energy inside at the opening (T0 with Y1).** The face is then a horizon of the README's area, so E(N) is
  inside it from the opening. *Test:* which source of that E(N) survives M's rulings.
  - (i) The README's energy is inside as one whole at the formation, and the long write is the README's encoding
    settling onto it rather than the energy arriving. Checked against 136 G (one thing, not two), 426 (no compact
    arrival), and source_capacity S2. A hole of E(N) holds exactly N ln2, by E(N)'s definition (136 (1)). Whether
    radiation of E(N) in the face's volume could hold N bits is S1's question; S4's figures say not (an estimate).
  - (ii) The pair supplies it: our side's E(N) matched by position 2's own (240). Checked against E1x: nothing of our
    E crosses.
  - (iii) A coupling's negative energy (GJW, Maldacena-Qi) holds the area with less positive energy. Checked against
    132's horizon (MQ-53: no horizon) and against 183 (the negative inside 273's region).
- **Y3. The channel.** Entanglement alone sends nothing (to READ). The README's N bits need a channel that can cross a
  horizon (143 A). *Test:* MSY's N_send <= g, with g at most the bits needed to open, against N bits through a passage
  of N ebits (137 (1)). The equality is the limiting case.
- **Y4. The closing.** When the N ebits are spent, the entanglement is zero and the throat pinches off (Van Raamsdonk).
  *Test:* does that closing meet 390 (closes when empty) without breaking 162 (fixed size through the write)? If the
  area falls as the ebits are spent, Y4 conflicts with 162 and gives way.
