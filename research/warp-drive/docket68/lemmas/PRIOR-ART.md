## Established (8)

- **B6′ (201)**, as a consistent model that can be realized and agrees with measurement:
  - On a positive-tension plane, gravity reduces to Einstein's at low energy (SMS).
  - A small mismatch between tension and bulk gives a late-time cosmological constant, the offset (Binétruy et al.).
  - One de Sitter plane carries no radion (Gen and Sasaki).
  - De Sitter planes in AdS₅ are exact, and they have thick versions (hep-th/0006069, hep-th/0701266).
  - ℓ stays below the table-top bound (Kapner et al.; Maartens–Koyama; Adelberger et al.), and the 1/r³ correction
    is confirmed (Duff and Liu).
- **101:** holes on the plane exist at every size, from five-dimensional Schwarzschild to large static ones (Kudoh
  et al.; Abdolrahimi et al.), and collapse forms them (Wang and Choptuik).
- **133:** one energy for each README. The hole's mass fixes its entropy.
- **158:** the write has a least time (Margolus–Levitin).
- **229:** a destination whose tension and offset are not negative lies within SMS's admissible planes.
- **336:** the hole radiates mainly onto the plane (Harris and Kanti, confirming Emparan, Horowitz and Myers).
- **386:** what crosses is information. A state is taken apart into classical information and entanglement and
  rebuilt (Bennett et al. 1993), and this has been demonstrated (Boschi et al.).
- **353:** general relativity is a controlled effective theory well below the Planck scale.

## Contrary: findings, recorded, not repaired (4)

- **RECON (437), "any position 2":**
  - Viable structure exists only over a bounded range of the constants (Adams 2019 and 2016; α_G/α < 10⁻³⁴).
  - RECON is provable inside the reconstructible region (433), not beyond it.
- **139 (1):** a negative-tension plane "is an anti-gravity world and hence should be excluded" (SMS). The theorem's
  destination already moved off it (226, 229).
- **427, "any tension":** for the same reason it holds for σ > 0, not for every σ.
- **122, censorship in the bulk:**
  - Five-dimensional black strings pinch off to naked singularities, generically (Lehner and Pretorius 2010–11,
    confirmed in 2022).
  - This bears on the O1b/O1c plan, which models the core on the warped black string, a string unstable in exactly
    that way (Gregory–Laflamme).

## Findings inside the in-part verdicts

- **131, the shortest exact binary form:**
  - The shortest form is Kolmogorov complexity, which is uncomputable.
  - ENCODE's fixed canonical code is computable, and the throat's size follows from it.
- **273, the bounce:**
  - The exterior is exact (Haggard and Rovelli), but the transition is quantum gravity.
  - Long time scales are "pathologically unstable" to accretion, while short ones are robust (Barceló et al.).
  - A semiclassical route exists in two-dimensional models (2608.03538).
  - A classical bounce with the energy conditions holding exists on a brane whose extra dimension is timelike
    (2610.04353), not Randall–Sundrum's.
- **183, the pair:** the averaged null condition is proved, but local null energy is unbounded below (Fewster and
  Roman).
- **100, everything entangled:**
  - Reeh–Schlieder establishes it for regions of a field.
  - For finitely many modes, entanglement "is not common at all".
- **390, closing:** closing a trapped region that null shells formed needs negative null energy out to infinity
  (1804.03912).
- **162, formation:**
  - A dust collapse on the plane cannot have a static exterior (Bruni, Germani and Maartens).
  - Pressure allows one (hep-th/0603254).
  - The numerical collapse settles to the static hole (Wang and Choptuik).
- **177:** a boundary coupling yields negative null energy in the bulk (Gao–Jafferis–Wall and its
  higher-dimensional form). Calling that an "appearance" is the reading's own word.
