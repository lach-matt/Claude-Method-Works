## Step 5: the walls, each as a question

**A. Formation.**
- **The wall.** No single shell can turn a place with no corridor into the corridor; it needs a change in the topology
  of space (LOOSE L6).
- **The sources.** Every source read says the same of every traversable wormhole, and none gives a mechanism
  (Maldacena–Milekhin p.14–15).
- **Yours.** Item 86 answer 3, *"likely made"*; item 101 answer 1.
- **Question: what makes the throat appear where there was none?** Is the corridor *made* (a change of topology), or
  *found* and widened (a throat already there)?

**B. The coupling (wall 9).**
- **What every corridor that can be crossed has needed, in what was read:**
  - a direct coupling between the two ends;
  - negative averaged null energy along the passage;
  - large entanglement of the one-to-one kind.
- **And what such a corridor carries:** no more information than was spent opening it (Gao–Jafferis–Wall;
  Maldacena–Stanford–Yang).
- **What the board computed.** The plane reads the passage's averaged null energy as negative (coin.py), and the bulk
  reads 0 (umbilic.py).
- **Yours.** Item 122: *"The rules apply, but do not restrict information"*.
- **Question: what couples the two positions?** And is the README exempt from the capacity bound, or bounded by it?

**C. The global bulk.**
- **The wall.** No five-dimensional spacetime is known with any static brane solution of this kind on its plane
  (Maartens–Koyama p.27, p.29).
- **The method.** Building one from the plane outward is not a well-posed problem. Bronnikov–Kim expect it to restrict
  the throat.
- **Yours.** Item 120, *"a complete bulk exists"*; item 121, to build it under closed-index criteria.
- **Question: do we build it numerically?** That is the only route the literature has used (Figueras–Wiseman), and it
  can confirm the corridor or refute it.

**D. Censorship.**
- **The wall.** Every censorship theorem read needs a boundary at infinity. Your plane is at finite distance, so none
  applies.
- **Yours.** Item 122, *"likely yes"*; items 117, 120, *"An NEC is never violated"*, it *"only ever appears to break"*.
- **Question: do we set out to prove a censorship theorem for a bulk bounded by your plane**, knowing it may fail?

**E. Stability.**

  are unstable (OUTSIDE.md).
