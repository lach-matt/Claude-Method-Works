## Step 5: the walls, each as a question

**A. Formation.**
- **What passes.** The mouth at position 1, an extremal horizon, is the kind of object known to form from ordinary
  collapse with no change of topology (Kehle–Unger).
- **What does not pass:**
  - Behind a collapse-formed horizon, the collapse decides what lies there. Nothing yet makes it the throat through to
    position 2.
  - A single shell cannot turn a place with no corridor into the eternal corridor.
  - Where the opening is a shell, its surface pressure is negative and diverges at the throat (LOOSE L6).
- **Yours.** Item 86 answer 3, *"likely made"*; item 101 answer 1.
- **Question: what forms the throat behind the mouth, through to position 2?** Is the negative pressure at the opening
  another place where the null condition only *appears* to break?

**B. The coupling (wall 9).**
- **What every corridor that can be crossed has needed, in what was read:**
  - a direct coupling between the two ends;
  - negative averaged null energy along the passage;
  - large entanglement of the one-to-one kind.
- **What such a corridor carries:** no more information than was spent opening it, as a parametric bound
  (Gao–Jafferis–Wall; Maldacena–Stanford–Yang).
- **What the board computed.** The plane reads the passage's averaged null energy as negative, and the bulk reads 0.
- **Yours.** Item 122: *"The rules apply, but do not restrict information"*.
- **Question: what couples the two positions?** And is the README exempt from the capacity bound, or bounded by it?

**C. The global bulk.**
- **The wall.** No five-dimensional spacetime is known with this corridor (the eq. 17 family) on its plane, as of the
  2010 review.
- **The method.** Building one from the plane outward is not a well-posed problem. Bronnikov–Kim expect it to restrict
  the throat.
- **Yours.** Item 120, *"a complete bulk exists"*; item 121, to build it under closed-index criteria.
- **Question: do we build it numerically?** That is the route the literature uses for brane black holes
  (Figueras–Wiseman), and it can confirm the corridor or refute it.

**D. Censorship.**
- **The wall.** Every censorship theorem read needs a boundary at infinity. Your plane is at finite distance. The
  nearest the literature comes is a remark that in 5D *"the topological censorship should work"* (Maldacena–Milekhin
  p.12).
- **What the board computed.** The corridor gets past topological censorship only because ANEC fails along the
  passage, as the plane reads it. The bulk reads 0. If the plane's reading is physical, the violation is real.
- **Yours.** Items 117, 120, 122: never violated, it only appears to be; a 5D censorship theorem, *"likely yes"*.
- **Question: do we set out to prove a censorship theorem for a bulk bounded by your plane**, one that would show the
  plane's violation is only a reading? It may fail.

**E. Stability.**

- **Yours.** Item 86 answer 5: the hold is *"incredibly short, maybe even immeasurable but not zero"*.
