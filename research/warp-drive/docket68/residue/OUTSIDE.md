### C8P-O3: the bulk scale k

### C8O-O9 and C8P-O5: stability

- **Extremal horizons carry the Aretakis instability for test fields.**
  - Aretakis, arXiv:1110.2007v1 p.10 Thm 5: *"For generic initial data … the quantity ψ² + (∂_rψ)² does not decay along
    H+."* That is a test scalar on extreme Reissner–Nordström, from the l = 0 conservation law (§12.1).
  - Aretakis, arXiv:1110.2009v1 p.2: higher derivatives *"blow up asymptotically along H+"*. Both concern extreme
    Reissner–Nordström.
- **Its premise is a double zero of g_vv at a regular null surface.** Milani, arXiv:2608.04461v1 p.14 (a 3D analogue,
  2026 preprint).
- **The r₀ = 2m member has that double zero.** In r, g_tt has a simple zero at 2m and g^rr a double one. In the regular
  null coordinate (dρ = √(g_tt·g_rr) dr), g_vv ∝ ρ² and κ = 0 (second reader's arithmetic; LOOSE L3 computes κ = 0).
  So the instability is expected but not proven for this metric.
- **Large white holes are unstable.**
  - Eardley, PRL 33 442 (1974), read from the abstract only.
  - Rovelli–Vidotto, arXiv:1805.03872v2 p.3: *"Large classical white holes are unstable."*
  - The mechanism is exponential blueshift at a non-degenerate horizon. No source treats a degenerate (κ = 0) white-hole
    horizon.
- **Rows stand.**
