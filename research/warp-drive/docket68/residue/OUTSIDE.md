## What passes

- **The one-way corridor is consistent with topological censorship.**
  - Friedman–Schleich–Witt, gr-qc/9305017v2 p.3, Theorem 1: *"If an asymptotically flat, globally hyperbolic spacetime
    … satisfies the averaged null energy condition, then every causal curve from J− to J+ is deformable to γ0 rel J."*
  - On a signal from a white hole, p.7–8: *"to passively detect the topology one must see a signal that originates in a
    white hole rather than J−"*.
  - So a black hole in and a white hole out (item 132) is the case the theorem leaves open. A two-way corridor is the
    case it forbids under ANEC (reader's inference, from the theorem as stated).
- **An instantaneous release of a finite energy is allowed by classical GR, in exactly the white-hole case.**
  - Cardoso–Ikeda–Moore–Yoo, arXiv:1803.03271v2, App. B p.9, on joining two Schwarzschild metrics on a null cone:
    *"The luminosity … is unbounded … not a counter-example … because the radiation comes out of a past horizon."*
  - The maximum power c⁵/4G is a conjecture, not a theorem. Barrow–Gibbons, arXiv:1408.1820v3 p.2: *"it was
    conjectured (Gibbons 2002)"*.
  - Its stated premise excludes past horizons. Cardoso et al. p.3: *"… free from past horizons is bounded above by the
    Planck luminosity."*
  - So C8O-O8 stands closed against item 94's instant on M's path, because the exit is a past (white-hole) horizon.
- **The corridor's infinite length at fixed time is known for extremal bridges.**
  - Maldacena–Susskind, arXiv:1306.0533v2 p.17: *"the Einstein-Rosen bridge of an extremal black hole has infinite
    length to begin with"*.
  - CURRENT.md X6 computed the same for r₀ = 2m.
- **The r₀ = 2m member is in the literature by name.**
  - Bronnikov–Konoplya, arXiv:1912.05315v1 p.4, on the same metric: *"In the intermediate case r0 = 2M we obtain a BH
    with a double horizon at r = 2M."*
  - Fig. 1 caption: *"The quasinormal ringing goes over into asymptotic power-law tails."* No growing mode is reported.
  - Its temperature vanishes. Lütfüoğlu, arXiv:2606.08351v1 p.2: *"The limiting surface a = 2M is cold."*

### C8P-O3: the bulk scale k

- **Bounds only; nothing fixes k.**
  - **The strongest with a confidence level:** ℓ = 1/k < 0.08 mm (95%) from the kinematic age of XTE J1118+480.
    Psaltis, astro-ph/0612611v1 abstract p.1; it rests on classical evaporation of brane black holes.
  - **Tighter, with no confidence level:** ℓ ≲ 1.3×10⁻² mm. Emparan–García-Bellido–Kaloper, hep-th/0212132v2 p.8
    eq. (14).
  - **Gravitational waves:** ℓ < 1.997 Mpc (95%) from GW170817. Visinelli–Bolis–Vagnozzi, arXiv:1711.06628v3 p.5.
  - **Brane tension:** λ > (1 TeV)⁴ and M₅ > 10⁵ TeV, from ℓ ≲ 0.1 mm. Maartens–Koyama p.11 eq. (42).
- **The relations, READ there:**
  - λ = 3M_p²/(4πℓ²) (eq. 25);
  - M₅³ = M_p²/ℓ (eq. 27);
  - κ₄² = λκ₅⁴/6 (eq. 70).
- **The torsion-balance limit is not an ℓ bound.** Lee et al., arXiv:2002.11761v1 p.5, give 38.6 μm for a Yukawa
  force at α = 1. The RS2 deviation is a power law instead, 1 + 2ℓ²/3r² (Maartens–Koyama eq. 41), so 38.6 μm is not ℓ.
