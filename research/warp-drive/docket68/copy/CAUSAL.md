## What follows the work

- **1. One shared "now" makes your teleportation causally safe, however often it is repeated.**
  - Suppose every teleport is instantaneous in *one* frame for the whole dimension (the board's reading R-SHARED).
  - Then no chain of teleports can return before it left. frame.py's reply keyed to that frame arrives at t = 0,
    never earlier.
  - Every network of corridors keyed to one frame is free of closed loops: 0 of 200 random networks, in exact
    arithmetic.
  - So your H-CORRIDOR-REPEATABLE (item 90) is safe on this reading.
  - This is the published resolution. Paradoxes need that one can send a signal backward in time in *any* frame, and
    "there can be no paradox if, in one particular reference frame, tachyons can only propagate forward in time"
    (Liberati, Sonego and Visser, p.12).
- **2. The board already holds a candidate for that frame:** the cosmic rest frame, against which the Sun moves at
  369.82 km/s (frame.py, from Planck 2018). Your charter's own hypothesis H-FRAME, "a preferred frame exists", is
  exactly what R-SHARED needs.
- **3. What "all observers" costs.**
  - For two places apart, "instantaneous" can hold exactly in one frame only. This is the relativity of simultaneity:
    for such a pair, there is always a frame where they are simultaneous and others where their order reverses (LSV
    p.11).
  - Observers moving relative to the shared frame read the departure and the arrival as apart.
  - For the Sun's frame, over the board's Proxima span, they are 1.65×10⁵ s apart (1.9 days); over Earth's year,
    1.52×10⁵ to 1.78×10⁵ s.
  - On R-SHARED those observers disagree about the timing, but never see a loop.

## What is ruled out, as the boundary

- **R-OBJECT** is the reading the board cannot make safe: each teleport instantaneous in its *own* object's frame.
  - Two objects in relative motion, each teleporting "instantaneously relative to" itself, close a loop. frame.py's
    reply keyed to the sender's frame arrives before the first message left: t = −3/5 at 3/5 c.
  - That happens at *any* nonzero relative speed (computed down to 10⁻⁶ c).
  - Over Proxima at the Sun's speed against the cosmic frame, the return would arrive 1.65×10⁵ s before it was sent.
  - This is the "tachyonic anti-telephone" (LSV p.12). Whether chronology protection forbids it is OPEN.
- **Contrast:** one corridor keyed to a different frame, set beside shared-frame ones, closes the loop again
  (frame.py's witness).
