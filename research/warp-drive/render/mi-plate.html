The Master Index

Nine formal indexes, sorted by which of five mathematical languages close them — charted on coordinates that survive their own admissibility test.

instrument mi.py indexes 9 distinct cells 8 chart (K, height, width)

This index inherits nothing from the one it replaces. Its predecessor was written on five coordinates, and two of them — arity and density — fail the criterion for a legitimate chart: append a coordinate that carries no information and both move, on nine indexes out of nine. The three coordinates here move on none.

01

Five languages, and how they rank

A language closes an index when every cell its operator generates is already in the index. The five are closure operators on a finite box, and they are not independent: seven containments hold everywhere, thirteen break somewhere.

03

The channel lattice

A set of closing languages is not arbitrary. It must be a down-set of the language poset — if a language closes, everything below it closes — which leaves exactly eight lawful positions out of the thirty-two subsets.

04

Why the quadrants are uneven

Turning a switch ON forces everything beneath it on as well, because a channel is a down-set. The order/algebra block sits above both minimal languages — statistics and information — so switching it on drags both in and leaves nothing free to vary. That is why its quadrant holds exactly one channel.

Geometry sits above statistics only. Information stays free, so its quadrant holds two. And with both switches off, both minimal languages are free and the quadrant holds four. One, two, four — the count of each quadrant is 2(minimal languages left free), and nothing about it is an accident of this corpus.

What K6 is, stated carefully

K6 is join-irreducible and meet-irreducible, a co-atom, and the sole occupant of its phase. It is also vacant — no seated index closes in exactly {order, algebra, information, statistics}. Its isolation is a theorem about the lattice; its emptiness is a fact about what has been seated so far. The two must not be read as one finding.

The self-membership question, answered and then narrowed

The master index sits at (2, 5, 2) and no index occupies that cell, so it is not a member of itself. That reads like a structural fact and it is not: self-membership was measured across eight different charts and holds in one of them — not this one. It is chart-dependent and rare, which is a weaker and truer statement than the one this tree first published.

06

Which demands are worth trusting

An index that does not close demands the cells it lacks. Most such demands are noise. There is now exactly one calibration for telling them apart, and it was checked against an independent ground truth nine times.

The Janet chart, one element short of a shell boundary, has all five languages demanding and their intersection is exactly one cell. That cell names the next element — and it does so correctly at every boundary tested, including Z = 170 and Z = 220, far beyond anything observed. The one demand ever found wrong scored three languages and thirty-six cells. So:

The signature of a trustworthy demand

All five languages demand, and their intersection is exactly one cell.

Nothing here is seated on the strength of a demand. These three pass the only calibration this tree has, which is a reason to test them and not a reason to believe them. The cell that three separate constructions once chased was never a calibrated demand — and now there is a standard by which to say so.

07

What this index refuses to conclude

The refusals are load-bearing. Each one marks a place where the construction could produce a number and the number would not mean what it appears to.

  • That a vacant channel is a gap to be filled. Five of the eight lawful positions hold nothing. That is a fact about what has been seated, not a prediction that something belongs there.
  • That K6's isolation and K6's emptiness are one finding. The first is a theorem about the lattice; the second is a census. They happen to be true of the same node.
  • That the withdrawn chart's exit explains anything. Withdrawing periodic layout 2-D made a universal claim true by removing its only counterexample. Removing a counterexample is not explaining it.
  • That a five-coordinate cell quoted elsewhere is wrong. Two of those five coordinates fail the admissibility test, so those readings are statements about a chart. They stand as records of what was measured; they are not re-derived here.
  • That the index ranks the languages. Geometry and the order/algebra block are incomparable. There is no total ranking, and the lattice is where that stops being an assertion.
