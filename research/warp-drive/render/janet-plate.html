The second and third differ in exactly one coordinate

ℓ and k are identical between them. Only the base changes — from the shell an electron occupies to the shell-plus-subshell that orders the filling. This instrument builds its index by re-charting the other's, in one comprehension, so the two perspectives cannot drift apart. And it reproduces a seated index exactly: janet() == mi.janet(), cell for cell. Nothing new is seated here — a seated object is read as a bundle.

03

It never transitions — and that was predicted

The shell fibration's sliding scale drops from K7 to K3 at the first shrinking shell. Sweep this one and there is no drop at all.

The law forbade it, and the law was fitted elsewhere

The shell fibration's law is that the K7 → K3 transition lands on the first shrinking shell — verified at nine reaches there. The Janet fibres never shrink. So the law says this sweep can have no transition, and it has none.

Stated at its true strength. This is the only cross-prediction in this tree made from a law and then checked on an index the law was not fitted to. It is weaker than it sounds — a law fitted on one family and confirmed on a close relative is corroboration, not independent test — and it is stronger than nothing, because the law could have failed and did not.

What is measured: K6 is reachable

That was open before, and it is now closed. The chart holds K6 at every complete shell reach from n+ℓ ≤ 3 through n+ℓ ≤ 11 — nine consecutive reaches across an order of magnitude. Only the two degenerate reaches give K7.

What is not measured: that it deserves a seat

The K6 chart is a coarsening, and that is the price. It maps 170 elements onto 82 cells — 88 elements share an address with another, and the chart cannot say which element is which. Every one of the nine seated indexes, and both fibrations, are injective on their own members. A non-injective chart of 170 elements is a weaker object than any of them.

No criterion in this tree governs dropping a coordinate — the chart criterion asks whether a coordinate survives an appended redundant one, which is a different operation. The nearest precedent cuts against seating: the re-charting census treats coordinate drops as showing a demand is chart-relative, not as producing new members. K6 is reachable and unseated. Whether it deserves a seat is a ruling, not a measurement.

What the three agree on, and what they don't

Agree: the seven lawful containments hold everywhere — on every index and every fibre of both bundles, zero broken. Dilworth holds on all of them. Every fibre of both bundles closes in all five languages.

Disagree: the channel, and only the channel.

07

What this refuses to conclude

  • That the Janet perspective is the right one because it reaches K7. Closing in more languages is not being more true. K7 means every operator is satisfied, which a complete rectangle achieves trivially — the shell fibration's K3 is a stronger statement about structure, not a weaker one.
  • That K6 is now occupied. A projection is not a seated index, and this one throws away which element is which for 88 of 170.
  • That the base is the only thing that matters. It is the only thing that differs here, between two charts sharing ℓ and k. Nothing measured says a third base would not behave differently again — and one is obvious and untried: ℓ itself.
