Two candidates were folded into one, and that is the point

A Russell–Saunders term index and a Landé interval-rule index were proposed separately over the same rows. A Landé triple lies inside exactly one term — every triple needs an L, an S and a J to exist at all — so the map from triples to terms is total and many-to-one. Seating both would put one body of rows on two vertices at two different cells, which is the case overlap.py's own section 0 names as the dangerous one.

Neither precedent licenses it. periodic layout 2-D was withdrawn for being a strict projection over the same members; madelung stayed because it is a strict coarsening over the same members. Landé is neither — it is a refinement of the member granularity, which multiplies the box instead of coarsening it. So there is one index, and the interval is banked per triple in the ledger.

So the verdict is the measurement's, not the author's

A coordinate whose alphabet is a function of a number nobody measured is not an axis. interval drops to the ledger, status MEASURED, and the index seats on (mult, L, parity, completeness) at 112 cells, box 270, cell (0, 13, 18) — a chart that is tolerance-independent at every tolerance tried.

What is refused at the door, and named

Bracketed jK, jj and Racah labels are refused, not coerced — a label like 2[5/2]* is not an LS term and has no L or S to test; forcing one would invent the measurement. 4,033 rows carry such a label. Four files are refused for an unreadable species and are named: BaLII.tsv, LEVELS-Fe-I.tsv, LEVELS-K-I.tsv, LEVELS-Kr-I.tsv. One file is skipped as a byte-identical duplicate: recovered/LuI__16455659.tsv.

On LuI, whose data rows are byte-identical to the corpus's own quarantine/LuI.FABRICATED.tsv: it is read anyway, because the label is unsupported. An independent capture under register 1639 agrees exactly on all 199 of its (config, term, J) rows — zero disagreements, a strict subset. 199 rows are corroborated, 35 undecided, and the file is neither trusted whole nor discarded whole. Nothing is repaired, moved or relabelled.

194 files were found where the candidate's own list held 43. The other 151 differ from those 43 only in whether their header is capitalised — which is a filing fact, not a fact about the data, and selecting by a file list would have lost them.

It does NOT test Russell–Saunders coupling, and the measurement says so

MIXED — a banked J outside the multiplet the term symbol implies — occurs on exactly ONE member of 5,132, the Al I 3s.3p.(3P*).6p 2S row, which banks J = 3/2 against a predicted {1/2}. So what completeness measures is the agreement between a banked label and its own banked J values: a statement about the table, not about whether nature couples that way. Calling it a coupling test is the claim the numbers refuse. The row is charted and left alone.
