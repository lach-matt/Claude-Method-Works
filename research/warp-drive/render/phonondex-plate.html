01

A member is a site-symmetry type, not a material

The member set is (space group, site-symmetry type up to conjugacy), and that is what makes the fetch disappear.

A material’s Γ-point phonon content is the sum over its occupied site orbits, and each orbit’s contribution depends only on its site-symmetry type. So the member set is not materials at all: it is the generating table from which every material’s answer follows by addition. It is finite, exhaustive, and needs no database. compose() performs the addition and the instrument checks it against six published crystal decompositions.

It is also deliberately coarser than the Wyckoff tables, and the coarsening is a finding rather than a shortcut. ITA lists P‑1’s eight inversion centres as eight Wyckoff letters, 1a…1h; all eight have the same site-symmetry group and contribute identically, so separating them would multiply members without adding a distinction. Measured: space group 2 seats 2 members in all, of site-symmetry orders 2, 1. The phonon representation cannot see the difference.

The guards, and the bugs they caught

Every number here is computed — no textbook character table and no Wyckoff table is read — so the guards are arithmetic, not inspection.

guardon every one of the 1,120 rows
modes = 3 × multiplicity, and the decomposition sums to itholds
|site symmetry| × |orbit| = |point group|holds
A wrong character table does not give a wrong-looking answer; it gives a fractional multiplicity, and that cannot be rationalised away. The 4 KINDS of development bug below were all found that way, and none by reading.
  • non-symmorphy: N_fixed from the rotation alone, ignoring t
  • a transposed class-algebra matrix and a non-identity first class
  • fixed points confused with orbit membership
  • a character-table cache keyed on the sorted rotation set
What this index refuses

It claims nothing about k ≠ Γ, where the little group’s representations are projective for non-symmorphic groups — a different computation, named as open rather than quietly omitted. It claims no frequencies: a frequency is a measurement and the symmetry content is complete without one. And it does not call its members materials — materials are sums of members. The first of those three refusals is discharged by kpointdex, the 26th row.

Where the data comes from

COMPUTED end to end from the 230 crystallographic space groups: spglib supplies the operations, they are transformed to the primitive basis, and the character tables come from Burnside's class-algebra method. No textbook character table and no Wyckoff table is read. The derivation is banked beside the capture and re-runnable with --derive.
