The construction is already paid for

All six carry a cell on the same admissible chart — (K, height, width) — because the chart criterion fixed it for all of them. So nothing is chosen: the hexad is just the set of those six cells.

An exact correspondence, not an approximation

Information's operator is join-closure. So the cells it demands are precisely the joins that escape — five escaping threads, deficit of exactly five, and the join-closure adds exactly those five cells and no others. The diagonals that leave the polygon are what the index fails to know.

 MI is in three of the five. The index of indexes is the vertex hardest to join with the rest — pair it with the refusal index, with provenance, or with the ladder and the thread leaves every time; pair it with either fibration and it lands. Nothing else is in more than two.
And the meets are lopsided

Five meets escape as well, but they land on only three distinct cells: the three K0 vertices all meet MI at the same place, (0, 5, 2). Meet-closure adds three where join-closure adds five. A hexagon drawn on paper would hide that asymmetry.

04

Seat the hexad in itself and it converges

The refusal index could not be seated — recomputing it over an inventory containing itself cycles, period two, on its faithful chart. The hexad takes the same test.

Fixed point in one step, and it contains itself

Add the hexad's own cell as a seventh member and recompute: step 1 gives 7 cells, K2, own cell (2, 4, 2); step 2 is unchanged. The seventh member is that cell, and adding it changes nothing.

So self-reference is not uniformly fatal here, and that is the finding. Two objects took the same test and gave opposite answers. The refusal index cycles because R is recomputed from the inventory — adding a member changes every other member's value. The hexad's members are cells, already computed, so a seventh disturbs nothing; only the hexad's own cell could move, and it does not.

Stated at its true strength

This is a verified fixed point, not a proof that convergence is forced. The difference between the two maps — one monotone in its input, one not — is offered as the explanation, not as a theorem.

05

What this refuses

  • To call the heptad a seventh master index. Its seventh member is a bookkeeping entry — the hexad's own cell — not a new object with its own members. The count of master indexes is six.
  • To read this convergence as licence to seat the refusal index. Different maps, different answers; that cycle stands exactly as reported.
  • To draw the hexad as a regular hexagon. A tidy hexagon would be a picture of the word "six", not of the index.
