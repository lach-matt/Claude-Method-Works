### The roots of L̂_b(0) that come from □^C, and the parameter exponents (proof file P-I11)

This file repairs the parameter exponents of [[ch:s22]]. The proof of [[lem:iii-i11-2-1]] listed, below the real part
3, only the root 2 among the indicial roots of L̂_b(0) that the zero energy solves meet. The list of
[[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] contains after its semicolons also the roots ι^C_(·,+) − 1 that come from □^C; they have real part
above 1, at every γ_C ≥ 10⁶ at least four of them lie in (1, 2), they enter û_0 with coefficients that are not zero in
general, and their u-exponents μ + 1 are not in the set Λ of [[def:iii-i10-1-1]] as it stands (notes/reports/V-I11.md,
confirmed in notes/reviews/R-V-I11.md). The change adds the generator 1 + 𝒵 to Λ, 𝒵 the set of the indicial roots of
L̂_b(0) with 0 < Re < 3, adds [[lem:s22-c-roots]], which locates four of these roots, and rewrites the sentences that
used the omitted roots. Section 3 (the placement) is chapter text; the other sections are notes for merging and are not
chapter text. Section 4 gives, for every site of Λ, Λ_*, ℰ_par, 𝒩, ℰ_♯, E′ and the Λ-valued claim, the reason why the
statement there holds after the change, and the complete argument for each result that the error reached.

#### 1. The change, and where it departs from the drafts of V-I11 §4

*The generator.* 𝒵 := {μ an indicial root of L̂_b(0) at ∂X : 0 < Re μ < 3}, and Λ gains the generator 1 + 𝒵 (item 1).
By [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] (a) and its list (the proof of item 7), 𝒵 consists of the root 2 of type s0 and the roots
ι^C_(·,+) − 1 with real part below 3 at the types s0, s1, sl and vl, l ≥ 2. The real part 3 is the bound of every zero
energy solve: [[lem:iii-i11-2-1]] has q′ ≤ 5, so the roots it meets have real part in (q − 2, q′ − 2) ⊂ (0, 3). Taking
all of (0, 3), and not only the roots met at level 0 (real part below 2 + ε_+ − ε), keeps Λ independent of ε_+, as (G⁺)
and (Ex1) need: they decrease ε_+ after Λ is fixed.

#### 2. The enlarged Λ: finiteness, conjugation, independence, and the conditions that quantify over it

(i) *𝒵 is finite.* The indicial family of L̂_b(0) at ∂X has the form of [[lem:iii-f1-2-4]] (each matrix is −ι² Id
plus lower powers of ι, [[sec:iii-f1-2]]). That lemma, with the strip |Re ι| ≤ 3, makes it invertible on W_π for all
but finitely many irreducible π, and on each of these finitely many W_π its determinant is a polynomial of degree
2 dim W_π with leading coefficient ±1; so the strip contains finitely many roots. Hence 1 + 𝒵 is finite, and with
[[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (a) (item 4) Λ ∩ {Re ≤ R} is finite for every R.

(ii) *Conjugation.* L_b is real, so the indicial family satisfies N(ῑ) = conj N(ι), and 𝒵 is closed under conjugation.
Λ is defined as the smallest set closed under conjugation that contains its generators, so nothing changes there.

(iii) *Independence.* 𝒵 is determined by the indicial roots of L̂_b(0), that is by the parameters of [[sec:iii-f1-1]]
(γ_Υ, e_Υ, z_Υ and v_C, e_C, γ_C), which [[thm:vi-c-2-1]] fixes in its steps (1)–(2); it does not depend on b ((P2) of
[[lem:iii-i15-1-1]]), on ε_K, ε_+, η_1, η_2, α, ε, on the class 𝒳 (ℰ_+, a_+, a_K) or on the data class (ℰ_0, ℓ_0,
ℓ_I and ℰ^C_I). So Λ is fixed, as before, once ℰ^C_I is fixed in step (3) of [[thm:vi-c-2-1]], before the constants of
step (4); and Λ, ℰ_par, 𝒩, ℰ_♯ and ℰ^♮ do not depend on b ([[sec:s22-setting]], item (iii) of the proof of
[[thm:iii-i15-3-1]]).

(iv) *Below 2 nothing moves.* Re μ > 1 for μ ∈ 𝒵 ([[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] (a)), so every element of Λ that involves
1 + 𝒵 has real part above 2. Hence Λ ∩ {Re ≤ 2}, π_1ℳ = Λ ∩ {Re < 2}, ℰ_♯ ∩ {Re ≤ 2}, ℳ_rot, 𝒩_ι, the motions
ℳ_(α′), the rows with Re λ ≤ 2 and ℰ⁰_src (whose row part is ℰ_rows ∩ {Re < 2}) are the same sets, and so are 𝒴⁰(β),
𝒴^♮(β) below the real part 2 and every admissibility condition of [[sec:iii-i8-1]] (levels below 3).

(v) *(G).* Λ ∩ {2 < Re ≤ 4} is finite, so δ_G := min({Re λ − 2 : λ ∈ Λ, 2 < Re λ ≤ 3} ∪ {Re λ − 3 : λ ∈ Λ,
3 < Re λ ≤ 4}) is positive, and the first two windows of (G) miss Λ for ε_K < δ_G. The third window: if
Re(λ + d_c) ∈ (2, 2 + ε_K] with λ ∈ Λ and d_c ∈ {d_c0, d_c1}, then Re λ ≤ 2 + ε_K − d_c < 2 once d_c > ε_K, and
λ ∈ π_1ℳ, a set that does not move by (iv); and d_c > ε_K follows from (G) at λ = 2 as before. So
[[def:iii-i10-1-3]] keeps its sentence "This is possible by [[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (a), as Λ does not depend on ε_K", and
ε_K < ε_Υ is unaffected. The only integer in 𝒵 is 2 (the integer entries of the list below 3), and 1 + 2 = 3; a
root μ ∈ 𝒵 with |μ| ≤ 50 other than 2 has d(Re μ, ℤ) ≥ ε_ind > ε_K ([[eq:iii-f1-2-2]]), so 1 + μ meets neither of the
first two windows; the roots with |μ| > 50, which [[eq:iii-f1-2-2]] does not control, are finitely many in the strip
and are covered by δ_G.

(vi) *The admissible α* ([[def:iii-i10-1-3]]). The exclusions over λ_0 ∈ Λ with Re λ_0 < 2 are unchanged by (iv). The
exclusion set of [[sec:iii-i11-1]] asks the conditions [[eq:iii-g4-4-2]] of [[prop:iii-g4-4-4]] at the finitely many
α_0 treated at levels 1 and 2 (their u-exponents lie in Λ ∩ {Re < 3 + η_2}, finite) and of [[prop:iii-g4-5-1]] at level
3. At every α_0 treated the conditions other than the root condition hold, whatever α_0 is: at level 1,
Re α_0 ∈ [2, 2 + η_1) ⊂ (1, 2 + ε_ind) and ℓ_zf = α − 2 − ε ∈ (2, 2 + ε_K), so ℓ_zf − Re α_0 + 2 > 2 − η_1 > 0,
ℓ_zf − min(Re α_0 − 2, 0) = ℓ_zf < 3 and ℓ_zf ∉ ℕ₀; at level 2, Re α_0 ∈ [1 + η_1, 2 + η_2) ⊂ (1, 2 + ε_ind) and
ℓ_zf = α − 3 − ε ∈ (1, 1 + ε_K), so ℓ_zf − Re α_0 + 2 > 1 − η_2 > 0, ℓ_zf − min(Re α_0 − 2, 0) < 2 + ε_K − η_1 < 3 and
ℓ_zf ∉ ℕ₀. The root condition ℓ_zf − Re α_0 + 2 ≠ −μ excludes, for each of the finitely many α_0, the finitely many ε′
at which it fails (it can fail only for a real root μ ∈ (−3, 0); these are finitely many by (f) of
[[prop:iii-f1-2-3]]). So the exclusion set stays finite
and the admissible α stay dense in (4 + ε_K − ε′_0, 4 + ε_K]. No condition on d(Re α_0, ℤ) enters, so the roots of 𝒵
with |μ| > 50 need none.

(vii) *η_1, η_2, δ_+.* [[sec:iii-i11-1]] takes η_1 < η_2 in (ε_K, ε_+), η_1 < ε_g, with no element of Λ of real part
2 + η_1 or 3 + η_2: finitely many values are excluded, by (i). [[sec:iii-i12-1]] then takes η_2 ∈ (ε_+ − δ_+, ε_+)
off that finite set.

(viii) *(G⁺) and (Ex1).* ε_+ < min{Re λ − 3 : λ ∈ Λ, 3 < Re λ ≤ 4}, a positive number by (i), independent of ε_+ by
(iii); [[def:iii-j-g-plus]]'s "Λ does not depend on the constants" and [[def:iii-j3-1-3]]'s "a smallness condition"
hold as written. The roots in 𝒵 with real part in (2, 3) (for instance the vector type roots ι^C_(vl,+) − 1) give
elements 1 + μ of Λ in (3, 4) and so enter this minimum.

(ix) *(A_J), (Ex2)–(Ex4), (A_K).* (A_J) quantifies over λ₀ ∈ Λ with Re λ₀ < 3 + ε_+, a finite set by (i); each λ₀
excludes the α with α − Re λ₀ ∈ {−Re μ} ∪ Re π_1ℰ_K, a discrete set; so the admissible α meeting (A_J) stay dense
([[def:iii-j-a-j]]). (Ex3)–(Ex4) and (A_K) exclude finitely many α or ε_K, as [[lem:iii-j3-1-4]] says: its proof uses
"Λ and the tf-exponents α_0 of levels 1–2, Λ-valued below 3 and finitely many", which (i) and F1 of Section 4 give.

(x) *The admissible levels* ([[def:iii-i13-1-1]]). The real parts of the rates of the triples (τ, λ, k), λ ∈ Λ, form a
discrete set by (i); (a)–(c) exclude finitely many β in every bounded interval; the levels stay dense; β ≠ 4 by (a);
every admissible α ≥ 3 of [[def:iii-i10-1-3]] is admissible there, by (G) as written (a rate in
(2 + ε_K − ε′_0, 2 + ε_K] would be Re λ − 1, Re λ or Re λ + d_c in that interval, against the three windows).

(xi) *The numbers of [[def:iii-i10-1-1]].* η_0 and J_* do not change (the increments do not). l_* bounds, at real part
at most 5, the multiplicities of the indicial roots of L̂_b(0), which includes the roots of 𝒵. k̄_0 bounds
ℰ⁰_src ∪ ℰ_++ ∪ 𝒢 ∪ (2 + ℰ_col) below 2λ_♯, sets that do not involve 𝒵. n(λ) and k̄(λ) are those of the enlarged Λ.

(xii) *The class.* (H3) for the new rows, (λ, k) + ℱ ⊂ ℰ_+, and ℰ_+ ⊃ (ℰ_♯ ∪ ℰ^♮) ∩ {Re < 3 + ε_+} of
[[sec:iii-i14-1]] are conditions on ℰ_+ alone, imposed with (H1), by [[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (b) (item 5); the least
ℰ_src of [[eq:iii-i9-1-3]] and 𝒴_1(α), 𝒴_2(α) grow with ℰ_rows, while 𝒴⁰(α) does not change, by (iv).

#### 4. Why every statement holds after the change

The proofs in the chapters use the omitted roots through four facts, which the change makes true.

*F1 ([[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (c)).* In the constructions of [[sec:iii-i10-3]] and [[sec:iii-i11-3]], applied to sources in
𝒴⁰(α) (and, by [[lem:iii-i13-1-3]] (b), in 𝒴^♮(β)), every exponent (λ, k) produced with 2 ≤ Re λ ≤ 3 + ε_+ has λ ∈ Λ
and k ≤ k̄(λ). Proof: the operations are those of (c) (item 3). Each operation other than the indicial roots maps Λ into
Λ, as before. The zero energy solve of level j ([[lem:iii-i11-2-1]] with q_j − 2 ∈ (0, 1) and q′ − 2 at most
2 + ε_+ − ε) meets roots with real part in (q_j − 2, q′ − 2) ⊂ (0, 3), which lie in 𝒵 and give the u-exponents
μ + j + 1 ∈ (1 + 𝒵) + j ⊂ Λ. Concretely: level 0 (window (1 − ε, 2 + ε_+ − ε)) meets the root 2 and the roots
ι^C_(·,+) − 1 with real part below 2 + ε_+ − ε, among them the four of [[lem:s22-c-roots]]; level 1 (window
(η_1, 1 + ε_+ − ε)) meets only roots with real part in (1, 1 + ε_+ − ε), which by [[eq:iii-f1-2-2]] and ε_+ < ε_ind
have |μ| > 50, and which give μ + 2 = (1 + μ) + 1 ∈ Λ; levels 2 and 3 (windows in (0, 1)) meet none
([[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] (a)). The ℕ₀-shifts of [[lem:iii-a-1-4]] add (1, 0). The logarithmic orders: item 6, the
induction on n(λ) with the roots counted among the raises at their exponents, at most J_* per exponent as the text
counts (a root met belongs to one Taylor level).

*F2 ([[lem:iii-i11-2-1]]).* The u-exponents of E′ are Λ-valued when those of the forcing are (item 7).

*F3 (Step 6 of the proof of [[prop:iii-i11-3-1]]).* Let μ ∈ 𝒵 be met at level 0. Step 1 and [[lem:iii-i11-2-2]] give
g_1 the tf-exponent μ + 1 (from E′ + 1). If Re μ < 1 + η_1, level 1 treats it by [[prop:iii-g4-4-4]] at α_0 = μ + 1
(Section 2 (vi): its hypotheses hold), and the s1 tower there has the rate α_0 − 1 = μ and the u-exponent 1 + μ. If
Re μ ≥ 1 + η_1, the zero energy solve of level 1 gives û_1 the exponent μ − 1 and [[lem:iii-i11-2-2]] gives g_2 the
tf-exponent μ (from E − 1 and E′ + 1); level 2 treats it if Re μ < 2 + η_2, and its s1 tower has the rate α_0 = μ and
the u-exponent α_0 + 1 = 1 + μ. In both cases 1 + μ ∈ 1 + 𝒵 ⊂ Λ, 2 < Re(1 + μ), and the logarithmic order k of the
tower is at most k̄(1 + μ) by F1. By (G), Re(1 + μ) ∉ (3, 3 + ε_K], so the rate Re μ is below α − 2 exactly when
Re(1 + μ) ≤ 3; then (1 + μ, k) ∈ ℰ_par ([[eq:iii-i10-1-2]]: λ = 1 + μ ∈ Λ, 2 ≤ Re λ ≤ 3, k ≤ k̄(λ)), the row

α_0 + ℰ_ind of the rounds of level 2 (u-exponents in Λ + ℰ_ind ⊂ Λ). Item 9 writes this into Step 6. Before the change,
1 + μ ∉ Λ for the roots μ ≠ 2, and Step 6's "with (λ, k) ∈ ℰ_par by [[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (c)" had no proof for these
towers (R-V-I11 §2, the paragraph on [[thm:iii-i11-4-1]]).
