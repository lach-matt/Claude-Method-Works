These tables belong to [[prop:stage-bounds-higher]], [[lem:envelope-lines]] and [[lem:profile-complex]]. An entry
written d.ddde±kk denotes the decimal number d.ddd × 10^(±kk). Every entry is an upper bound, rounded up to four
significant digits, for the quantity named in the heading of its table; and each entry is obtained from entries
printed earlier in these tables, or from the numbers named in the text, by the operation the heading states,
the result being rounded up. Throughout, ā := 3.70565, which exceeds 1/a by [[lem:lambda0-point]] (a).
