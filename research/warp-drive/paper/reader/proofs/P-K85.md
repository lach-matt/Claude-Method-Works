**Lemma {#lem:edge-localisation}** (Localisation at every depth.) Let env be positive and log-concave on (v_s, v_s +
x0], with κ := (log env)′ > 0, |κ′| ≤ c_R κ^(1+α) for some α ∈ (0, 1), (H_D), and (B_(c′)) with c′ := max(c, M), c the
cost of [[lem:edge-degree-two]], there. There are λ_D, x_D > 0, given in the proof, such that for λ ≤ λ_D the bounds of
[[lem:edge-degree-two]] hold with a reach ρ_v ≤ 1/κ(v) at every boundary point X with x = v − v_s ≤ x_D at which
ε_M(v + 1/κ(v)) ≤ λ⁵, and at those points

    |r2(X)| ≤ C κ(v)^(2c′) env(v)².                                                                  {#eq:edge-localised}

*Proof.* Put N_* := N − 5 in case Ω and N_* := N_P − 3 in case P, so that [[lem:layer-remainder]], resp.
[[lem:layer-remainder-past]], bounds w by C λ^(N_*) ε_M(v1). Let ψ(τ) := τ − τ0 in case Ω and ψ be the function of
[[lem:layer-remainder-past]] in case P, ψ_max := 2 + τ_end − τ0 ≥ ψ, and e_0 := 2C1λ, resp. e_0 := 2C_Pλ. Let ϖ := t^(3/2)
on P and ϖ := 1 on Ω, so that ψ′ = 1/ϖ. For a covector α let |α|_e be the Euclidean norm of its Cartesian components,
and for a symmetric form B let ‖B‖ be its norm as a bilinear form for |·|_e. For V ≤ v_s + 1 and e > 0 put

    Δ(V) := sup_(X ∈ S(V)) ϖ(X) ‖(g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹)(X)‖,      R(V, e) := S(V) ∩ {v + e ψ(τ) ≤ V}.

By [[eq:eq-c1]], resp. the choice of C_P in [[lem:layer-remainder-past]], 2Δ(V) ≤ e_0 for every V.

(1) *A tilted remainder estimate.* Let V ≤ v_♯, ε_M(V) ≤ λ³ and 2Δ(V) ≤ e ≤ e_0. Then the bound E_2(τ) ≤ C_w λ^(N_*)
ε_M(V) of step 6 of the proof of [[lem:layer-remainder]] (case Ω), resp. of the proof of [[lem:layer-remainder-past]]
(case P), holds on R(V, e) in place of the region there, for every τ, with the constant C_w of those proofs and the norms
taken on R(V, e) ∩ Σ̂_τ. In case P also

    E^Ω(τ) := Σ_(i+|β|≤2) (‖∂ ∂^i Ω^β w(τ)‖ + ‖∂^i Ω^β w(τ)‖) ≤ C_w λ^(N_*) ε_M(V),

C_w enlarged once. If ε_M(V) ≤ λ⁵, the form with four derivatives of step (2) of the proof of [[lem:edge-degree-two]]
holds on R(V, e) as well.

Indeed, the face {v + eψ(τ) = V} has the conormal α := dv + eψ′ dτ, with g0⁻¹(α, α) = −2eψ′ − e²ψ′²(1 + μ/r²)
([[prop:vaidya]] (d)) and |α|_e² = (1 + eψ′)² + 1, as α = (1 + eψ′) dτ + x̂·dx. Since eψ′ ≤ e_0 ≤ 1 and
|(g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹)(α, α)| ≤ Δ(V) ψ′ |α|_e² ≤ (e/2) ψ′ |α|_e²,

    g_λ⁻¹(α, α) ≤ ψ′ (−2e − e²ψ′(1 + μ/r²) + e + e²ψ′ + e³ψ′²/2) = e ψ′ (−1 − eψ′ μ/r² + e²ψ′²/2) ≤ −eψ′/2 < 0.

So α is timelike for g_λ, and α(Y) = 1 + eψ′(1 + μ/r²) > 0 for Y := −g0⁻¹ dτ, which is future timelike for g_λ
([[sec:eq-setting]]); hence v + eψ increases along future causal curves of g_λ and R(V, e) lies to the past of its face.
The slices of R(V, e) are {r ≤ ρ(τ)} ∩ S(V), ρ(τ) := V − eψ(τ) − τ, and ρ decreases. R(V, e) ⊂ {v ≤ V} does not meet the
outer face of Ω, where v ≥ V + 1, nor the outer boundary of P, where v = V_P − h_P(τ) > V + 1, and R(V, e) ∩ Σ_τ0 is the
same set as for the tilt e_0, since ψ(τ0) = 0 on Ω. The proofs of [[lem:layer-remainder]] and
[[lem:layer-remainder-past]] use their tilted face only through its flux, which is nonnegative and is dropped, their
slices only through the decrease of ρ, and their hypothesis ε_M(v1) ≤ λ³ only at points with v ≤ v1; so they run on
R(V, e) with V in place of v1 and with the same constants, and so does their form with four derivatives. For E^Ω in case
P, apply ∂^i Ω^β, i + |β| ≤ 2, to [[eq:layer-w-equation]] and write its principal part as −(1/2) □̃ −
(1/2)(g_λ^(ab) − g0^(ab)) ∂_a ∂_b, □̃ := g0^(ab) ∂_a ∂_b. The rotations commute with □̃ ([[sec:past-inequalities]]),
and [Ω_ab, ∂_c] is a coordinate derivative, so the commutators are sums of products of ∂^j Ω^γ (g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹),
j + |γ| ≥ 1, with second derivatives of ∂^(i′) Ω^(β′) w of a lower total order, as in step (iii) of the proof of
[[prop:far-region]]. On P, g_app⁻¹ − g0⁻¹ is λ times a profile of class E_(3/2) ([[thm:past-approximant]]), and w with
its coordinate derivatives and rotations to the total order three is at most C t^(−2) by [[eq:far-energy]],
[[eq:near-bootstrap]] and [[lem:past-sobolev]] (a), as in the proof of (P-iii) in [[sec:past-proof]]; so these
commutators are at most C t^(−3/2) times energies of lower order, and the Gronwall kernel stays integrable on P. The
forcing term ∂^i Ω^β Ric^H(g_app) obeys the bound of [[lem:layer-flat-past]] (d), since that proof bounds each monomial
through the classes E_p(ε^d), which are defined with the rotations; the nonlinear terms are bounded as in step (3) of
the proof of [[lem:layer-remainder-past]], every factor with at most one rotation by the same global bounds. On Ω the
rotations have coefficients at most b(τ0), so they cost a constant there. The induction on i + |β| gives the bound.

(2) *Pointwise bounds.* Let V and e be as in (1), and 0 < y ≤ 1. Then

    (i)  |w(X)| ≤ C_S y^(−1/2) E^♭(τ(X))   at every X ∈ Ω with v(X) + eψ_max + y ≤ V,
    (ii) t^(3/2) |w(X)| ≤ C_S E^Ω(τ(X))    at every X ∈ P with v(X) + eψ_max ≤ V   (case P),

with E^♭ := E_2 in case Ω and E^♭ := E^Ω in case P, and C_S depending on a, b(τ0) only. For (i), let τ := τ(X); the
slice R(V, e) ∩ Σ_τ is {a ≤ r ≤ ρ(τ)}, and ρ(τ) ≥ V − eψ_max − v(X) + r(X) ≥ r(X) + y, so I := [r(X), r(X) + y] lies in
it. For |β| ≤ 2 put f_β(r) := ‖Ω^β w(τ, r, ·)‖_(L²(S³)), a Lipschitz function with |f_β′(r)| ≤ ‖∂_r Ω^β w(τ, r, ·)‖_(L²(S³))
almost everywhere. For r ∈ I, f_β(r(X)) ≤ f_β(r) + ∫_I |f_β′|; the mean over r ∈ I and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality give

    f_β(r(X)) ≤ y^(−1/2) (∫_I f_β²)^(1/2) + y^(1/2) (∫_I f_β′²)^(1/2) ≤ a^(−3/2) (y^(−1/2) ‖Ω^β w(τ)‖ + ‖∂_r Ω^β w(τ)‖),

the norms on the slice, since r³ ≥ a³ on it. H²(S³) ⊂ L^∞ with the rotations, as in the proof of
[[lem:layer-null-pointwise]], gives |w(X)| ≤ C Σ_(|β|≤2) f_β(r(X)); on Ω, |x| ≤ b(τ0), so ‖Ω^β w‖ + ‖∂_r Ω^β w‖ ≤ C
Σ_(p≤3) ‖∂^p w‖ ≤ C E_2; and y ≤ 1. For (ii), let τ := τ(X) ≤ τ0 and r1 := v_s − τ ≥ v_start − τ = r_in(τ). The points
(τ, r, x̂(X)) with r1 ≤ r ≤ r(X) have v ∈ [v_s, v(X)], so they lie in R(V, e) ∩ P⁺, and w = 0 where v = v_s
([[prop:layer-seed-past]]). Hence f_β(r(X)) ≤ ∫_(r1)^(r(X)) |f_β′| ≤ (v(X) − v_s)^(1/2) r1^(−3/2) ‖∂_r Ω^β w(τ)‖, with r1 ≥
r_in(τ) ≥ t ([[lem:past-domain]] (d)) and v(X) − v_s ≤ 1; ∂_r = x̂·∂_x commutes with the rotations, so ‖∂_r Ω^β w‖ ≤
‖∂ Ω^β w‖ ≤ E^Ω, and H²(S³) ⊂ L^∞ as before gives (ii).

(3) *Constants and thresholds.* Let n_0 be the supremum of ‖g0⁻¹‖ over Ω, resp. P ∪ Ω; it is finite, as g0⁻¹ =
η⁻¹ − (μ/r²) L0 ⊗ L0 with μ/r² ≤ μ(v_end)/a² and |L0|_e² = 2. Let C_k bound |g_λ − g0|/λ there (item 4 of [[thm:main]],
resp. items 3 and 4 of [[thm:past-construction]] with [[thm:past-approximant]]). Where n_0 |g_λ − g0| ≤ 1/2, ‖g_λ⁻¹‖ ≤
2n_0 and g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹ = −g0⁻¹ (g_λ − g0) g_λ⁻¹, so ‖g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹‖ ≤ C_g |g_λ − g0| with C_g := 2n_0². Further:

- C_a: ϖ |g_app − g0| ≤ C_a λ ε_(c′)(v) at every point of S(v_s + 1) with that v, by [[lem:layer-flat]] (a)–(c) and
  [[prop:layer-identification]] (case Ω; |g_j| ≤ C ε_(2j+1)(v) for ε ≤ 1, and 2N + 1 ≤ c′), and by
  [[lem:layer-flat-past]] (a)–(c) on P (each profile of g_app − g0 in E_(3/2)(ε), r ≥ t on P⁺, g_app = g0 on P ∖ P⁺);
- C_G: ϖ |w| ≤ C_G λ^(K−3), by step 1 of the proof of [[thm:uniform-existence]] on Ω and item 3 of
  [[thm:past-construction]] on P (there |w| ≤ C_G λ^(K−3) t^(−2) and t ≥ 1);
- C_w and C_S of (1) and (2), and C_B := Σ_(i≤c′) c_i (c_0 := 1), so that, by (B_(c′)), [[lem:edge-kappa]] (c) and κ ≥ 1,

      ε_(c′)(V) = Σ_(i≤c′) sup_(v′≤V) |env^(i)(v′)| ≤ Σ_(i≤c′) c_i κ(V)^i env(V) ≤ C_B κ(V)^(c′) env(V)

  for v_s < V ≤ v_s + 4x_D, with x_D as below.

Put θ_D := (4 ψ_max C_g C_B (C_a + 2C_S C_w + C_G))⁻¹. Let x_D > 0 be so small that 4x_D ≤ min(x0, v_♯ − v_s, 1),
4x_D ≤ x_j of [[lem:edge-kappa]] (c) for every j ≤ c′, and, for every y ∈ (0, 4x_D], κ(v_s + y) ≥ 1 and y κ(v_s + y) ≥ 4
([[lem:edge-kappa]] (a)), and

    κ(v_s + y/2) κ(v_s + 4y)^(c′+1) env(v_s + 4y) ≤ θ_D      for every y ∈ (0, x_D],                    (D)

which (H_D) with c = c′ + 1 allows. Let λ_D := min{1, λ0, 1/(2C1), 1/(2 n_0 C_k), (8 ψ_max C_g C_G κ(v_s +
x_D/2))^(−1/(K−3))} in case Ω, with λ_P and 1/(2C_P) in place of λ0 and 1/(2C1) in case P. Let λ ≤ λ_D. Then e_0 ≤ 1,
‖g_λ⁻¹ − g0⁻¹‖ ≤ C_g |g_λ − g0| everywhere, and

    Λ_0 := (4 ψ_max C_g C_G λ^(K−3))⁻¹ ≥ 2κ(v_s + x_D/2).

κ is continuous, nonincreasing and tends to ∞ at v_s ([[lem:edge-kappa]] (a)), so y_* := sup{y ∈ (0, x_D] : κ(v_s + y)
≥ Λ_0} satisfies 0 < y_* < x_D/2 and κ(v_s + y_*) = Λ_0. Put x̄ := 2y_*.

(4) *The tilt at every depth.* For 0 < x ≤ x_D put e(x) := min(e_0, 1/(ψ_max κ(v_s + x))), and consider

    [x]:      2Δ(v_s + 2x) ≤ e(x).

As 2Δ ≤ e_0 always, [x] says 2Δ(v_s + 2x) ψ_max κ(v_s + x) ≤ 1. In both steps below we use, for v_s < V ≤ v_s +
4x_D, 2Δ(V) ≤ 2C_g sup_(S(V)) ϖ (|g_app − g0| + |w|), and C_a λ ε_(c′) ≤ C_a ε_(c′) as λ ≤ 1.

*[x] for x̄/2 ≤ x ≤ x_D.* Here the bound C_G λ^(K−3) of the whole remainder suffices: 2Δ(v_s + 2x) ψ_max κ(v_s + x) is
at most

    2C_g ψ_max C_a C_B κ(v_s + 2x)^(c′) env(v_s + 2x) κ(v_s + x/4) + 2C_g ψ_max C_G λ^(K−3) Λ_0 ≤ 1/2 + 1/2,

the first term by κ(v_s + x) ≤ κ(v_s + x/4), κ ≥ 1 and (D) at y = x/2, the second by κ(v_s + x) ≤ κ(v_s + y_*) = Λ_0.

*[2x] implies [x] for 0 < x < x̄/2.* Put V := v_s + 4x and e′ := e(2x). Then 2Δ(V) ≤ e′ ≤ e_0 by [2x], and

    ε_M(V) ≤ ε_(c′)(v_s + 4y_*) ≤ C_B κ(v_s + 4y_*)^(c′) env(v_s + 4y_*) ≤ C_B θ_D/κ(v_s + y_*/2) ≤ C_B θ_D/Λ_0 ≤ λ^(K−3) ≤ λ³,

by V < v_s + 2x̄ = v_s + 4y_*, (D) at y = y_*, κ(v_s + y_*/2) ≥ Λ_0, 4C_B θ_D ψ_max C_g C_G ≤ 1 and K ≥ 6. So (1)
applies on R(V, e′). Every X ∈ S(v_s + 2x) satisfies v(X) + e′ψ_max + x ≤ v_s + 2x + 1/κ(v_s + 2x) + x ≤ V, as
2x κ(v_s + 2x) ≥ 4; so (2) with y := x ≤ 1 gives ϖ |w| ≤ C_S C_w x^(−1/2) λ^(N_*) ε_M(V) on S(v_s + 2x). With
x^(−1/2) = 2(4x)^(−1/2) ≤ 2κ(V)^(1/2) ≤ 2κ(V) (as 4x κ(V) ≥ 4 and κ ≥ 1), λ ≤ 1 and ε_M ≤ ε_(c′), 2Δ(v_s + 2x) ψ_max
κ(v_s + x) is at most

    2C_g ψ_max (C_a + 2C_S C_w) C_B κ(V)^(c′+1) env(V) κ(v_s + x/2) ≤ 2C_g ψ_max (C_a + 2C_S C_w) C_B θ_D ≤ 1/2,

by κ(v_s + x) ≤ κ(v_s + x/2) and (D) at y = x. This is [x].

Every x ∈ (0, x̄/2) is covered: if m ≥ 1 is the least integer with 2^m x ≥ x̄/2, then 2^m x < x̄ ≤ x_D, so [2^m x] holds
by the first step, and [2^j x] for j = m − 1, …, 0 by the second, as 2^j x < x̄/2 for j < m. So [x] holds for every
x ∈ (0, x_D].

(5) *The reach.* Let X be a boundary point with x = v − v_s ≤ x_D and ε_M(v + 1/κ(v)) ≤ λ⁵. Put e := e(x), ρ_v :=
eψ_max ≤ 1/κ(v) and v1 := v + ρ_v ≤ v_s + 2x, since x κ(v_s + x) ≥ 4. Then 2Δ(v1) ≤ 2Δ(v_s + 2x) ≤ e by [x],
ε_M(v1) ≤ λ⁵ ≤ λ³, and X ∈ R(v1, e), as v(X) + eψ(τ(X)) ≤ v + eψ_max = v1. By (1), the form with four derivatives of
step (2) of the proof of [[lem:edge-degree-two]] holds on R(v1, e), and steps (3) and (4) of that proof use nothing
else of the region: the bounds of [[lem:edge-degree-two]] hold at X with this v1 and reach ρ_v ≤ 1/κ(v). Moreover
ε_c(v) ≤ C_B κ(v)^(c′) env(v); κ(v1) ≤ κ(v) and env(v1) ≤ e^(κ(v)(v1 − v)) env(v) ≤ e env(v), as log env is concave with
slope κ(v) at v; so η(v1) ≤ ε_(c′)(v1) ≤ e C_B κ(v)^(c′) env(v), and [[eq:edge-degree-two]] gives [[eq:edge-localised]]
with C := 3e² C_2 C_B². ∎
