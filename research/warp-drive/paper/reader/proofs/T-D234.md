### The tables of the residual bounds {#sec:tab-d-tables}

These tables define the two approximants of [[def:tl5-approximants]] and carry the bounds of [[prop:tl5-points]],
[[prop:tl5-box-first]], [[prop:tl5-boxes-second]] and [[prop:tl5-coarse]]. In all of them a number written m e k
means m × 10^k, and the minus sign is −.

#### The coefficients {#sec:tab-d-coefficients}

*The object.* For each of the two approximants of [[def:tl5-approximants]] and each field F ∈ {R, m, n, W, s}, the
12,400 numbers C^F_ijk (0 ≤ i ≤ 24, 0 ≤ j ≤ 30, 0 ≤ k ≤ 15) of [[eq:tl5-fields]]. Every result of
[[sec:tl5-residuals]] uses them.

*The format.* The first table (the first approximant) is followed by the second. Within a table the fields come in the
order R, m, n, W, s, and for each field and each i there is a block headed "F, i = i:" listing the 496 numbers
C^F_ijk in the order (j, k) = (0, 0), (0, 1), …, (0, 15), (1, 0), …, (30, 15), separated by spaces and filled into
lines of at most 121 characters. Each number is an exact decimal of at most seventeen significant digits, and these
decimals define the approximants. The two tables have 25,250 lines.

#### The constants of the fields {#sec:tab-d-constants}

*The object.* The numbers L^F_γ of [[lem:tl5-chebyshev]], rounded upward to four significant digits, for both
approximants, every field and every multi-index γ with 2 ≤ |γ| ≤ 7; [[lem:tl5-field-box]] uses them.

*The format.* For each approximant and each field, a line "F:" followed by the 116 numbers in the order of γ by
|γ| = 2, 3, …, 7 and, within an order, with (γ₁, γ₂) decreasing lexicographically. 99 lines. Part (b) of
[[sec:tab-d-worked]] gives the first approximant's constants.

#### The values at the points of G {#sec:tab-d-points}

*The object.* For each approximant and each point (t_p, t_q, x_r) of the grid G of [[sec:tl5-bounds]], the smallest
integer v with max_ab |f_ab(t_p, t_q, x_r)| ≤ v·u, where u = 10⁻⁵ for the first approximant and u = 10⁻⁷ for the
second; [[prop:tl5-points]] and [[prop:tl5-boxes-second]] use them. The points x_r are numbered increasingly:
x_0, …, x_9 are the midpoints of [−1 + d_k, −1 + d_(k+1)] for k = 0, …, 9, x_10, …, x_17 are the numbers
−3/4 + (2r + 1)·3/32 for r = 0, …, 7, and x_18, …, x_27 are the midpoints of [1 − d_(k+1), 1 − d_k] for
k = 9, …, 0.

*The format.* For each approximant 576 lines "p q: v_0 v_1 … v_27", one for each pair (p, q), 0 ≤ p, q ≤ 23, in
lexicographic order. 1,152 lines. The largest entries are 242 (first approximant, p = 0, q = 23, r = 10) and 373
(second approximant, p = 0, q = 23, r = 6).

#### The numbers of the boxes {#sec:tab-d-boxes}

*The object.* For every box b of the subdivisions in the proofs of [[prop:tl5-box-first]], [[prop:tl5-boxes-second]]
and [[prop:tl5-coarse]], an upper bound for the number κ(b) of [[def:tl5-certificate]] (for [[prop:tl5-coarse]], for
κ_ab(b) with ab ∈ {ll, nn, xx, 11, 22}).

*The format.* (i) The box B* of [[prop:tl5-box-first]]: its sub-box (i, j, k), 0 ≤ i, j, k ≤ 31, is
[−1/2 + i/64, −1/2 + (i + 1)/64] × [−1/2 + j/64, −1/2 + (j + 1)/64] × [k/128, (k + 1)/128]. For each pair (i, j)
two lines "i j:" carry the smallest integers v_k with κ(b) ≤ v_k × 10⁻⁶, for k = 0, …, 15 and k = 16, …, 31.
2,048 lines; the largest entry is 298, for the sub-box (0, 31, 0) of [[sec:tab-d-worked]].

(ii) The boxes B1, …, B4 of [[prop:tl5-boxes-second]], in this order, each divided into n³ equal sub-boxes with
n = 8, 4, 4, 16 and the sub-box (a, b, c) the a-th in t, the b-th in σ and the c-th in x, counted from 0 at the
lower ends. For each pair (a, b) a line "a b:" carries the n smallest integers v_c with κ ≤ v_c × 10⁻⁶. 356 lines with
the headings; the largest entries in B1, …, B4 are 61, 492, 488 and 174.

(iii) The region Π of [[prop:tl5-coarse]]: for each of the 416 boxes, labelled "i j r" with the t- and σ-intervals
[−1 + i/2, −1/2 + i/2] and [−1 + j/2, −1/2 + j/2] and r the number of the x-interval (in the order of the points
x_r, the twenty outer intervals and the six intervals [−3/4 + r/4, −1/2 + r/4]), the five numbers κ_ll, κ_nn, κ_xx,
κ_11, κ_22 rounded upward to two significant digits; three boxes to a line. 139 lines. The largest entries are
4.7e4, 1.4e5, 1.8e7, 1.5e6 and 1.4e6.
