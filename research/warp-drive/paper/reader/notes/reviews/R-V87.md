significant figures". That number is 2.41 × 10⁻³, not 2.42 × 10⁻³. The preposition "with" and the contrast with the
first half of the sentence fix the intended sense: the least number with three significant figures that is an upper
bound. Saying exactly that removes the second reading.

    2.42 × 10⁻³.
