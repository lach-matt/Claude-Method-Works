### F1 (Major). The development of [[thm:std-ctg-neighbourhood]] does not give (d) or M̃ as stated

Location: P-F123.md lines 490–500 (the paragraph after def:tl5-cip-development); it affects (d) at line 484 and its

[[thm:std-ctg-neighbourhood]] satisfies (a)–(c), and (d) is its conclusion, the development containing a future
neighbourhood of the two hypersurfaces. Its uniqueness is not used."

What is wrong. Two things that def:tl5-cip-development needs are not what the theorem gives.

1. (d) asks for an open U ⊂ M̃ containing ι(N) with U ∩ J⁺(ι(N)) ⊂ M. Near the far end spheres
   ι({u*} × {1/2} × S³) and ι({−10} × {v*} × S³), J⁺(ι(N)) ∩ U contains points outside the domain of dependence of
   N; for example the continuation of a generator of ι(N₀) beyond u = u*. A past directed causal curve from such a
   point can go to v < 1/2 without meeting N. No development of the data on N contains these points, so (d) cannot be
   "its conclusion". The proof behind [CTG23, Theorem 1.1] confirms the issue: [CCTW16, proof of Theorem 5.3, p. 55]
   parametrises the hypersurfaces as [0, ∞) × Y ("As shown in Section 4.1.1, without loss of generality we can
   parameterise each of N± as [0, ∞) × Y"; Section 4.1.1, p. 39). So the theorem is about hypersurfaces with no far
   end. If it is applied to [−10, u*) × S³ instead, the future neighbourhood can thin out as u → u*. Then the uniform
   λ₀ of lem:tl5-cip-chart (3) at c = u* is not available.
2. M̃. lem:tl5-cip-chart needs an open Lorentzian manifold containing ι(N) in its interior. It uses it for the
   exponential maps with t ∈ [−δ, 0) and t ∈ (σ(y), σ(y) + δ], and for the inverse-function arguments "near each
   point it extends smoothly ([[lem:tl5-cip-extend]] (a), in charts of M̃)". The theorem gives a development that
   contains a future neighbourhood of the hypersurfaces. It gives no ambient manifold beyond them, and the text does
   not say where M̃ comes from.

Both repairs are standard. For 2 the reading needed is that the development is smooth up to the hypersurfaces, as
Rendall's metric is on the closed region {x¹ ≥ 0, x² ≥ 0} ([[thm:std-cp-rendall]]). That reading should be stated,
like the reading of the data notion. The other option is to weaken (d) to what lem:tl5-cip-chart (3) uses (short
null geodesics from ι(N₀), normal to S_c and transverse to ι(N₀), lie in M). That still needs the extension of the
data in item 1 at c = u*.

given up to a conformal factor, the rest by ordinary differential equations along the generators) and says the data
on each hypersurface "have to be complemented by certain data on N⁺ ∩ N⁻". [CCTW16, p. 55] says its frame data are
"determined from the general relativistic initial data by a standard procedure". [CTG23] is still a preprint: I found
no journal version.

Replacement: in item 16, replace "No other entry changes." by "In [[thm:std-ctg-neighbourhood]], after its last line,

    The data are those of the theorem the authors recall in their Section 1 (p. 2): "A classical theorem of Rendall [13]
    shows that characteristic initial data for the vacuum Einstein equations on two three-dimensional characteristic
    hypersurfaces intersecting transversally at a submanifold S lead to a vacuum spacetime that covers a future
    neighborhood of S. The result has been extended to higher dimensions in [2]", [2] being [CBCM10]; their proof
