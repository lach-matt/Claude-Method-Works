## N3 (b): the mathematical claim, step by step

The claim is m + 5 ≤ d′_0, by m ≤ d_* and d′_0 ≥ d_0 + d_Φ ≥ d_* + 5.

1. **m ≤ d_*.**
   - m is the order of the fixed forcing seminorm in which the proof of lem:iii-j2-2-3 needs R_j → 0. That is where
     S^(0,0)_ν is bounded (43:313–316).
   - That proof gets (L_j − L_(b_0))u_j → 0 there "for h_j → 0 in 𝒳^(d′), d′ fixed ([[lem:iii-j2-2-1]] (b) at that
     order)" (43:316–317).
   - lem:iii-j2-2-1 (b) at the order k reads the background at k + d: ‖Au‖_k ≤ C_k(‖u‖_(k+2) + ‖β‖_(k+d)‖u‖_d)
     (43:206). At k = m that order is m + d_BG ≥ m.
   - "None of these orders depends on k, and d_* is their maximum" (43:321).
   - So m ≤ d′ ≤ d_*. The chapter's wording, "(b) at the order m, which reads the background at an order at least m,
     and d_* is the largest of the orders so read", is a faithful paraphrase.
   - The conclusion also survives the other parse of "at that order" in 43:317. However (b) is applied to bound an
     order-m seminorm, it reads the background at an order at least m.
2. **d′_0 ≥ d_0 + d_Φ.**
   - The proof of thm:iii-j2-4-2 reads ‖Φ(U)‖_(d_0) small "for ‖U‖_(𝒟^(d_0+d)) small" (43:446–447), with d the loss
     of U ↦ Φ(U), which is d_Φ.

3. **d_0 = d_* + D.** lem:iii-j2-2-3 fixes d_0 := d_* + D, with D the total loss of the compositions of sec:iii-j2-3,
   the d of thm:iii-j2-4-1 (43:295–296). thm:iii-j2-4-1's proof takes its d_0 from there (43:426–427).
4. **D is at least the loss of the forward solution operators.**

     at 43:396.
