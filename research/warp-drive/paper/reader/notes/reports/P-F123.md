## Route, and why

1. **Uniqueness without symmetry, avoiding the axes and maximal developments.**
   - lem:tl5-cip-frame: general formulas for six frame components of Ric for any 5D metric in double-null form, in any
     chart of S³ (Koszul connection table plus frame contraction).
   - lem:tl5-cip-jets: from these formulas, two vacuum double-null metrics with the same data on N₀ ∪ N₁ have equal
     transverse jets of all orders there. This is an induction by linear ODEs along generators:
     - on N₀, first a closed transport for ∂_v^k b from Ric(e₃,∂_A), then for ∂_v^k γ and ∂_v^k W from Ric_AB and
       Ric(e₃,e₄);
     - on N₁, first ∂_u^k γ and ∂_u^k W from Ric_AB and Ric(e₃,e₄), then ∂_u^k b from Ric(e₄,∂_A), a second-order ODE
       whose corner datum comes from the N₀ step.
   - So one Seeley extension of g₁ across N serves both metrics (lem:tl5-cip-extend).
   - Take τ = u + v + 1/(u*−u) + 1/(s−v) and the first point of disagreement in τ. Local harmonic coordinates (linear
     waves, BGP Theorem 3.2.11) and the reduced equations (CBCM10 (2.7)–(2.9), flat background) make the difference a
     solution of a linear normally hyperbolic system. It vanishes by one-sided uniqueness (lem:tl5-cip-wave, from BGP
     Lemma A.5.9, Theorem 1.3.13, Corollary A.5.4 and Theorem 3.1.1).
   - The resulting local isometry is the identity because the gauge is geometric. Outgoing coordinate curves and
     ingoing generators are null pregeodesics, and followed back they enter the agreement region, which fixes u, y and v.
   - The same argument handles the corner and boundary points: the common extension turns them into interior points.
2. **Existence.**
   - [CTG23] supplies one development. Its uniqueness is not used.
   - def:tl5-cip-development fixes what is taken from it. The data are Rendall's, in the formulation of CP12
     Theorem 2.1 (new Appendix A entry thm:std-cp-rendall); g_N determines them.
   - lem:tl5-cip-chart builds the double-null chart from exponential maps of null normals. It uses the inverse function
     theorem plus injectivity on compact sets, transversality by a Jacobi-field identity, and sides through the sign of
     g(·, V̲). It identifies Φ*g with g_N along N, including W (non-affinity plus corner value) and b on N₁ (via the shift
     ODE).
3. **(F3)** applies (F1) to the data that a solution induces on {v = s}, then glues across {v = s}. The jets match by
   lem:tl5-cip-jets (a), whose hypotheses allow the two metrics to lie on opposite sides.

Neither the reduced 2+1 system (singular at the axes) nor [CCTW16, Theorems 4.1, 4.3, 3.9] is used. The only black
boxes are [CTG23] for existence, and BGP, CBCM10 and Seeley, which are all already in Appendix A. Of the routes in
S-CIVP §3.8, this needs fewer external results.

## Regularity verdict, in detail

Smoothness enters in three places:
- [CTG23] is a smooth-category theorem. Its Sobolev remark is not a statement.
- The common extension needs jets of all orders, and BGP is for smooth coefficients.
- The gluing in (F3) needs jets of all orders.

The engine behind [CTG23], CCTW16 Theorem 3.9, needs ℓ ≥ 7 for Y = S³, on fields that include the Weyl tensor. That
means seven derivatives of the curvature along the generators. The 10 l5 data have P, Q ∈ C³ \ C⁴ in v, so the
curvature has one. No quoted result gives (F1) for such data. Lowering the regularity would need a Luk–Rodnianski-type
low-regularity double-null theory in 4+1, which is unpublished; [LR20] is 3+1.

**What would have to change (the author's call):** replace env by an envelope in C_c^∞([1/2, 7/2]), such as that of
lem:envelope-constant, with u* < u₀. The data of def:tl5-problem are then smooth and thm:tl5-cip applies, provided R
stays positive.
- **Cost:** the certified cone results (sec:cone-approximant, lem:cone-ellipse, which uses that sin⁴ is entire) and
  the residual tables (sec:tl5-residuals) are specific to sin⁴ and would have to be redone. The positivity bounds of
  prop:cone-exact would have to be re-derived.
- **(H1)–(H3) stay open** in either case. That is the other agent's part.

**Finite-regularity restatement:** Placement item 15 gives lem:tl5-continuation-ck as an optional C^k version, with a
complete proof by the stated changes.
- (H2) is replaced by (H2_k): bounds on derivatives of order k+1 in a convex-chart atlas, k ≥ 2.
- It still assumes (F1)–(F3) in class C^k, which nothing establishes. So it is not needed now, and it is marked "not to
  be placed unless a finite-regularity theory is added".
