## Verdict on chapter 71

**Chapter 71's +3 holds. The charge 20,183 = 20,180 + 3 of lem:cs-elem (e) is right, and no number in chapter 71
moves.** The split loss (d_F; d_F + 3, d_F) at the q-background is right, and so is (d_F; d_F, d_F) at the other three
backgrounds. Both are weaker than what the proofs give (see below), so both are true.

The old "+5" of chapter 43 is not a competing charge. It is the 5 that the proof of thm:iii-i-5-3 adds to the loss of
thm:ii-f1-1-1: the 2 metric derivatives in the coefficients of P − P_0, plus the 3 of Sobolev embedding in dimension
five. That 5 is already inside d_F: eq:fwd-split gives d = D_0 + D_1 + d′ + d″ + 1 + 5 = 20,180. In the old sentence,
"‖P − P_0‖_((0;k+d)) ≤ C_k ‖β‖_(k+d+5)", d was the loss of thm:ii-f1-1-1, not that of thm:iii-i-5-3. The sentence was
right at the first three backgrounds. At the q-background it needs k + d + 8 in that notation. The closing phrase
"[[eq:iii-i-5-2]] with ‖β‖ in place of ‖h‖" claimed the same orders, and that is false at the q-background: the order
of ‖β‖ must be k + d + 3.

## What the proofs give at each background

Notation:
- d is the loss of thm:iii-i-5-3, which cor:iii-h2-5-1 keeps at b; d = 20,180 by thm:fwd-loss and cor:fwd-near-b0.
- E^j = H^(j,(β_I+1,β_++2,1/2))_b and G^j = H^(j,(β_I,β_+,−3/2))_b.
- T_β is the forward solution operator, and β = (h, p⃗, q).

**Mechanism.** The proof of thm:iii-i-5-3 applies thm:ii-f1-1-1 with the loss d − 5. In the relaxed form of
rem:ii-f1-7-1 this is 20,175 = D_0 + D_1 + d′ + d″ + 1. Then eq:ii-f1-1-5 gives

    ‖u‖_(G^k) ≤ C_k (‖f‖_(E^(k+d−5)) + ‖P − P_0‖_((0;k+d−5)) ‖f‖_(E^(d−5))).

The proof's "tame form" bounds ‖P − P_0‖_((0;k+d−5)) ≤ C_k(|S| + ‖h‖_(𝒳^(k+d))).
- At g_p⃗ the |S| becomes |S| + |S⁽⁰⁾_1| (lem:iii-i7-1-3 (b)). At b it becomes |b − b₀| + |S| (cor:iii-h2-5-1).
- All of these are ≤ C|p⃗|, and the term C|p⃗|‖f‖_(d−5) is absorbed in ‖f‖_(k+d−5).

So **the estimate depends on the background only through ‖h̃‖_(𝒳^(k+d)), via ‖P − P_0‖_((0;k+d−5))**. The bound on
the background is ‖h̃‖_(𝒳^m):
- h̃ = h: ‖h‖_(𝒳^m) ≤ ‖β‖_m.
- h̃ = h + h^(−1)(S): ≤ ‖h‖_m + C_m|S| ≤ C_m‖β‖_m (lem:iii-i4-2-5).
- h̃ = h + h_tot(p⃗): ≤ ‖h‖_m + C_m|p⃗| ≤ C_m‖β‖_m (lem:iii-i7-2-5 (a), lem:iii-i8-2-5, lem:iii-i9-2-5).
- h̃ = h + h_rem(q) + h_tot(p⃗): ≤ C_m(‖h‖_m + |p⃗| + ‖q‖_(𝒬^(m+3))) ≤ C_m‖β‖_(m+3).
  - h_(b,rem)(q) is a finite sum of fixed tensors times the components of q and their t_*-derivatives of order at
    most 3: S_rem with 1 (through h^(≤1)_(b,s1)), b_rem with 0, and Y_ψ with at most k_μ ≤ 3 (def:iii-g2-table-1-1:
    max k_μ = 3, at s1).
  - On functions of t_*, ∂_(t_*) = t_*⁻¹(t_*∂_(t_*)), with t_*∂_(t_*) the b-vector field of 𝒬.

The forward estimate at each background, every order explicit:
