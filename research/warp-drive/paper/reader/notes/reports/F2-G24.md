## The G2 judgment

I agree with the reader. The preprint's Lemma 5.14, as the reader quotes it, concludes positivity on tensors
ν ⊗ e + ν′ ⊗ f with ν′ ⊥ ν. That follows from causal positivity alone. I had worked out the same 2-plane case while
writing the fix: in an orthonormal frame with ν = dz⁰ and ν′ = dz¹, the form is B((A − L)u₊, u₊) + B((A + L)u₋, u₋),
where A − L and A + L are ℓ of the two future null covectors of the plane. A slice-wise Fourier argument needs only
that case. My counterexample tensor (three directions) is not of that form, so it does not bear on [Hin26a]. My F-G24
report should not be recorded as "the citation was misdescribed". The correct record is: **the slice-wise use of
[Hin26a, Corollary 5.15] is replaced by a pointwise route, which needs e < 2/D, which holds (e = 1/102400 < 2/5).**
The crosswalk row sec:iv5-6 now says exactly this.
