### (i) New lemma lem:tl5-cip-data-extend

It is inserted after the paragraph that follows def:tl5-cip-development, and is written out in full. It extends the data past u* and v* by η > 0, with u* + η < 0, and keeps the constraints.

- **On N₁.**
  - Ω := (det γ/det γ₀)^(1/6) and γ̂ := Ω⁻²γ. Then χ̂ is trace-free, and Ric(e₄, e₄) = −(3/Ω)(Ω″ − 2W′Ω′ + |χ̂|²Ω/3). I checked this from the first line of eq:tl5-cip-ricci.
  - W and γ̂ are extended by the operator of lem:tl5-cip-extend (a). The extended γ̂ is renormalised to det γ₀.
  - Ω solves the linear equation with the data at v = 1/2. By uniqueness it is the given Ω on [1/2, v*], and it is positive for η small.
  - b then comes from lem:tl5-cip-shift.
- **On N₀.**
  - The coordinates are transported by the flow ψ_u of b. Since ∂_u(ψ_u*γ) = ψ_u*(∂_uγ + ℒ_bγ) = 2ψ_u*χ̲, the second line of eq:tl5-cip-ricci takes the form of the first.
  - W and b are extended freely, since N₀ carries only Ric(e₃, e₃) = 0. γ̃ is extended as on N₁, and then pushed back.
- **The extended data are smooth data in the sense of def:tl5-cip-data** (u* + η, v* + η):
  - smooth on each hypersurface;
  - equal on S;
  - equal to g_N on N;
  - G-invariant (the extension operator acts on u and v alone; γ₀ is invariant; ψ_u commutes with G);
  - in double-null form (γ positive definite);
  - and the constraints hold.

### (ii) The paragraph after def:tl5-cip-development

The paragraph is rewritten.
- Nit N5 is included: the angular coordinates on N₀ are those constant along e₃, and the conformal factor has value 1 and derivatives 0 on S.
- The theorem is applied to the extended data, on the hypersurfaces without their far ends.
- M̃ comes from the proof of [CTG23]. That proof is the argument of [CCTW16, Thm 5.3]. On p. 55 it gives the solution in a double-null coordinate system (p, q, z) that covers [0, a₁′] × [0, a₀′] × S³ and [0, a₀] × [0, a₁] × S³, with the hypersurfaces at {p = 0} and {q = 0}.
- The metric is smooth up to the boundary, as Rendall's metric is on {x¹ ≥ 0, x² ≥ 0}.

**Departure from the reader's text (and why).** The reader extended *the metric* across the boundary "in charts, glued by a partition of unity", and claimed that a future causal curve from ι(N) "stays on the future side". I made two changes:
- **The ambient manifold.** Gluing charts presupposes an ambient manifold. The double-null coordinates give one: ℝ² × S³.
- **The extension.** The claim fails for a general extension. Outside M, ∇p can become spacelike, and then a causal curve can leave {p ≥ 0} (for example u′ = −c|u|^(1/2) from u = 0).
  - So I extend **the inverse metric** g⁻¹ instead. Its (pp) and (qq) components vanish on the two boxes, and the extension operator is linear, so they vanish on M̃ as well.
  - Then p and q are null on all of M̃, hence nondecreasing along future causal curves. So (d) holds with U = M̃, since {p, q ≥ 0} ∩ M̃ ⊂ M.
- The gluing function and the L-shaped M₀ are chosen so that the two box extensions are only used where they are defined.

lem:tl5-cip-chart is otherwise unchanged. Its (1) and (3) now rest on these facts.
