## Method

- (C2) at 10⁻⁶: disc_ι(64·10¹⁸P) = 144𝔇(κ), where 𝔇 has degree 9 and printed integer coefficients. Descartes' rule
  allows at most four positive roots, and four integer brackets with sign changes show none of them is an integer.
- (C4) and the resultant part of 𝖹₁ at 10⁻⁶: Res(𝒜_k, ℬ_k) = 4𝔖(k, κ), with 𝔖 printed (52 coefficients, degree 15
  in k and 3 in κ). For each k = 3..51 there is exactly one positive root, and it lies strictly between consecutive
  values l(l + 2).
- Both tables were re-parsed from the file and re-verified. All other bounds are hand inequalities.
- The interval count uses exact discriminants and resultants of degree up to 393 in h, checked by root isolation.
  It is pending because it is not written out.
