## Route taken: direct kernel triviality at γ_Υ > 0, with no perturbation

1. **The model in time-harmonic form** (lem:tf-gauge-modes). N_tf of □̄^Υ is the spectral family of the Minkowski
   model. In the frame (dt̄, dx^i), and after conjugating by e^(iσ̂r), its components (φ, ω_i) solve damped Helmholtz
   equations:
   - (Δ − σ̂² − 2iσ̂γ(2−e)/r)φ = −(2γe/r)∇·ω;
   - (Δ − σ̂² − 2iσ̂γ/r)ω_i = −(2γ/r)∂_iφ.

   I checked this exactly against the definitions 2(δ̄+Ē)Ḡδ̄* in sympy (`modes.py`).
2. **Decay.** By thm:i-c-4-3 (5), a kernel element decays like r^(−3/2−λ), where λ = γ(1−e) > 0 is the S̄-threshold. So
   no flux appears at infinity, for any θ ∈ [0, π], including θ = 0 and θ = π.
3. **Two inequalities with a sign** (lem:tf-damped-helmholtz). They come from Green's formula with v and with v/r, for
   every θ ∈ [0, π]:
   - 2c‖v‖₊² ≤ |⟨f, v⟩|;
   - ‖∇v‖₊² ≤ ‖v‖₊² + ½‖f‖₋².

   The norms are L²(dx/r) and L²(r dx).
4. **Closing the loop.** Combining these gives ‖∇φ‖₊² ≤ Λ_tf‖∇φ‖₊², with
   Λ_tf = (1 + 2γ²)(4e²/(2−e)² + 8γ²e²).
   - If Λ_tf < 1, then φ = 0 and so ω = 0.
   - (iii) follows from index 0 along γ ↦ τγ, with the spaces held fixed.
   - The result is prop:tf-gauge-explicit.
5. **γ_1** (lem:tf-lin-reduction). After Steps 1–4 of the proposition (η_0 = 0), the steps are:
   1. Solve □̄ω_0 = −2Ē^ΥḠu_0 with the flat 1-form inverse (prop:i-c-5-3).
   2. h_0 = u_0 − δ̄*ω_0 satisfies harmonic gauge and DRic h_0 = 0, so □̄h_0 = 0. The flat tensor tf-admissibility then
      gives h_0 = 0.
   3. Then ω_0 lies in the tf-kernel of □̄^Υ, so ω_0 = 0 by step 4.

   This replaces Step 6 entirely and removes the dependence of γ_1 on h and γ_C.
