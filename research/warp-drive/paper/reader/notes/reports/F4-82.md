## Departures from the reviewer, with reasons

     Before, these followed from the weighted bound only with a loss of n^(1/2).
3. **The m1 added sentence is corrected.** The reviewer's sentence says that replacing S by the union of its images
   under G keeps (V1). That is false for the injectivity clause: two points of one orbit can have different sections.
   For example, with β0 = 2α (∂_αβ0 = 2) and sections {0} and {π}, each section is injective but their union is not
   (checks.py, item 7).

   - S^G is compact, and every condition of (V1), (V2) and the ray clause of (Ω) passes to subsets.
   - supp q ∩ (Σ_τ0 × S¹) ⊂ int S^G, because supp q is G-invariant and compact orbits have neighbourhoods whose
     G-images lie in int S.
