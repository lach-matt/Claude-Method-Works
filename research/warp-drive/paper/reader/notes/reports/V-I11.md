## Verdict

**Error, and it reaches statements.** The roots ι^C_(·,+) − 1 that come from □^C are indicial roots of L̂_b(0) in the
sense the lemma uses (they are on the list of [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]], after the semicolons), they have real part in
(1, 3), and at every admissible parameter point several of them lie in (1, 2): for every γ_C ≥ 10⁶ (h ≤ 10⁻⁶), every
z ∈ (0, ½], under (C1), there are real roots of types s0, s1 (two) and s2 in (1, 2), besides 2. They are in the window
(1 − ε, 2 + ε_+ − ε) of the zero energy solve of level 0 (Step 1 of the proof of [[prop:iii-i11-3-1]]), and they enter
û_0 with coefficients that are not zero in general. So:

1. The sentence "Below the real part 3 the only such root is 2" of [[lem:iii-i11-2-1]] is false at every admissible
   point, and E′ must contain the (μ, ·), μ such a root, in the window.

## 1. The indicial roots of L̂_b(0) with real part in (0, 5]

**Which operator and which roots.** The lemma expands û at ∂X, where L̂_b(0) = ρ²A with A b-elliptic, by
[[lem:iii-a-1-4]]; the boundary spectrum is that of the indicial family of A. Its roots are those of L_b^(0)
([[lem:iii-f-2-1]] (3), [[sec:iii-f1-1]]) and do not depend on b ((P2) of [[lem:iii-i15-1-1]]); 35:1021 says so
("the indicial roots of [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]]: those of L̂_b(0)"). The convention is r^(−ι) = ρ^ι (root 2 at s0 is the
mass, ρ²).

**How □^C enters.** [[lem:iii-f1-2-1]]: at each type the roots of L_b^(0) are, with multiplicity, −l and l + 2, the
roots of □^Υ plus 1 and the roots of □^C minus 1, less the roots of D. The □^C roots enter through the identity
δ̄Ḡ(2L̄) = □̄^C δ̄_(Ē_Υ)Ḡ (34:405): an indicial solution at ι^C − 1 is a constraint-violating mode whose gauge 1-form
δ̄_(Ē_Υ)Ḡ is an indicial solution of □̄^C at ι^C. D removes only the root 2 of □^C at s0 and s1 and the root 3 at v1.
So the ι^C_(·,+) − 1 are roots of L̂_b(0), and [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] lists them after the semicolons (34:425–437). The
paper itself treats them so elsewhere: Step 3 of [[prop:iii-f4-5-1]] and [[prop:is-iota]] put r + 2, r = ι^C − 1, into
ℰ^ι_ind; [[prop:is-mult]] counts the ι^C − 1 of real part in [−101, 5] among the roots of L̂_b(0); [[lem:ec-lb-ind]]
reads ι₂(h) − 1 as an indicial root of L_b^(0).

**The list with real part in (0, 5].** By [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]] (a) none has real part in (0, 1]. After the
semicolons, with the bounds of its proof (ι^Υ_(sl,l+j) ∈ (l + j, l + j + ¼), ι^Υ_(s0,2) ∈ (2, 2 + z/3]):

| type | entries in (0, 5] not from □^C | from □^C |
|---|---|---|
| s0 | 2; 1 + ι^Υ_(s0,2) ∈ (3, 3 + z/3]; 4 | ι^C_(s0,+) − 1 |
| s1 | 3; 1 + ι^Υ_(s1,2) ∈ (3, 13/4); 1 + ι^Υ_(s1,3) ∈ (4, 17/4); 5 | ι^C_(s1,+,1) − 1, ι^C_(s1,+,2) − 1 |
| s2 | 4; 1 + ι^Υ_(s2,3) ∈ (4, 17/4) | ι^C_(s2,+,j) − 1, j = 1, 2, 3 |

| sl, l ≥ 4 | none | ι^C_(sl,+,j) − 1 |

All the ι^C_(·,+) − 1 have real part above 1 ((C1): every root of □^C_b has real part ≥ 2 or < −C_0, the only one of
real part 2 being the root 2 at s0 and s1, which D removes). By [[lem:iii-f1-2-4]] (strip |Re ι| ≤ 5) only finitely
many lie in (0, 5]. Below the real part 3 the roots are therefore exactly 2 and the ι^C_(·,+) − 1 of real part below 3;
the proof of the lemma lists the left column and omits the right one.

**Where the □^C roots lie, at every admissible point.** With h := γ_C⁻¹ ∈ (0, 10⁻⁶], (v_C, e_C) = (1/2, 1/102400) and
e := e_C, the roots of □^C_b at scalar types 0 and l ≥ 1 and vector type l are those of P_0 = −(ι − 2)C, P_l and p_(v)
of [[prop:np-10-12]] ([[lem:iv4-1-3]]); they do not depend on z; (C1) holds for every such h ([[lem:iv4-1-3]] (a)).
Exact evaluation of the polynomials (checked against the determinant of [[eq:s14-nl]] multiplied out):

- *s0.* C(2) = 3h + 6h² + 15e + 6eh > 0 and C(3) = −2 + 20e − 6h + 12eh < 0. C has exactly one root above 2 (proof of
  [[prop:np-10-54]]), so ι^C_(s0,+) ∈ (2, 3), real: a root ι^C_(s0,+) − 1 ∈ (1, 2) of type s0.

**Which are met.** The windows of the four zero energy solves of [[prop:iii-i11-3-1]] are (q_j − 2, q′ − 2):
level 0, (1 − ε, 2 + ε_+ − ε); level 1, (η_1, 1 + ε_+ − ε); level 2, (η_2, ε_+ − ε); level 3, (κ − ε − 2, κ − 2) with
κ < 3. Levels 2 and 3 lie in (0, 1], where there are no roots. At level 1, a non-integer root with |μ| ≤ 50 has real
part at distance ≥ ε_ind > ε_+ from 1 ((f) of [[prop:iii-f1-2-3]]), so none is met (one with |μ| > 50 is not excluded by
the paper, and the repair covers it). At level 0 every ι^C_(·,+) − 1 with real part below 2 + ε_+ − ε is met: all of
types s0 and s1, ι₂ − 1 and the other s2 roots, the s3 root above, and at h = 10⁻⁶ all 2629. The vector roots in
(2, 3) are not met.

**They enter û with nonzero coefficients.** Fix a type τ and let μ_* be the root of type τ with least positive real
part (for τ = s3 it lies in (1, 1 + 10h]). Take k ∈ ker 𝒩(μ_*) of type τ and w := χρ^(μ_*)k, χ = 1 near ∂X. Then
K_w := L̂_b(0)w ∈ C_c^∞ + ρ^(Re μ_*+4)C^∞ (as L̂_b(0) − ρ²𝒩 ∈ ρ⁴Diff_b and 𝒩 annihilates ρ^(μ_*)k), and at b₀ the rows
r(w) vanish for τ ∉ {s0, s1, v1} (pairings with states of those types), so (w, 0, 0) = L̃_b(0)⁻¹(K_w, 0, 0) by
injectivity. The coefficient of ρ^(μ_*) in the first component of L̃_b(0)⁻¹(K, 0, 0) is a continuous linear functional of
K on ρ^aH^∞_b, a − 2 slightly above Re μ_* ([[lem:iii-a-1-4]]); it is k ≠ 0 at K_w, so it is nonzero at some
K ∈ C_c^∞ of type τ, by density. So the coefficients are not zero in general: the forcing F of the lemma is arbitrary
(g_0(0) = ∫ f dt_* for f compactly supported in 𝒴⁰(α)), δ_(g,E_Υ)G_g û solves □^C(0)(·) = 2δ_gG_g(F − columns), and
nothing makes the constraint part vanish. The gauge/constraint structure is the reason these roots exist, not a reason
for their coefficients to vanish.

## 2. What E′ is, and what replaces "Λ-valued"

**E′.** The first sentence of the lemma is right as a definition: E′ is what [[lem:iii-a-1-4]] builds from
(E − 2) ∪ (1 + ℰ_col) and all the indicial roots with real part in (q − 2, q′ − 2). These lie in 𝒵 := {μ indicial root
of L̂_b(0) : 0 < Re μ < 3}, as 0 < q − 2 and q′ − 2 ≤ 3. So

  E′ ⊂ ((E − 2) ∪ (1 + ℰ_col)) ∪̄ ⋃ {(μ, l_*) : μ ∈ 𝒵, q − 2 < Re μ < q′ − 2}, plus ℕ₀,

and at level 0 of [[prop:iii-i11-3-1]], û_0 has the exponents (ℰ^♭ − 1) ∪ (1 + ℰ_col) and (μ, ·) for every μ ∈ 𝒵 with
Re μ < 2 + ε_+ − ε (2 and the ι^C_(·,+) − 1 in (1, 2 + ε_+ − ε)).

**Λ-valuedness.** A root μ met by the solve of level j is the u-exponent μ + j + 1 (the dictionary of
[[sec:iii-i11-1]]: "a zero energy solution at level j with the exponent w at ∂X [corresponds] to the u-exponent w + j
+ 1"); at level 0 this is μ + 1, real part in (2, 3). Λ ∩ {Re < 3} is generated by ℰ_++, 𝒢, ℰ^C_I, λ_♯, 3, 2 + ℰ_col
and the increments of real part below 3/2, all built from roots of □^Υ, from σ_Υ and from integers: it does not depend
on γ_C and it is real ([[prop:is-lambda]] lists it at z = 4·10⁻⁶: 29 numbers plus p𝔡₀ + q𝔡₁). μ + 1 is a root of a □^C
polynomial and moves with h. A non-real μ + 1 is never in Λ. For the real roots near 2 (ι₂(h) at s2, the root in (2, 2
+ 10h) at s3, and their analogues at higher degree), every ν ∈ Λ ∩ (2, 2 + 10⁻⁵] has P_l(ν) equal to a polynomial in h
with constant term 8v(ν − 2)Q_5(ν) ≠ 0 (4Q_5(2 + t) = −e(15 + 3κ) + (2 + 10e)t + (5e − 2)t² < 0 for 0 ≤ t ≤ 10⁻⁵, at
every κ), so ν is a root for finitely many h only; as Λ ∩ (2, 2 + 10⁻⁵] is finite, such μ + 1 lie in Λ for finitely
many h only. **What replaces "Λ-valued"**: Λ-valued for Λ enlarged by the generator 1 + 𝒵, and only then. Nothing else
changes in the count of [[lem:iii-i10-1-2]] (c): a root met is a raise at one Taylor level, already counted by J_* and
l_*.
