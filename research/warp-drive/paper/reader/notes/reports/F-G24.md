**One finding the caller must know (G2).** The hypothesis that the old text recorded for [Hin26a, Lemma 5.14 /
Corollary 5.15] ("ℓ positive definite on future causal covectors") is *not sufficient* for what the energy estimate
uses. The energy identity produces the quadratic form 𝒬_(G,η,ℓ) on all of T* ⊗ E (not only on rank-one tensors
ζ ⊗ ω), and that form need not be positive under that hypothesis: the new lemma prints an explicit counterexample
(D = 3, E = ℝ², 𝒬 = 1 − 2s² < 0). What is true, and is now proved, is positivity for the actual damping symbol
ℓ = ℓ_(g,c,e) with c past timelike and **0 < e < 2/D** (a dimension-dependent smallness: 2/5 in 4 + 1, 1/2 in
3 + 1). The paper's e = 1/102400 satisfies it, so prop:iv5-6-1 and thm:iv6-2-1 stand as stated; only the
internal citation was imprecise. I judged this "the cited lemma's hypothesis was misdescribed", not "a step is
false", since the step's conclusion holds and is now proved; flagging it in case you read it otherwise.
