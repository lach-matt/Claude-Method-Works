**lem:tl5-cip-extend (d).** This is a general local zero-extension lemma, and (c) is now proved as a case of it.
- Statement: let O ⊂ ℝⁿ be open (for instance a chart image) and F ⊂ O relatively closed with interior F°. Let h vanish on O ∖ F°, be smooth on F°, and have all its partial derivatives on F° extend continuously to F with the value 0 on F ∖ F°. Then h is smooth on O.
- No regularity of ∂F is needed. The proof uses the mean value theorem on each component of a coordinate segment inside F°.
- P-H29's three uses fit it directly:
  - w, with F = D̄ ∩ U_p;
  - F_q, with F the closure of 𝒱_q ∖ Q₊, whose non-interior points lie on N⁺;
  - δ̃, in the same way.

**lem:tl5-cip-dn-form.** This is a new lemma before lem:tl5-cip-chart; it states what steps (4) and (5) prove.
- Setting: D′ = [−10, u₁] × [1/2, v₁] × S³ with u₁ ≤ u* and v₁ ≤ v*, and g a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian metric on D′.
- (a) If g⁻¹(du, du) = g⁻¹(dv, dv) = 0 and ∂_v = f∇u with f < 0, ∂_v being future directed, then g is in double-null form.
- (b) If g is in double-null form and vacuum, and (a)–(c) of def:tl5-cip-development hold on N ∩ D′ with (D′, g) and the inclusion in place of (M̃, g̃) and ι, then g = g_N on N ∩ D′.
