## Verdict

**The staged route holds.** No step fails.

Consequences:
- thm:tl5-cip applies to the 10 l5 problem without changing the envelope, provided u* < u₀.
- **thm:ten-l5 (b) can be strengthened.** There is s ∈ (1/2, 7/2] and a solution of def:tl5-problem on {−10 ≤ u ≤ u*, 0 ≤ v ≤ s} × S³ with these properties:
  - it is the seed for v ≤ 1/2;
  - it is smooth for v ≥ 1/2;
  - it is of class C³, and its third derivatives are Lipschitz;
  - it is unique among solutions whose restrictions to {v ≤ 1/2} and to {v ≥ 1/2} are smooth.
- The width s − 1/2 is not estimated.
- Regularity is no longer an obstruction anywhere. Existence on a *given* region needs (H1)–(H3) only.

## What was checked

1. **Stage one is smooth data, in the precise sense used.**
   - def:tl5-cip-data, which matches [CP12, Thm 2.1]'s data, asks for:
     - γ smooth on each hypersurface and continuous at S;
     - the corner quantities on S.
   - It asks for no further corner compatibility.
   - On N₀ = {v = 1/2}: for u* < u₀ the seed is analytic there (lem:tl5-seed (a)–(c)).
   - On N₁ ∩ [1/2, 7/2]:
     - env is the restriction of sin⁴, which is entire, so P and Q are analytic up to the ends.
     - W is analytic.
     - R solves ∂_r²R = −wR. Here w is analytic on [r_s, r_e] × [−1, 1], and the initial data do not depend on x.
     - β is smooth by lem:tl5-cip-shift, with b_S = ζ_S = 0.
     - The leaf metrics are smooth on S³ by the new lem:tl5-cip-axis (b). The axis conditions hold because 2P + Q = (1 + x)m and P + 2Q = (1 − x)n.
   - Agreement at S follows from lem:tl5-seed (d).
   - The constraints hold:
     - Raychaudhuri, by prop:cone-exact;
     - the shift constraint, by lem:tl5-cip-shift;
     - on N₀, because the seed is vacuum.
2. **Gluing to the seed at v = 1/2: class C³.**
   - lem:tl5-cip-jets (a), stopped at m = 3, carries agreement of the v-jets on Z to agreement on H. Its induction uses only lower orders, as written.
   - S-T6's symbolic computation in the class gauge (st6/jets_on_N0c.out) confirms the triangular structure. For example, on {v = c} the coefficient of ∂_u∂_vβ in Ric_ux is nonzero, and Ric_ux has no first v-jet of R, P, Q or W.
   - Data regularity on N across v = 1/2 and v = 7/2:
     - W is smooth.
     - P and Q are C³, not C⁴. Their fourth derivatives jump by 4!(π/3)⁴ times smooth factors.
     - **R is C⁷.** A and B are σ³ times smooth functions, so w is σ⁶ times a smooth function and R − r = O((r − r_s)⁸). I checked this symbolically (scratchpad cas/staged_orders.py: H/σ⁶ has no σ in the denominator; lowest power t⁶ in H and t⁸ in R − r). S-T6 says C^{4,1}. That holds, but it underestimates: w is quadratic in A and B.
     - **β is C⁴** (S-T6: C^{4,1}, agreed). The leaf metric is C³, so F_A is C², ζ is C³ and b is C⁴.
   - So the data on N are C³ across both ends.
   - Gluing then gives components in charts of S³ that are C³, with Lipschitz third derivatives. Ric is continuous and vanishes.
   - R, P, Q, W and β are C³ in (u, v, x) up to the axes. This uses the new axis lemma (a) on the stage side; the seed's functions do not depend on x.
3. **Stage two from v = 7/2.**
   - Data: a stage-one solution on D_(7/2), smooth up to v = 7/2, and g_N on [7/2, v*]. There env = 0, R is affine in r with coefficients smooth in x, and b continues smoothly.
   - After the shift v ↦ v − 3 these are smooth data, so thm:tl5-cip applies.
   - Gluing at 7/2 works the same way, with m = 3.
4. **Uniqueness among piecewise-smooth solutions.**
   - On {v ≤ 1/2}: prop:tl5-cip-unique, after the translation v ↦ v + 1/2, gives the seed.
   - On each stage: (F2).
   - Uniqueness among all C² solutions stays open. S-T6 agrees.

## Where I differ from S-T6

S-T6 §6 says that "thm:ten-l5 is unchanged: existence beyond v = 1/2 still needs (H1)–(H3)". That is true only for a *given* region D. (F1) of thm:tl5-cip, applied to stage one, gives a solution on some strip D_s with no estimate at all. Combined with the C³ gluing, this is existence beyond the seed. So thm:ten-l5 (b) is strengthened, as the coordinator anticipated.

## Changes to the proof file

- **New lemma lem:tl5-cip-axis**, after lem:tl5-cip-form.
  - (a) For a smooth G-invariant double-null metric, R, P, Q, W and β are smooth functions of (u, v, x) up to x = ±1. The proof restricts to s ↦ (cos s, sin s): the restriction is even about 0 and π/2, Hadamard's lemma applies, and K(−1) > 0 follows from γ ≥ cγ₀.
  - (b) Conversely, R, P and Q that are C^k in v and smooth in x, and satisfy the axis conditions, give leaf metrics on S³ whose chart components are C^k in v. This is the former inline A, B argument.
- **lem:tl5-cip-extend (b)** now also covers products of intervals, and has a C^m version.
- **lem:tl5-cip-jets (a)** has a finite-order version (k ≤ m), with one sentence added to the proof.
- **The paragraph after lem:tl5-cip-shift** is rewritten:
  - the data on N are smooth on [0, 1/2], [1/2, 7/2] and [7/2, ∞);
  - they are C³ in v across the ends (R is C⁷);
  - β is smooth on each stage and C⁴ in v.
- **rem:tl5-cip-axes** cites the axis lemma.
- **New subsection sec:tl5-cip-staged, with cor:tl5-cip-staged:**
  - (a) stage-one data are smooth, so (F1)–(F3) hold for v* ≤ 7/2;
  - (b) stage-two data, given a stage-one solution on D_(7/2), are smooth after v ↦ v − 3;
  - (c) seed plus stage one, plus stage two, give a solution of def:tl5-problem of class C³, smooth on each piece, with C³ class functions; conversely, a piecewise-smooth solution has this form and is unique.
- **rem:tl5-cip-regularity** is rewritten:
  - smoothness is used at three places;
  - the data are smooth per stage, and only the gluing is done at finite order;
  - C³ is sharp, because ∂_v⁴(P, Q) jumps;
  - the obstruction is (H1)–(H3) only.
  - The C_c^∞-envelope point is kept as a one-sentence aside: it applies in one stage, posed at v = 0, provided R > 0.
- The section introduction mentions the stages.
