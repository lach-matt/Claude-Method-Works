# Five-Dimensional Vaidya Black Holes as High-Frequency Limits of Vacuum Spacetimes

### Large data, T² symmetry, and settling to Schwarzschild–Tangherlini

**Matthew Lach** · Independent Researcher · November 2026

## Abstract

Huneau and Luk proved that generic, small-data, polarized U(1)-symmetric solutions of the Einstein–null dust system with
angularly separated dusts are weak limits of vacuum solutions on a fixed time interval, in an elliptic gauge in which
smallness is needed even for local existence. We prove a five-dimensional counterpart on a large background. For vacuum
metrics with a T² isometry and both reflections, the Einstein equations reduce exactly to 2+1 gravity coupled to two
free waves with the flat, positive-definite target (3/2) dψ² + 2 dω². The background is five-dimensional Vaidya, a black
hole of mass μ0 grown by an ingoing shell, on a domain from before the shell reaches the hole to the surface, inside or
on the horizon, where the curvature reaches a fixed scale l5. For every amplitude A > 0 and every smooth compactly
supported envelope env, with no smallness of either, we construct T²-symmetric vacuum metrics g_λ on that domain, of
wavelength λ ≤ λ0, with |g_λ − g0| ≤ Cλ, ∂g_λ bounded and ∂g_λ → ∂g0 weakly but not strongly, where g0 is Vaidya with
dμ/dv = (2/9) A² env², the null dust of Isaacson's flux. The proof uses the wave-map gauge relative to the limit, an
approximate solution of every order whose powers of λ beat every nonlinear term, a phase-shift identity for the one
large coefficient of the linear problem, and the constraints solved outward in r as a parabolic–hyperbolic system. When
μ0 > √72 l5², the outgoing expansion of the round spheres has one zero on each column crossing the horizon, converging
to it at O(λ), and after the shell every sphere's mass is the limit's to O(λ²). The same solutions are constructed from
the waves' free data at past null infinity, where they obey the five-dimensional Bondi mass law: their Bondi mass is
μ(v) + O(λ) during the shell and μ + (2/9) λ² A² ∫ env'² + O(λ³) after it. Outside the black hole, a continuation of
their data beyond a ball settles in the class to Schwarzschild–Tangherlini, and perturbations without symmetry that are
small in Hintz's data class settle to slowly rotating Myers–Perry black holes; the proof carries Hintz's proof of the
nonlinear stability of Kerr into five dimensions in full, with linear stability in the gauge of the nonlinear problem as
its linearisation. Further, the development of g_λ's own data settles without gluing, in a class wider by half-integer
orders, and continues inside the black hole to the curvature boundary; the construction extends to two crossing null
dusts and to restricted massless Vlasov limits; the constants are stated as far as they are explicit, among them an
upper bound for λ0 at a numerical point; at equality, μ0 = √72 l5², timelike curves of g_λ cross the boundary in a layer
whose width tends to zero with λ; and at the physical wavelength 10 l5 there is no separation of scales, existence
holding where the solution is the seed.
