- **(b) 375 is corrected by a new entry citing it** — the change 375 announced was not made and the entry says so.

## 3 · 28a-06 — *Fifty-nine* is a 172-row datum printed beside a 162-row count

**What the volume prints.** Main **L11848–L11853**: *"The Mathematical Compendium names a work for every one of its
objects — **162 works, 1669 to 2026** … **Fifty-nine of them are the works listed above**; the remainder are the
attributions of standard results — Newton 1669, Leibniz 1682, Euler 1748, Gauss 1809, Moore 1910, Birkhoff 1940 and
their kind … (register 1736)."*

**What the Register records. 1736 is the source and is NOT withdrawn.** It states its own basis in terms:
*"Matching the compendium's **'172 works, 1669–2026'** against the main volume's References **by author and year**
found **fifty-nine** shared…"* — and then records the **ten removals** that took 172 to 162, made afterwards, with
*"the count restated at 162 with the prior count kept beside it."*

**Measured, under CONVENTION AY** (stated before scoring: *the works listed above* is the References unit from its
heading — the **last** of two occurrences, L11503 — down to the line before the claim paragraph, because a sentence
about a list is not an entry in it; a compendium row matches if a surname in its `work` cell is paired with the
row's own year, read **positionally**, each parenthesised year taking the surnames of the window before it and a
window with no surname carrying the previous window's authors; a surname is a capitalised token followed by a
comma-and-capital, an ampersand, *and*, or a parenthesised year, so journal abbreviations are excluded by
construction. The extractor was **asserted on fourteen named citations and asserted to admit no journal token
before any verdict printed**):

**52 of 162 by author and year, against the printed Fifty-nine.** Bracketed: **52** exact, **55** at ±1 and ±2,
**57** at ±3, **58** at ±5 — **no tolerance up to ±5 reaches 59** — and **70** with the year dropped, which
overshoots. So *fifty-nine* is reachable by no author-and-year reading of the 162 rows, and only by a match looser
than the one the record says was made.

**28a-08 rides with it** (a finding about an earlier instrument, recorded not repaired): r2-ch28a §4's *"16 of 162"*
was **not** a bound on the overlap — its surname extractor returned **no surname at all from 123 of the 162 rows**,
because the compendium table prints a bare surname (`| 1669 | Newton |`) while the extractor required a following
initial, bracket, ampersand, comma or dash. Properly extracted the surname-only overlap is **70 of 162**, so the
honest bracket is **52 < 59 < 70**, not 16 < 59. That matters in the book's direction: **a bound below the figure
reads as refutation; one above it does not.**

**The ruling.** One of:

- **(a) The figure is re-taken on the list it is printed beside** — *Fifty-nine* becomes **52** (or the number your
  chosen matching convention yields on the 162 rows), with a new Register entry citing 1736.
- **(b) The sentence names the list the figure belongs to** — *Fifty-nine* is kept and the sentence says it is the
  172-row match of register 1736, taken before the ten removals, exactly as 1736 states.
- **(c) Both figures printed** — 59 of 172 as the recorded match, 52 of 162 as the count on today's list, which is
  what 1736's own *"the prior count kept beside it"* already does for the 172/162 pair.

*Docket 17 / 37.* **28a-09** is adjacent and unruled: 1736's *"the rest classical attributions this volume never
listed"* is falsified at one site — the compendium's `| 1929 | Janet |` (mc L3431) and References' *Janet, C.
(1928)* (main L11777) are one work under two years, and the 1929 row is one of *the rest*.

## 4 · 28b-07 — the sentence is true; its container over-reaches

**What the volume prints.** §R.7 *What is not cited, and why* (main L11807). Löwdin block lead-in, **L11808**:
*"Entered at registers 1701–1712. The companion paper carries the full list; **these are the works Chapter 35 rests
on**."* Three-body block lead-in, **L11819**: *"Entered at registers 1713–1724."* Montgomery's line, **L11821**:
*"Montgomery, R. (2014) … — shape space, the metric, the potential; **every structural object of Chapter 36 is his
or older.**"* And at **L11854**: *"**§R.7's two blocks are the works the chapters rest on**, not those lists."*

**Measured, under CONVENTION PROV** (stated before scoring: a provenance claim printed **inside** a block but
addressed **to** a chapter is a claim about the **chapter**, and is scored over the objects **the chapter names**,
each dated by the year the block gives it — never over the block's own contents. A sentence about a chapter is not
an inventory of the list it sits in):

**The sentence at L11821 is TRUE.** Chapter 36 names **ten** of the three-body block's works — Brudno, Chenciner,
Euler, Jacobi, Lagrange, Maupertuis, Montgomery, Moore, Painlevé, Saari — and the newest year among them is
**2015, Montgomery's own**. The **six** block works that post-date him — **Fleischer, Knauf, Stone, Leigh, Kol** and
**this work's own companion paper** — appear in **no part of Chapter 36**. The chapter's only other dated outsider is
**Xia 1992**, at L9930 inside §36.5's list of what the chapter does *not* claim, and Xia appears in no part of the
block.

**So what over-reaches is the BLOCK's scope, not the sentence and not the chapter.** L11808 and L11854 both assert
the blocks *are the works the chapters rest on*; six three-body works are in the block and in no part of Chapter 36,
and on the Löwdin side **Madelung is in neither Chapter 35 nor registers 1701–1712** — the one work of either block
with no home anywhere in the record. Of the Löwdin block's **seven** prior-art works (the eighth bullet is the
companion paper), only **four** are named in Chapter 35 — Löwdin 1950 and 1969, Griffin–Andrew–Cowan, Pulay 1969.

**The ruling.** The sentence at L11821 stands as written. For the block, one of:

- **(a) The block narrows** to the works the chapters actually name — six bullets leave the three-body block and
  Madelung leaves the Löwdin block, or move to a labelled second tier.
- **(b) The block's lead-ins stop saying the chapters rest on it** — L11808 and L11854 are re-worded to *the works
  entered with the chapters at registers 1701–1712 / 1713–1724*, which is true as printed and asserts no dependence.
- **(c) A stated two-tier form** — *rests on* and *entered alongside* as marked groups within each block.

*Docket 9(b) / 17.* **28b-08** rides with it, unruled: block names living **only** in register 1721 within 1713–1724
number **twelve**, not the eight recorded — **Baker, Dechter, Montanari and Pixley** (L11839's constraint-consistency
line) sit in 1721's tenth owner-group, in no other entry of the run, and in no part of Chapter 36.

**Engaged and NOT scored, recorded for you.** Register 1721 prints **ten** owner-groups against its stated **seven**
closures. A question may close to more than one group, and the audit-7 ledger mapping question to owner is **not a
member of this build**, so the reading cannot decide it. What *does* reconcile: §36.2's *eight closed* and §36.3's
*seven belonged to others; the eighth* agree — the eighth is the withdrawn degree-8 polynomial (register 1719),
given to Lagrange's resolvent method 1770 at register 1720, which is why 1721's owner list names neither Lagrange
nor Euler.
